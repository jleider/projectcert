# Alabama State Department of Education — EL Guidebook 2025-2026

Source URL: `https://www.alabamaachieves.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/EL_20251210_ELGuidebook2025-2026_v1.pdf`
Published: 2025-12-10 (per filename and document footer "Amended
11/21/25" inside).
Snapshot: `al-el-guidebook-2025-2026.pdf` / `al-el-guidebook-2025-2026.txt`

The current ALSDE guidance for identifying, assessing, and serving
English Learners. Replaces the 2023-2024 EL Guidebook (which is also
still on the alabamaachieves.org site under uploads/2024/03/).

## What it tells us — ELP assessment

The Guidebook is consistent throughout: Alabama administers the
**WIDA ACCESS** suite (ACCESS for ELLs and Alternate ACCESS) as the
state's annual ELP assessment. Identification uses the WIDA Screener
suite. Notes about 2025-2026 specifics (retained from the snapshot):

- "For 2025-2026 year, Alabama will not use WIDA Alternate Screener.
  Continue use of [the prior screener]." (line 238)
- "Students must be assessed on the WIDA ACCESS English proficiency
  test until they are proficient in English with a composite score of
  4.8 or above."
- The Guidebook's table of contents includes "WIDA Consortium" (page 44)
  as a structural section, "ACCESS Testing" (Part V), exit criteria
  for ACCESS and Alternate ACCESS, and "WIDA Secure Portal and WIDA
  AMS/DRC" (page 51) — i.e., Alabama is operationally embedded in the
  WIDA system.

## What it tells us — ESL teacher certification

Page 49 / Part III concludes with a section titled "**English as a
Second Language Teacher Certification — State Requirements**" that
points educators back to the Teacher Certification chapter of the
Alabama Administrative Code (Chapter 290-3-3-.43) and to
`tcert@alsde.edu`. The Guidebook does *not* itself impose new EL
requirements on non-ESL teachers. It does require LEAs to staff
their EL programs with "Qualified personnel (state certification
and/or ESL licensure)" (line 1414), which means a state-certified
ESL teacher *or* a teacher with an ESL endorsement, but does not
impose a state-wide SEI mandate on all teachers.

## What it tells us — Seal of Biliteracy

The Guidebook references the Alabama Seal of Biliteracy (Act 2022-200)
as a graduation recognition program; full criteria are in the separate
Seal of Biliteracy Guidelines document.
