# Sealofbiliteracy.org — Alabama state record

Source URL: `https://sealofbiliteracy.org/state/al`
Snapshot: `sealofbiliteracy-al.html`

Third-party (Velazquez Press / Californians Together) registry of
Seal of Biliteracy state adoption status. For Alabama:

- **Adoption date**: 2022-04-06.
- **Awarding entity**: Alabama State Department of Education.
- **State contacts**: Maria Franco (English Language Education &
  World Language Education Administrator); Teresina Vazquez
  (Education Specialist for Seal of Biliteracy and World Languages).
- **Source**: links to Alabama HB46 (2022 Regular Session) which
  became Act 2022-200.

This is a corroborating source for the year. Cross-checked against
the ALSDE Guidelines PDF (`al-seal-of-biliteracy-guidelines.md`).
The two dates differ slightly (April 1 vs. April 6, 2022) but resolve
to the same statute and the same calendar year; schema `year: 2022`
is unambiguous.
