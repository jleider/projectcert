# WIDA Consortium membership page

Source URL: `https://wida.wisc.edu/about/consortium`
Snapshot: `wida-consortium.html`

The WIDA Consortium's public membership directory. The page enumerates
42 member states, territories, and federal agencies, and lists Alabama
explicitly:

> "Alabama, Alaska, Colorado, Delaware, District of Columbia, Florida,
> Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Kansas, Kentucky, Maine,
> Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Montana,
> Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, North
> Carolina, North Dakota, Northern Mariana Islands, Oklahoma, Palau,
> Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, U.S.
> Virgin Islands, Utah, Vermont, Virginia, Washington, Wisconsin,
> Wyoming, plus the Bureau of Indian Education and Department of War
> Education Activity."

The page also features a testimonial from Susan Beard, Education
Specialist, Alabama State Department of Education, regarding the
benefits of WIDA membership — confirming Alabama is an active
operational member, not merely a historical signatory.
