## Why this source

State-prescribed competency document that AR-approved educator
preparation programs use to align ESL endorsement coursework. The
companion to the Praxis 5362 test-out pathway in Appendix A of the
licensure rules.

## Structure

> "In addition to the Arkansas Teaching Standards, the English Second
> Language (ESL) Teacher, grades K-12, shall demonstrate knowledge and
> competencies in the following areas:"

Five competency domains, each cross-walked to a TESOL standard and a
Praxis 5362 section:

| # | Domain | TESOL | Praxis 5362 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Language | Standard 1 | Sections 1 and 2 |
| 2 | Culture | Standard 2 | Section 5 |
| 3 | Planning, Implementing, and Maintaining Instruction | Standard 3 | Section 3 |
| 4 | Assessment | Standard 4 | Section 4 |
| 5 | Professionalism | Standard 5 | Section 6 |

## Implications for schema fields

- `eld.requirements.languageProficiency = false` — no non-English
  proficiency exam required at the competency level. The five domains
  cover linguistics, second-language-acquisition theory, culture,
  pedagogy, assessment, and professionalism.
- `eld.requirements.test = true` — the document is explicitly aligned
  to Praxis 5362, which is required for licensure (per the
  licensure-assessments page).
- `eld.requirements.coursework = null` — competencies are listed but
  no minimum credit-hour count is specified at the state level (each
  approved program designs its own coursework to cover them; the
  test-out path skips coursework entirely).
- `eld.requirements.practicum = null` — no state-mandated practicum
  hours specified for the endorsement.
