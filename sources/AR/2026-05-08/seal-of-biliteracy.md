# AR — Arkansas Seal of Biliteracy

**Primary URL:** https://dese.ade.arkansas.gov/Offices/learning-services/curriculum-support/arkansas-seal-of-biliteracy
**Secondary URL:** https://sealofbiliteracy.org/state/ar
**Snapshots:** `seal-of-biliteracy.html`, `sealofbiliteracy-ar.html`
**Retrieved:** 2026-05-08 by projectcert-2026

## Key facts

- Pilot launched March 2017.
- Officially endorsed by the Arkansas State Board of Education in
  **June 2018**.
- Result of an AFLTA + ARKTESOL collaborative initiative.
- Listed as "Approved State Seal" on the national Seal-of-Biliteracy
  registry (sealofbiliteracy.org/state/ar).
