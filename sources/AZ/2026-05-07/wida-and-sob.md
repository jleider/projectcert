## Seal of Biliteracy

Source retrieved 2026-05-07:
`https://sealofbiliteracy.org/state/az`
Snapshot: `sealofbiliteracy-az.html`

> "Arizona Seal of Biliteracy
> Seal of Biliteracy Status: Approved
> State Seal Adoption Date: 2016-05-12"

Statutory backbone: A.R.S. § 15-258 ("State seal of biliteracy
program; requirements; diploma; program termination"), enacted by SB
1239, signed May 12, 2016. The page also notes the program has a
sunset date: "ends on July 1, 2026 pursuant to section 41-3102."

current adopted status. If the legislature does not reauthorize, this
field should be reviewed mid-2026.
