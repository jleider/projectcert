# Arizona — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** Fall 2021
- **Snapshot date:** October 1, 2021
- **EL count:** 93,379
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 1,139,268
- **EL percent:** 8.2%
- **Source label:** NCES Digest of Education Statistics 2023, Table 204.20
- **Source URL:** https://nces.ed.gov/programs/digest/d23/tables/dt23_204.20.asp

## SEA methodology

NCES Table 204.20 defines English Learners as "students identified as English learners (ELs) can participate in language assistance programs to help ensure that they attain English proficiency and meet the academic content and achievement standards expected of all students." Data come from the Common Core of Data (CCD), 'State Nonfiscal Survey of Public Elementary/Secondary Education,' October 1 snapshots.

Arizona Department of Education maintains current EL data through their Office of English Language Acquisition Services (OELAS) at https://www.azed.gov/oelas/el-data, but the site returns HTTP 403 access restrictions and is not publicly accessible via standard web crawling.

## Notes / caveats

- **No data newer than fall 2021 found.** NCES Table 204.20 remains capped at fall 2021 as of May 2026. The December 5, 2024 NCES press release on overall K-12 enrollment included fall 2023 data, but Table 204.20 has not been updated with fall 2022 or 2023 EL breakdowns.

- **Arizona Department of Education website access restricted.** Direct attempts to fetch https://www.azed.gov/oelas/el-data, https://www.azed.gov/oelas/archived-el-demographics, https://www.azed.gov/accountability-research/data returned HTTP 403. The "EL Data PELL Meeting September 7, 2022" presentation slide deck (which may contain fall 2022 data) at https://www.azed.gov/sites/default/files/2022/09/Fall%202022%20PELL%20Meeting%20-%20EL%20Data%20Presentation_Branded.pdf also returned 403.

- **Arizona Auditor General reports (Nov 2023) reference 14% but methodology unclear.** The Auditor General's "Baseline Study of Arizona's English Language Learner" and "ELL Highlights" documents (Nov 2023) are referenced in search results as stating "14 percent of Arizona students are EL," but the actual PDFs do not render and the school year/snapshot date for that figure are unconfirmed. The 14% figure is plausibly consistent with growth from 8.2% (fall 2021) if 2-3 years of enrollment increases occurred.

- **Terminology.** Arizona uses "English Learner (EL)" as the official term (not ELL, ESL, ESOL). AZELLA (Arizona English Language Learner Assessment) is the state assessment.
