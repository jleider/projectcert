# CDE English Language Proficiency Assessments for California (ELPAC)

URL: https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/tg/ep/
Retrieved: 2026-05-08 via WebFetch + curl (HTML snapshot saved as

Issuer: California Department of Education (CDE).

## Key statement

"The ELPAC is the required state test for English language proficiency
(ELP) that must be given to students whose primary language is a
language other than English."

ELPAC is California-specific (not a WIDA or ELPA21 consortium
assessment). California is not a member of either consortium for ELP
assessment purposes.
