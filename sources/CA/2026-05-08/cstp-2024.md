## Keyword scan vs. professionalStandardsMentions

- diverse: TRUE — many references including "California's diverse
  learners" (intro), CSTP 1D ("responsive to students' diverse
  experiences, cultures, languages"), 4A-4 ("Collaborate with diverse
  groups of colleagues"), and standards 6 references.
- cultural: TRUE — pervasive, including "culturally and linguistically
  responsive and sustaining" (CSTP 1B-1, 2C-1, 3A-1, 4B-5), 2A-2
  ("diverse cultural and linguistic backgrounds"), 5A-5, 6B-5, etc.
- linguistic: TRUE — pervasive in same elements as cultural; also 1B-1
  ("linguistic … identities"), 2A-1 ("cultural, linguistic"), 6F-5
  ("students' educational, linguistic, cultural").
- el: TRUE — explicit references to "English learners":
  - 3C-5: "Use the California Practitioners' Guide for Educating
    English Learners with Disabilities."
  - 3C-6: "Use the English language development standards, the English
    Learner Roadmap, and levels of language acquisition to guide the
    selection and organization of subject matter materials appropriate
    for individual English learners."
  - 4B (referenced in workgroup notes): includes "English learners" in
    the populations to whom assessment data are connected.
  - "achieving students, and English learners" (line ~828).

## Coding implication

professionalStandardsMentions:
- diverse: true (unchanged)
- cultural: true (unchanged)
- linguistic: true (unchanged)
- el: true (unchanged)

The 2024 CSTP retains the four mentions present at baseline-2019; if
anything, the explicit "English learner" references increased in this
edition (e.g., 3C-5 and 3C-6 cite the EL Roadmap and Practitioners'
Guide by name).
