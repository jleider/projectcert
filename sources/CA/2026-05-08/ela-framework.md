# English Learner Authorization Framework (CTC educator-prep page)

URL: https://www.ctc.ca.gov/educator-prep/ela
Retrieved: 2026-05-08 via WebFetch.
Issuer: California Commission on Teacher Credentialing (CTC).

## Summary

Three primary authorization routes outside the embedded-in-credential
pathway: CTEL Certificate (exam, approved coursework, or combination);
Bilingual Authorization (add-on to Multiple/Single Subject/Education
Specialist credential, earned concurrently or after via exam or
approved program); and CCSD (Certificate of Completion of Staff
Development) for CTE and Services credential holders to provide SDAIE.

References a 2021 update to Bilingual Authorization standards.

States that the Commission updated preliminary teacher credentials to
include ELA preparation, "for California prepared teachers who earned
a Ryan or Standard teaching credentials prior to the inclusion of the
ELA preparation and authorization within the preliminary teacher
credential" — confirming embedded ELA preparation is now standard for
newly-issued preliminary credentials.
