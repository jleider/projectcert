# Serving English Learners (CL-622)

URL: https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/serving-english-learners-(cl-622)
Retrieved: 2026-05-08 via WebFetch.
Issuer: California Commission on Teacher Credentialing (CTC).

## Key statements

- "All teacher candidates admitted to a California Multiple or Single
  Subject Teacher Credential Program on or after July 1, 2002 complete
  embedded English learner course work" (per AB 1059).
- The leaflet enumerates the documents that authorize EL instruction:
  embedded EL authorization in Multiple/Single Subject credentials,
  Bilingual Authorization, CLAD Certificate, Single Subject World
  Language: ELD, Bilingual Crosscultural Specialist Credential
  (legacy), University/District Intern credentials with EL or bilingual
  authorization, Emergency CLAD/Bilingual permits, CCSD.
- Teachers assigned to provide ELD, SDAIE, or primary-language
  instruction must hold an appropriate authorization.

## Coding implication (SEI mandate)

California's SEI-style requirement is the embedded EL authorization in
every Multiple Subject and Single Subject preliminary credential issued
since 7/1/2002. Functionally equivalent to a one-course (in fact, a
sequence) of EL preparation required of every newly-credentialed
classroom teacher. Coding `sei.mandatedForAllTeachers: true` (unchanged
from baseline). The CDE/CTC framing is "embedded EL authorization"
rather than "SEI mandate," but the policy effect on the teacher
workforce is the same.
