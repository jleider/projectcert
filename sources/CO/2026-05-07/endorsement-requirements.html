Colorado Teacher Endorsement Requirements

The information on this page pertains to endorsement requirements for TEACHER licensure candidates only. (Return to Educator Licensure and select the credential type for more information about endorsements for other license types.) Applicants also must meet all other requirements for the license sought; programs completed outside Colorado will be evaluated against Colorado's endorsement standards for comparability. Refer to the Teacher webpage for details about all requirements of the license or authorization you seek.

First-Time Colorado Teacher License Candidates

Applicants seeking their first Colorado teacher license may demonstrate content knowledge in a number of ways:

  • either by meeting requirements as specified below OR
  • via demonstration of Multiple Measures of Content Competency (select the button below that best describes your situation to determine how you would like to fulfill content knowledge requirements)

Only those endorsements followed by "[ALT]" below are eligible for Colorado alternative teacher preparation programs.

Colorado ALTERNATIVE Candidates

Colorado TRADITIONAL Candidates

OUT-OF-STATE Candidates

Individuals completing programs at non-Colorado institutions of higher education must hold/qualify to hold a license in the content area of preparation in the state of preparation and meet Colorado's endorsement requirements.

Colorado Teacher License-Holders

Holders of a Colorado initial or professional teacher license may apply to add an endorsement to their license by meeting the requirements for that endorsement below.

  • Individuals seeking to renew or advance an expired Colorado Initial Teacher License must meet the requirements for their endorsement(s) as they are specified below.
    • If the endorsement area has since been discontinued, contact Educator Licensing so that we may assist you.

Endorsement Requirements

Holders of a valid, Colorado initial or professional teacher license may qualify to add an endorsement to their license only by meeting the requirements for that endorsement below.

  • Individuals completing programs at non-Colorado institutions of higher education must hold/qualify to hold a license in the content area of preparation in the state of preparation.
  • Where permitted, if seeking an endorsement via 24 semester hours, a letter grade of B- or better must have been earned for each course. (Each course must have a unique number, letter grade and number of credits awarded;  professional development courses and Pass/Fail not accepted. Quarter or trimester hours will be converted to semester hours.)
  • Agriculture, Food and Natural Resources (grades 6-12) [ALT]
  • Business/Marketing (grades 6-12) [ALT]
  • Computer Science (grades K-12) [ALT]
  • Dance (grades K-12) [ALT]
  • Drama Theater Arts (grades K-12) [ALT]
  • Early Childhood Education (ages 0-8) [ALT]
  • Early Childhood Special Education (ages 0-8) [ALT]
  • Elementary Education (grades K-6) [ALT]
  • English Language Arts (grades 6-12) [ALT]
  • Family and Consumer Sciences (grades 6-12) [ALT]
  • Health (grades K-12) [ALT]
  • Mathematics - Middle School (grades 6-8) [ALT]

Agriculture, Food and Natural Resources (grades 6-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in agriculture; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the agriculture endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5701 (147)

Business/Marketing (grades 6-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in business or marketing; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the business/marketing endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5101 (154) or Praxis 5561 (157)

Computer Science (grades K-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in computer science; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the computer science endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5652 (149)

Dance (grades K-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in dance; or
  • 24 semester hours of specific coursework as identified on the dance endorsement worksheet

Drama Theater Arts (grades K-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in drama/theater arts; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the drama endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5641 (153)

Early Childhood Education (ages 0-8) [ALT]

  • Passing scores on each content exam below:
    • Praxis 5024 (160) or an approved Multiple Measures submission for Education of Young Children AND
    • Praxis 5205 (159) or an approved Multiple Measures submission for Teaching Reading 

Early Childhood Special Education (ages 0-8) [ALT]

  • Completion of an approved program from a regionally accredited institution in early childhood special education that includes student teaching and practicum; and
  • Passing scores on each content exam below:
    • Praxis 5024 (160) or an approved Multiple Measures submission for Education of Young Children AND
      • may be waived with a valid Colorado teacher license/ECE endorsement
    • Praxis 5205 (159) or an approved Multiple Measures submission for Teaching Reading AND
    • Praxis 5692 (159) or an approved Multiple Measures submission for Early Childhood Special Education
      • Praxis 5691 (159) also accepted if taken before 08.31.2023; accepted thru 08.31.2028

New! Only for Colorado professional teacher license-holders with an early childhood education (ages 0-8) or special education generalist (ages 5-21) endorsement earned via completion of an approved program

  • 24 semester hours of coursework (and demonstration of content knowledge as specified above) as identified on the applicable early childhood special education endorsement worksheet:
    • ECSE worksheet for early childhood education endorsement holders
    • ECSE worksheet for special education generalists

Elementary Education (grades K-6) [ALT]

  • Passing scores on each content exam below:
    • Praxis 5205 (159) or an approved Multiple Measures submission for Teaching Reading AND
    • Praxis 5903 (157) elementary math or an approved Multiple Measures submission for Elementary Math
      • 5003 (157) also accepted until 08.31.2026 

English Language Arts (grades 6-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in English/English Language Arts; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the English language arts endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5038 (167)

Family and Consumer Sciences (grades 6-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in family and consumer sciences; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the FCS endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5123 (151)
    • 5122 (153) also accepted until 08.31.2030.

Health (grades K-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in health; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the health endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5551 (155)

Mathematics - Middle School (grades 6-8) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in math: or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the middle school math endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5164 (157)
    • Praxis 5169 (165) also accepted if taken between 09.01.2018 and 08.31.2022; accepted thru 08.31.2027
  • Mathematics - Secondary (grades 6-12) [ALT]
  • Music (grades K-12) [ALT]
  • Physical Education (grades K-12) [ALT]
  • Science (grades 6-12) [ALT]
  • Social Studies (grades 6-12) [ALT]
  • Special Education Generalist (ages 5-21) [ALT]
  • Special Education Specialist -- Deaf/Hard of Hearing (ages 0-21)
  • Special Education Specialist - Visual Impairment (ages 0-21)
  • Technology Education (grades 6-12) [ALT]
  • Visual Arts (grades K-12) [ALT]
  • World Languages (grades K-12) [ALT]

Mathematics - Secondary (grades 6-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in math; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the secondary math endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5165 (159)
    • Praxis 5161 (152) also accepted if taken between 09.01.2016 and 08.31.2022; accepted thru 08.31.2027

Music (grades K-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in music; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the music endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5113 (161)

Physical Education (grades K-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in physical education; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the PE endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5091 (148)

Science (grades 6-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in any of the sciences identified on the science endorsement worksheet; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5436 (141)
    • Praxis 5435 (152) also accepted if taken before 08.31.2023; accepted thru 08.31.2028

Social Studies (grades 6-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in social studies, history or political science; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the social studies endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5581 (153)
    • 5081 (150) accepted until 08.31.2030.

Special Education Generalist (ages 5-21) [ALT]

  • completion of an approved program for the preparation of special education generalists from a regionally accredited college or university or 24 semester hours as identified on the SpEd Gen added endorsement worksheet; and
  • passing score on each content exam below:
    • Praxis 5355 (145) or an approved Multiple Measures submission for Foundations of Special Education 
      •  5354 (151) accepted until 08.31.2029
    • Praxis 5903 (157) elementary math or an approved Multiple Measures submission for Elementary Math 
      • may be waived with a valid Colorado teacher license/elementary endorsement)
      • 5003 (157) accepted until 08.31.2026
    • Praxis 5205 (159) or an approved Multiple Measures submission for Teaching Reading

Special Education Specialist -- Deaf/Hard of Hearing (ages 0-21)

  • master’s or higher degree from a regionally accredited institution in special education deaf/hard-of-hearing or its equivalent, as determined by the department; and
  • completion of an approved program from a regionally accredited institution for special education specialists: deaf/hard-of-hearing that includes prescribed field experience requirements.

Special Education Specialist - Visual Impairment (ages 0-21)

  • master’s or higher degree from a regionally accredited institution in special education visual impairment or its equivalent, as determined by the department; and
  • completion of an approved program from a regionally accredited institution for special education specialists: visually impaired that includes prescribed field experience requirements.

Technology Education (grades 6-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in technology education (industrial arts); or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the technology education endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5053 (157)
    • 5051 (159) also accepted until 08.31.2030.

Visual Arts (grades K-12) [ALT]

  • bachelor's or higher degree in fine art/visual arts (with emphasis on studio arts); or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework as identified on the visual arts endorsement worksheet; or
  • Praxis 5135 (158)

World Languages (grades K-12) [ALT]

American Sign Language, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Latin, Mandarin Chinese, Russian and Spanish only

  • bachelor's or higher degree in the identified world language; or
  • 24 semester hours of coursework in the world language as identified on the world languages endorsement worksheet; or
    • for French -  Praxis 5174 (162)
    • for German -  Praxis 5183 (163)
    • for Japanese - Praxis 5661 (156)
    • for Latin - Praxis 5601 (152)
    • for Mandarin Chinese - Praxis 5665 (164)
    • for Russian - Praxis 5671 (130)
    • for Spanish - Praxis 5195 (163)
  • Exams not available for ASL or Italian

Added Endorsements

The following are added endorsements only--you first must hold a valid Colorado teacher license in another content area--and may not be met via "multiple measures" or alternative pathways.

  • Adapted Physical Education (ages 3-21)
  • Culturally and Linguistically Diverse Education (grades K-12)
  • Culturally and Linguistically Diverse Bilingual Education (grades K-12)
  • Gifted Education (ages 4-21)

Adapted Physical Education (ages 3-21)

  • must hold a Colorado teacher license with a physical education endorsement earned via completion of an approved program; and
  • completion of a bachelor- or higher-level approved program in adapted physical education for school-aged children, including a 200-hour practicum across elementary and secondary grade levels.

Culturally and Linguistically Diverse Education (grades K-12)

  • Completion of a Colorado-approved CLD program; or
  • 24 semester hours of specific coursework as identified on the CLD added endorsement worksheet

Culturally and Linguistically Diverse Bilingual Education (grades K-12)

  • Completion of a Colorado-approved Culturally and Linguistically Diverse Bilingual program; or
  • Coursework on the CLD added endorsement worksheet AND fulfillment of competency requirements outlined on the CLD Bilingual added endorsement worksheet

Gifted Education (ages 4-21)

  • Completion of an approved program for the preparation of gifted education educators, including prescribed field experience and student teaching requirement or 24 semester hours of specific coursework requirements outlined in the gifted education added endorsement worksheet; and
  • Praxis 5358 (157)
  • Mathematics Specialist (grades K-12)
  • Mentor Teacher (grades K-12)
  • Reading Specialist (grades K-12)
  • Teacher Librarian (grades K-12)

Mathematics Specialist (grades K-12)

  • must hold a valid Colorado teacher license with an elementary education (grades K-6), middle school mathematics (grades 6-8) or mathematics (grades 6-12) endorsement; and
  • three years' full-time, demonstrated classroom experience as an elementary, middle school math or secondary math teacher; and
  • completion of an approved preparation program for mathematics specialists, which includes a supervised practicum or internship as a mathematics specialist.

Mentor Teacher (grades K-12)

  • must hold a valid Colorado professional teacher license; and
  • completion of a CDE-approved mentor teacher training program, including a minimum of one full school year of successful service as a mentor teacher for a teacher candidate participating in clinical practice.

Reading Specialist (grades K-12)

  • must hold a valid Colorado teacher license; and
  • three or more years' full-time licensed teaching experience; and
  • completion of a master's degree in reading and an approved graduate program for reading specialists which includes a supervised practicum or internship as a reading specialist.
  • Must be knowledgeable about research-based literacy instruction as outlined in rule 4.02(5)-4.02(13) and the Colorado Academic Standards in reading, writing and communicating.
  • Individuals completing programs outside Colorado may be required to demonstrate content knowledge and competency via the Praxis 5302 exam.

 

Teacher Librarian (grades K-12)

  • must hold a valid Colorado teacher license; and
  • completion of an approved program in library science, media science or similar area, including a supervised practicum or internship which includes both elementary and secondary school library experience.
  • Individuals completing programs outside Colorado may be required to demonstrate content knowledge and competency via the Praxis 5312 exam.

Discontinued Endorsements

Unless otherwise indicated below, these discontinued endorsements are issued only upon renewal of an active Colorado initial teacher license or an active or expired Colorado professional teacher license.

  • Business (7-12)
  • Early Childhood Special Education Specialist (ages 0-8)
  • Gifted & Talented Specialist (PreK-12)
  • Gifted Education Core (ages 4-21) Gifted Education Specialist (ages 4-21)
  • Instructional Technology (K-12) Instructional Technology Specialist (K-12)
  • Linguistically Diverse Education (K-12)/Linguistically Diverse Bilingual Education (K-12)

Business (7-12)

  • Discontinued 05.30.2019.
  • Qualifying renewing license-holders will receive the business/marketing combined endorsement.

Early Childhood Special Education Specialist (ages 0-8)

  • Discontinued 05.30.2024.
  • See early childhood special education above

Gifted & Talented Specialist (PreK-12)

  • Discontinued 03.30.2016
  • See gifted education above.

Gifted Education Core (ages 4-21) Gifted Education Specialist (ages 4-21)

  • Discontinued 03.31.2026. 
  • See gifted education above.

Instructional Technology (K-12) Instructional Technology Specialist (K-12)

  • Discontinued 11.30.2022.
  • See computer science above.

Linguistically Diverse Education (K-12)/Linguistically Diverse Bilingual Education (K-12)

  • Discontinued 12.31.2012.
  • See culturally and linguistically diverse education and culturally and linguistically diverse bilingual education above.
  • Marketing (7-12)
  • Reading Teacher (K-12)
  • School Librarian (K-12)
  • Special Education Specialist (ages 5-21)
  • Speech (7-12)
  • Trade and Industry (7-12)

Marketing (7-12)

  • Discontinued 05.30.2019.
  • Qualifying renewing license-holders will receive the business/marketing combined endorsement.

Reading Teacher (K-12)

  • Discontinued 11.30.2022.
  • See reading specialist above.

School Librarian (K-12)

  • Discontinued 03.30.2016.

Special Education Specialist (ages 5-21)

  • Discontinued 05.30.2024.
  • See special education generalist above.

Speech (7-12)

  • Discontinued 11.30.2022.
  • See English language arts above.

Trade and Industry (7-12)

  • Discontinued 11.13.2019.
  • See either business/marketing above or a related CTE authorization.
