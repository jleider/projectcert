# CT Seal of Biliteracy — adoption

Source: https://portal.ct.gov/sde/seal-of-biliteracy/seal-of-biliteracy
Retrieved: 2026-05-08

## Confirmed

Connecticut's State Seal of Biliteracy was adopted via Public Act
17-29 (HB 7159), signed June 6, 2017, with the State Board of
Education establishing criteria by September 1, 2017, and the seal
first available to graduating classes beginning 2018. The CSDE
continues to publish current guidelines and resources for districts.
