# CT SEI mandate — does Connecticut require SEI for all teachers?

Sources reviewed 2026-05-08:
- https://portal.ct.gov/SDE/English-Learners/Bilingual-Education
- https://portal.ct.gov/sde/services/teachers-and-administrators/certification

## Finding

Connecticut does NOT have a state-wide SEI endorsement mandate for
all teachers. CT relies on:

1. **Bilingual programs** triggered by Connecticut General Statutes
   §10-17f when 20+ ELs of the same language background are enrolled
   in a school, and
2. **ESL/sheltered-content instruction** for ELs where bilingual
   programs are unavailable, with that instruction delivered by
   appropriately certified teachers (TESOL cross-endorsement or
   standalone Initial TESOL certificate).

The "SEI mandated for all teachers" pattern is specific to MA, AZ, CA,
and (phasing in) NV per the project schema notes. Connecticut is
not on that list. The only EL-related credentialing requirements in
CT are the bilingual and TESOL endorsements documented in their own
snapshot files.
