## Relevant subject areas (from "All Grades Pre-K-12" table)

Both as primary "All Grades" credentials AND as added "Endorsement" entries:

- Bilingual Education (Pre-K-12), $50
- Bilingual Special Education (Pre-K-12), $50
- English as a Second Language (Pre-K-12), $50

The document confirms: applicants may file for primary certification in
these areas, and holders of an active full DC credential in another
area may also apply for them as added endorsements.

## Implication for schema

- credentials.bilingual.offered = true, standalone = true, addOn = true
- credentials.eld.offered = true, standalone = true, addOn = true
- ESL is the canonical SEA term in DC (mapped to our "eld" category).

DC does not have a separate SEI/sheltered English credential. SEI
mandate for all teachers: false (DC offers ESL endorsement and several
program models — sheltered content is one of five — but does not
mandate sheltered English instruction training for all teachers).
