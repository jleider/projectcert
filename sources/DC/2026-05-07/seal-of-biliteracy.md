# DC State Seal of Biliteracy

Source URL:
https://sealofbiliteracy.org/state/dc/

Retrieved: 2026-05-07

DC adopted a Seal of Biliteracy on December 4, 2014 (DCPS Seal of
Biliteracy Award). Awarded to high school juniors and seniors who
demonstrate proficiency in English and another language.
