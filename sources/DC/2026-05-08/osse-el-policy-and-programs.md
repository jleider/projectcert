## Key facts

- DC's annual ELP assessment is **ACCESS for ELLs** (WIDA Consortium).
- OSSE adopts the **WIDA English Language Development Standards
  (2020 edition)**; uses the **WIDA Screener K-12** for EL
  identification.
- Five program models recognized for LEAs: dual language,
  inclusion/collaborative teaching, content-based ESL, sheltered
  content, newcomer.
- Page does **not** mandate sheltered English instruction training
  for all teachers — schools select models based on student needs and
  proficiency levels.
