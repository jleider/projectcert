- **Snapshot date:** 2024-06 (enrollment audit date)
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** ~13,910 (calculated from 14.1% of 98,652 total)
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 98,652
- **EL percent:** 14.1%

- **Source URL:** https://osse.dc.gov/page/data-and-reports-0
- **Local terminology:** EL (English Learner); also referenced as "English language proficiency" in some contexts

## SEA methodology

The Office of the State Superintendent of Education (OSSE) conducts an annual enrollment audit each spring to determine the number of students at each public school in the District. The audit covers all public schools in DCPS and charter networks. EL identification is based on the OSSE Home Language Survey (administered to all newly enrolling students) followed by screening using an appropriate language proficiency assessment tool (within 30 school days). Students are classified as "receiving English learner services" based on official identification; the definition encompasses those enrolled in one of five evidence-based instructional models: Dual Language, Inclusion/Collaborative Teaching, Content-based ESL, Newcomer programs, or Sheltered Content instruction. Students exit EL status when they meet established proficiency criteria on the ACCESS for ELLs assessment.

The denominator is K-12 public school enrollment (DCPS and charter schools combined). The snapshot date for enrollment audits is the official enrollment count date in the spring of each school year, typically around June. OSSE publishes enrollment audit data with student privacy suppression for certain subgroups per the Student Privacy and Data Suppression Policy (established November 2020).

## Comparison to NCES

NCES Digest Table 204.20 provides the following District of Columbia EL percentages for overlapping fall snapshots:

- Fall 2018: 9.6%
- Fall 2019: 10.5%
- Fall 2020: 10.4%
- Fall 2021: 11.3%

The most recent NCES data is fall 2021 (11.3%), while OSSE's current published figure is 14.1% from the 2023-24 school year (approximately June 2024). **Conclusion: DIVERGES**. The OSSE 2023-24 figure (14.1%) is 2.8 percentage points higher than the NCES fall 2021 figure (11.3%). This represents a substantial increase over the ~2-year gap. This divergence may reflect genuine demographic changes (DC EL enrollment has grown 73% since 2013-14, substantially outpacing total enrollment growth of 20%), methodological differences, or changes in identification practices. OSSE notes that a testing assessment revision in 2017-18 produced a noticeable spike in identified EL students. The edscape dashboard confirms enrollment growth: SY2023-24 saw an increase of more than 800 EL students over the prior year, and SY2024-25 saw an additional 230 students. The methodology appears consistent in terms of the student population captured (all identified ELs regardless of program participation), but the snapshot month differs (NCES = October, OSSE = approximately June), which could affect counts.

## Notes / caveats

1. **Absolute count is calculated, not directly published.** OSSE publishes 14.1% with a total enrollment of 98,652; the EL count of ~13,910 is derived from this calculation and is not the specific cited figure.

2. **Recent growth is substantial and documented.** The edscape dashboard provides detailed historical trend data showing: SY2023-24 enrollment increased by more than 800 students (the highest recent annual increase); SY2024-25 grew by an additional 230 students. Since SY2013-14, EL enrollment has grown by approximately 5,300 students (73% increase).

3. **Snapshot month difference with NCES.** OSSE conducts spring enrollment audits (~June), while NCES uses October fall snapshots. This timing difference could introduce seasonal variation in counts.

4. **Data suppression policies.** OSSE applies privacy suppression using blurred categories ("< 1%" or "> 99%") for certain disaggregated subgroup data, though state-level aggregates like the 14.1% figure are published in full.

5. **No published comparison to prior SEA years.** OSSE does not appear to publish a historical series of annual EL percentages for the state level comparable to the NCES series (fall 2011–2021). The 2021-22 fact sheet and enrollment audit reports are available but were not accessed to extract historical state-level percentages for inter-year OSSE comparison.

6. **Program enrollment is not classified enrollment.** OSSE's figure counts all identified EL students regardless of program participation, consistent with the research requirements.
