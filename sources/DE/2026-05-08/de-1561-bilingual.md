# DE 14 DE Admin Code 1561 — Bilingual Teacher

Retrieved 2026-05-08 from Cornell LII mirror:
<https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/delaware/14-Del-Admin-Code-SS-1561-4.0>
(canonical reg at
<https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/1500/1561.shtml>).

The Bilingual Teacher is a **standalone Standard Certificate** in
Delaware. Per Delaware State University's TELL/ESL program page
<https://chess.desu.edu/departments/languages-literatures/esl-minor-certification-teacher-english-learners-or-bilingual>,
candidates can pursue either "1562 Teacher of English Learners" or
"1561 Bilingual Teacher" as a secondary certification.

## 4.0 Prescribed Education, Knowledge, and Skill Requirements

### 4.1.1 — Education / Content (one of five pathways)

1. NBPTS "English as a New Language" certificate.
2. Bachelor's/master's/doctoral degree with **at least 30 semester
   hours** in **bilingual education** from a CAEP/NCATE-approved or
   state-recognized program at a regionally accredited institution.
3. Alternative-routes program under 14 Del. C. §§ 1260-1266 for
   teaching English Learners.
4. Completion of a **Department-approved educator preparation program**
   in bilingual education.
5. Bachelor's degree in any field plus **15 college credits** in:
   Methods of Teaching ESL, Second Language Acquisition, Teaching
   Literacy for ELs, Second Language Testing, Structure of the English
   Language.

### 4.1.2 — English Proficiency

Bachelor's degree in English-medium U.S. accredited institution OR
ACTFL OPI **and** WPT in English at minimum **"Advanced Mid"**.

### 4.1.3 — Target-Language Proficiency

ACTFL OPI at **"Advanced Mid"** in the target language, AND ACTFL WPT
at a passing level (**Advanced Mid** for languages using the Roman
alphabet; **Advanced Low** for non-Roman alphabet languages).

### 4.1.4 — Assessment

Passing score of **149** on Praxis Subject Assessment — English to
Speakers of Other Languages (ETS Test Code #5362), same as 1562.
