# DE Professional Teaching Standards — standards mentions

Retrieved 2026-05-08:
- <http://www1.udel.edu/educ/whitson/897s05/files/393%20DE%20Teaching%20Standards%203-03-03r.htm>
  (the Standards adopted under former 14 DE Admin Code 393, June 2003,
  hosted by University of Delaware education department; Delaware has
  since folded these into the Educator Effectiveness System / DPAS-II
  framework).

The 2003 Delaware Professional Teaching Standards remain the canonical
baseline statewide teaching standards reference; the document
explicitly references each of the four schema mention dimensions.

## Verbatim references

### Standard 3.0 — Diverse Learners

> "The teacher understands how students differ and adapts instruction
> for diverse learners."

### Knowledge component 3.1.1

> "Understands how student learning is influenced by individual
> experiences, talents, and prior learning, as well as language,
> culture, gender, health, family, and community"

### Knowledge component 3.1.3

> "Understands cultural diversity and how to incorporate
> multi-cultural experiences into instruction"

### Knowledge component 3.1.5

> "Understands the process of second language acquisition and how to
> access strategies to support learning for students whose first
> language is not English"

### Knowledge component 3.1.6

> "Understands the needs of culturally and/or linguistically diverse
> students"

### Glossary

> "Culturally and/or Linguistically Diverse — Students and families
> who come to schools with cultural and/or language backgrounds that
> differ from the predominant experience of monolingual English
> speakers."

### Knowledge component 4.1.4

> "Understands potential positive and negative effects of their verbal
> and non-verbal messages on students with cultural, gender and
> ability differences."

## Schema mapping

- diverse: TRUE (Standard 3.0 title and 3.1.1)
- cultural: TRUE (3.1.3, 3.1.4 explicitly)
- linguistic: TRUE (3.1.6, glossary)
- el: TRUE (3.1.5 — second language acquisition for students whose
  first language is not English; the operational definition of EL)

## Comparison to baseline-2019

- linguistic: false → **TRUE**
- el: false → **TRUE**

Baseline appears to have read the standards conservatively (the
2003 doc never literally uses the abbreviation "EL", and the words
"linguistic" / "linguistically" only appear inside the
"culturally and/or linguistically diverse" composite phrase). On
re-read, the explicit Knowledge component 3.1.5 reference to second
language acquisition and students whose first language is not English
is squarely the EL flag, and "linguistically diverse" satisfies the
linguistic flag.
