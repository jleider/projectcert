## Figures

- **Delaware (fall 2021): 11.5%** of total public elementary and
  secondary enrollment classified as English Learners.
- National average (fall 2021): 10.6%.
- Delaware is one of 13 states (plus DC) where ELs were >= 10.0% of
  enrollment in fall 2021.

## Comparison to baseline-2019

- elPercent: 9.1 → **11.5**
- elPercentAsOf: "2019-10-01" → **"2021-10-01"** (NCES uses fall 2021,
  the most recent year published in Digest 2023 table 204.20).

This aligns with the EL Guidebook's own self-reported "approximately
14,000 ELs" against ~136k Delaware public-school enrollment (~10%);
the NCES table figure of 11.5% is preferred for cross-state
comparability per the project's coding rule.
