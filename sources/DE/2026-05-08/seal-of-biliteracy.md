# DE Seal of Biliteracy — Certificate of Multi-literacy

Retrieved 2026-05-08:

- Delaware General Assembly bill record (HJR 4, 149th GA):
  <https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail?LegislationId=25823>

Delaware's program is officially titled the **Delaware Certificate
of Multi-literacy**, established by **House Joint Resolution No. 4**
of the 149th General Assembly, with the Department of Education
publishing criteria by August 30, 2017 and the Certificate first
available to graduates in **school year 2017-18**.
