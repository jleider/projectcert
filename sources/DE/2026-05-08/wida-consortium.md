  references "the Annual WIDA ACCESS for ELLs 2.0 assessment" and
  "Alternative ACCESS for ELLs" throughout.

Delaware is a WIDA Consortium member state. The state's annual
English Language Proficiency assessment is **ACCESS for ELLs 2.0**
(and Alternate ACCESS for ELLs for students with the most significant
cognitive disabilities).
