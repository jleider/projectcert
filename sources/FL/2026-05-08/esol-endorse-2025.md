# 2025 Florida Teacher Standards for ESOL Endorsement

- URL: <https://www.fldoe.org/file/7582/ESOLEndorse25.pdf>
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08
- Adopted by State Board of Education: 2025-08-20, under Rule 6A-4.02451, F.A.C.
- Effective: September 2025; programs must submit revised endorsement
  matrices to FLDOE by 2026-06-01.

## Document structure

The 2025 Florida Teacher Standards for ESOL Endorsement are organized
into five strands (each one corresponds to one of the five 3-semester-
hour content areas in Rule 6A-4.0244):

1. **Cross-Cultural Communication and Understanding (CU)** — CU.1
   Learning of Academic Content, Language, and Culture for ELLs.
2. **Applied Linguistics (AL)** — AL.1 Language as a System.
3. **Methods of Teaching ESOL (MT)** — MT.1 ESOL Requirements and
   Practices; MT.2 English Language Acquisition and Development; MT.3
   Standards-Based ESOL and Content Instruction.
4. **ESOL Curriculum and Materials Development (CM)** — CM.1 Planning
   for Standards-Based Instruction for ELLs; CM.2 Effective Use of
   Resources and Technologies.
5. **Testing and Evaluation of ESOL (TE)** — TE.1 Key Factors and
   Considerations in Assessment for ELLs; TE.2 Classroom-Based
   Assessment for ELLs.

## Coding scheme

`<Strand>.<Standard>.<Performance Indicator>` (e.g., `MT.2.2`). The
document is the descriptor companion to Rule 6A-4.0244 (endorsement)
and informs the FTCE 047 ESOL K-12 framework.

## Relevance to schema

- Confirms `eld.offered = true`, `eld.standalone = true` (FTCE 047 K-12
  certification), `eld.addOn = true` (academic endorsement).
- Strand-level structure covers methods, curriculum, applied
  linguistics, cross-cultural, and assessment — matches the five
  3-credit areas mandated by 6A-4.0244.
- No language-proficiency requirement (no expectation that the
  candidate demonstrate proficiency in a language other than English).
- No standalone practicum requirement in the standards themselves;
  practicum is at the discretion of the approved-program institution.
