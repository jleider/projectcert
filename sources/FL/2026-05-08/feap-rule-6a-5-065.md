# Rule 6A-5.065, F.A.C. — Florida Educator Accomplished Practices (FEAPs)

- History: New 1998-07-02; amended 2011-02-13, 2022-11-22, 2023-02-21,

## Structure

Six practices in two categories:

**Quality of Instruction**

1. Instructional Design and Lesson Planning
2. The Learning Environment
3. Instructional Delivery and Facilitation
4. Assessment

**Continuous Improvement, Responsibility and Ethics**

1. Continuous Professional Improvement
2. Professional Responsibility and Ethical Conduct

## Keyword scan (verbatim)

- **diverse / diversity**: YES.
  - 2.h "Adapts the learning environment to accommodate the differing
    needs and **diversity** of students…"
- **cultural**: YES.
  - 2.d "Respects students' **cultural** linguistic and family
    background"
- **linguistic**: YES.
  - Same sentence as above (2.d).
- **language**: YES (in "oral and written communication", and as part
  of "cultural linguistic"). No reference to "English language
  learner" / ELL / EL.
- **EL / ELL / English learner**: NO. The current FEAPs do not
  explicitly reference English Learners.
