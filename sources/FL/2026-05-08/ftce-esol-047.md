## Confirmation

The FTCE 047 ESOL K-12 subject area examination is the standard test
path to the standalone ESOL K-12 certification (Rule 6A-4.0245). The
test framework includes competencies in culture and ELLs, applied
linguistics, ESOL curriculum and materials, methods, and ESOL testing
and evaluation — paralleling the five strands of the 2025 Florida
Teacher Standards for ESOL Endorsement.
