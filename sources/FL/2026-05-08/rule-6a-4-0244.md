# Rule 6A-4.0244, F.A.C. — ESOL Endorsement specialization requirements

- URL: <https://www.fldoe.org/teaching/certification/certificate-subjects/administrative-rules/6a-4-0244.stml>
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08
- History: New 1989-10-10, last amended 2009-04-21. Pointer rule;
  current substantive content lives in Rule 6A-4.02451 (the 2025
  standards) per the August 2025 SBE action.

## Specialization requirements (endorsement)

Prerequisite: bachelor's or higher degree with certification in another
subject.

Coursework: **fifteen (15) semester hours** in English for Speakers of
Other Languages, distributed across these five areas (3 hrs each):

1. Methods of Teaching ESOL
2. ESOL Curriculum and Materials Development
3. Cross-Cultural Communication and Understanding
4. Applied Linguistics
5. Testing and Evaluation of ESOL

Alternate path: complete a Florida school district's approved
inservice add-on program (the "60 hours per area × 5 areas = 300
inservice points" district route).

## Relevance to schema

- `eld.addOn = true` (endorsement); `eld.requirements.coursework = true`
  (15 SH mandated). The leider-2021 coding has `coursework = null`,
  which we now have grounds to set to `true`.
- `eld.requirements.program`: an approved program is one of two
  pathways (the other is district inservice); since the rule mandates
  *coursework* across five named areas regardless of route, we keep
  `program = null` (it's not a strict requirement — district
  inservice is also accepted).
- No practicum, no test, no language-proficiency requirement at the
  *endorsement* level.
