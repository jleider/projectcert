# Rule 6A-4.0245, F.A.C. — ESOL K-12 Certification specialization

- URL: <https://www.fldoe.org/teaching/certification/certificate-subjects/administrative-rules/6a-4-0245.stml>
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

## Specialization requirements (standalone certification)

A bachelor's or higher degree with an undergraduate or graduate major
in English to Speakers of Other Languages satisfies specialization for
certification in English to Speakers of Other Languages (Grades K-12).

Per the FLDOE certificate-subjects page and the FTCE site, the
alternate path to satisfy specialization for the K-12 certification is
the FTCE ESOL K-12 (test code 047) subject area examination.

## Relevance to schema

- `eld.standalone = true` (ESOL K-12 is a standalone certificate).
- `eld.requirements.test = true` (FTCE 047 is the standard
  exam-based path; consistent with leider-2021 coding).
- `eld.requirements.program`: holding a major satisfies specialization,
  but a major is not strictly required (the test path exists). Coded
  as `null` (not a strict requirement of the certification).
