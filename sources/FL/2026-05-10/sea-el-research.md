- **ELL count:** ~360,000 (cited without specific year or snapshot date on SALA website)
- **Context:** "One out of ten students in Florida are identified as English Learners (ELs) or English Language Learners (ELLs)"

- **ELL count:** 269,534
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 2,833,186 (K-12, all grades)

## SEA methodology

**FDOE definition:** An English Language Learner (ELL) is an individual who:
- Was not born in the United States AND whose native language is a language other than English; OR
- Comes from a home environment where a language other than English is spoken; OR
- Is an American Indian or Alaskan Native and whose education has been significantly affected by a language other than English.

**Data collection:** FDOE conducts two formal surveys per school year:
- **Survey 2:** October (captures membership for FTE funding)
- **Survey 3:** February (secondary count)

**Reporting:** Districts submit separate records for each K-12 student identified as "LY" (English Language Learner) in the student information system during surveys 2, 3, and 5.

**Data structure:** FDOE publishes membership data by school and by school/race-ethnicity cross-tabulation. Data files are available through the "Know Your Data" tool (edudata.fldoe.org) and downloadable from the FDOE Students Publications page.

## Comparison to NCES

NCES Digest Table 204.20 baseline (Fall 2021 = October 2021 Survey 2):
- Fall 2018: 282,002 (9.9%)
- Fall 2019: 278,498 (9.7%)
- Fall 2020: 264,546 (9.7%)
- Fall 2021: 269,534 (9.5%)

**Conclusion:** NCES figures show a declining ELL count from 2018–2021 (282k → 270k), with percentage stable at 9.5–9.9%. The SALA figure of ~360,000 cited on the FDOE website does **not match** historical NCES counts and appears to include a broader population (possibly including former ELLs in monitoring years or PK rather than K-12 only). **Status: Diverges significantly. Needs clarification.**

## Notes / caveats

1. **Data gap (2022-2025):** NCES has not yet published fall 2022 or later data; FDOE has not published a consolidated state-level ELL enrollment figure with a clear snapshot date for 2023-24 or fall 2024.

2. **The ~360,000 figure:** Published on the SALA website without a year or survey reference. May reflect:
   - All PK-12 (not K-12 only), inflating the count
   - A different classification (e.g., including recently exited / monitoring students)
   - An outdated snapshot from an earlier year re-posted without revision

3. **Data availability path:** To obtain verified fall 2023 or fall 2024 figures, would need to:
   - Download the "English Language Learners by School By Race/Ethnicity, Survey 2" Excel files from https://www.fldoe.org/accountability/data-sys/edu-info-accountability-services/pk-12-public-school-data-pubs-reports/students.stml
   - Aggregate statewide (school-by-school totals)
   - Cross-reference against the corresponding year's final FTE enrollment total

4. **Denominator note:** FDOE Survey 2 (October) reports both PK-12 and K-12 separately. NCES Digest Table 204.20 reports K-12 only. Ensure alignment when comparing.

5. **Term variance:** FDOE uses "English Language Learner" (ELL) and "English for Speakers of Other Languages" (ESOL) interchangeably; no distinction between ELL identification and ESOL program enrollment.
