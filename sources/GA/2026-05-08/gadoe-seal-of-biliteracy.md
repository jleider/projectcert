## Summary

GADOE's official program page for Georgia's Seal of Biliteracy. Seal is
awarded to graduating high-school students demonstrating proficiency in
English plus one or more additional languages.

## Key facts

- Authorizing statute: O.C.G.A. § 20-2-159.5 (HB 879, 2016 Ga. Laws 618).
- Effective date: July 1, 2016.
- Adoption year for our schema: **2016**.
