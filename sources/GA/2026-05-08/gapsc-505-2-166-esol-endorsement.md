# GaPSC Rule 505-2-.166 — ESOL Endorsement (certification rule)

Source: https://www.gapsc.com/Rules/Current/Certification/505-2-.166.pdf
Effective: January 1, 2026
Retrieved: 2026-05-08
File: gapsc-505-2-166-esol-endorsement.pdf (saved 2026-05-07)

## Summary

This is the **certification rule** for the ESOL Endorsement (field code 825,
per the Certificate Field Codes sheet revised January 2026). It is the
companion to 505-3-.89 (the educator-preparation program rule).

## Key facts

- **Endorsement (add-on)**: ESOL is offered as an endorsement that may be
  added to a level-4-or-higher renewable Professional or Five-Year Induction
  certificate in any teaching, service, or leadership field, or to a Permit
  certificate in a world-language field.
- **Three-Year Non-Renewable / Three-Year Induction** variants exist for
  educators whose employing LUA requests the certificate.
- **In-field statement**: "An individual with the ESOL Endorsement is
  in-field to teach English/ESOL courses when combined with the English 6-12
  certificate, or ESOL courses when combined with any teaching certificate
  appropriate to the grade level."
- Procedural requirements (state-approved program completion or comparable
  out-of-state credential) are governed by 505-2-.14 (general endorsements
  rule).

## Schema implications

- `eld.addOn: true` — ESOL exists as an endorsement.
- Standalone ESOL certification is established separately (P-12 ESOL field
  code 885, see Certificate Field Codes); 505-2-.166 itself only covers the
  endorsement variant.
