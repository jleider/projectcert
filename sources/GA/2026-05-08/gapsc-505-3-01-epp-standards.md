# GaPSC Rule 505-3-.01 — Requirements and Standards for Approving EPPs

Source: https://www.gapsc.com/rules/current/educatorpreparation/505-3-.01.pdf
Effective: July 1, 2025
Retrieved: 2026-05-08
File: gapsc-505-3-01-epp-standards.pdf

## Summary

The umbrella rule governing how Georgia approves educator preparation
providers (EPPs) and their programs. Procedural rather than content-bearing.

## Key facts

- "GaPSC approval standards for EPPs and programs shall at a minimum be
  adapted from the most recent version of the standards of the Council
  for the Accreditation of Educator Preparation (CAEP)." — paragraph 4(b)2.
- Defines program types (initial, endorsement, advanced/degree-only),
  approval cycles, and reporting requirements.
