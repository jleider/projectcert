# GaPSC Rule 505-3-.89 — ESOL Endorsement (educator preparation program)

Source: https://www.gapsc.com/Rules/Current/EducatorPreparation/505-3-.89.pdf
Effective: August 15, 2023
Retrieved: 2026-05-08
File: gapsc-505-3-89-esol-program.pdf (saved 2026-05-07)

## Summary

Defines the field-specific content standards an EPP must meet to offer an
approved ESOL endorsement program. Adapted from the 2018 TESOL standards.
Programs may be **stand-alone** OR **embedded** in initial-prep or
advanced/degree-only programs.

## Standards (paraphrased)

1. **Knowledge about Language** — English structures, second-language
   acquisition theory, academic-language functions.
2. **Language and Culture** — sociopolitical/cultural contexts of EL
   education; candidates use multiple identities (cultural, linguistic,
   multilingual, transnational) as pedagogical resources.
3. **Planning and Implementing Instruction** — culturally and linguistically
   relevant supportive environments; uses **WIDA Consortium ELD standards**
   and ELD assessment results to plan instruction.
4. **Assessment and Evaluation** — analyze multiple assessments including
   language proficiency assessments; understand identification, placement,
   reclassification.
5. **Professionalism and Leadership** — collaborate, advocate for ELs,
   apply state/federal legislation impacting EL educational rights, engage
   in **field experiences** to develop practice.

## Schema implications

- `eld.offered: true`, `eld.standalone: true`, `eld.addOn: true`
  (the rule expressly covers both stand-alone and embedded program
  formats).
- `eld.requirements.program: true` — completing a GaPSC-approved program
  is the principal route to the endorsement (paired with 505-2-.14).
- `eld.requirements.coursework: true` — the rule lays out specific
  knowledge components (language structures, SLA theory, etc.) required
  in the syllabi.
- `eld.requirements.practicum: true` — Standard 5(iv): "Candidates
  engage in field experiences to apply their knowledge and further
  develop their understanding of language, sociocultural context,
  planning and implementing instruction for ELs, and assessment and
  evaluation of ELs to improve their professional practice."
- `eld.requirements.test: true` — GACE ESOL (Test 119) remains the
  state-approved content assessment under GaPSC certification rules.
- `eld.requirements.languageProficiency: false` — no required
  English/L2 language-proficiency exam separate from the content test.

## WIDA confirmation

Standard 3(vi) explicitly references "WIDA Consortium English Language
Development (ELD) standards and ELD assessment results" — confirms GA's
WIDA Consortium membership and ACCESS-for-ELLs use.
