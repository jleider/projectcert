# Iowa Seal of Biliteracy — Iowa DOE

- URL: <https://educate.iowa.gov/pk-12/standards/instruction/biliteracy-seal>

Iowa DOE page on the state biliteracy seal. Defines a biliteracy seal
as "an award given by a district to recognize students who have
attained proficiency in two or more languages, one of which is
English, by high school graduation."

Adoption: Senate File 475, signed by Governor Kim Reynolds in the 2018
legislative session. Matches the baseline `year: 2018`.
