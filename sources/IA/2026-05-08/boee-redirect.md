# Iowa Board of Educational Examiners — redirected to Iowa DOE

- URL: <https://boee.iowa.gov/>
- Snapshots: `boee-redirect.html`, `boee-headers.txt`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

`boee.iowa.gov` 301-redirects to
`https://educate.iowa.gov/educator-licensure`. Structural change since
2019: the BoEE's standalone web presence was folded into the Iowa
Department of Education's Educator Licensure office as part of state
government consolidation. The Board of Educational Examiners itself
still exists under Iowa Code chapter 272 and remains the licensing
body, but it no longer operates a separate public website. Worth
flagging in the JSON's `notes` so future maintainers don't chase a
dead domain.
