# ELPA21 — Iowa DOE

- URL: <https://educate.iowa.gov/pk-12/standards/specialized-instruction/english-learners/elpa21>
- Snapshot: `elpa21-iowa.html`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

Iowa DOE's dedicated ELPA21 page. "ELPA21 stands for English Language
Proficiency Assessment for the 21st Century and is an assessment
system that provides information regarding student proficiency of the
English language overall, as well as in reading, writing, speaking,
listening, and comprehension." Iowa is a member of the ELPA21 state
collaborative.

ELPA21 consortium members (per ELPA21 documentation, 2025): Arkansas,
Iowa, Louisiana, Mississippi, Nebraska, Ohio, Oregon, Tennessee, West
Virginia (full assessment); Arizona and Connecticut administer Alt
ELPA only.
