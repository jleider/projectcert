# Iowa endorsements list — Iowa DOE

- URL: <https://educate.iowa.gov/educator-licensure/endorsements-list>
- Snapshot: `endorsements-list.html`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

Master list of all 77 endorsements offered in Iowa. Confirms:

- **Endorsement 104: K-12 English Language Learners** is the lone
  ELD-style endorsement.
- **No** Bilingual or Bicultural endorsement is listed. Iowa offers
  K-8 and 5-12 world-language endorsements (Chinese, French, German,
  Japanese, Latin, Russian, Spanish, Norwegian, Italian, Portuguese,
  "Language (other)") and a Native Language Teaching Authorization,
  but none of these are bilingual-instruction credentials in the
  schema's sense (DBE/DLBE/TBE/heritage rolling up to `bilingual`).

Validates `credentials.bilingual.offered: false` (no bilingual
endorsement) and `credentials.eld.offered: true` (Endorsement 104).
