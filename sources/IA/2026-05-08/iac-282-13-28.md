# Iowa Administrative Code 282-13.28(20) — ESL endorsement

- URL: <https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/iowa/Iowa-Admin-Code-r-282-13-28>
- Snapshot: `iac-282-13-28.html`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

The state-minimums rule for the K-12 English as a Second Language
endorsement (Iowa's ELD endorsement; numbered 104 in the catalog).

Authorization: holder may teach English as a second language in
"kindergarten and grades one through twelve."

Content: 18 semester hours organized into four areas:

1. **Pedagogy** — "Methods and curriculum to include the following:
   Bilingual and ESL methods. Literacy in native and second language."
2. **Linguistics** — psycholinguistics, sociolinguistics, "Knowledge
   of first and second language proficiency" and acquisition.
3. **Cultural and Linguistic Diversity** — history; theory, models,
   and research; policy and legislation.
4. **Current Issues** — transient populations.

Notably absent at the rule level:

- No prescribed practicum hours.
- No content test (no Praxis subject-test gate).
- No language-proficiency requirement (no demonstrated proficiency in
  a second language).

Schema mapping for `credentials.eld.requirements`:

- `program: true` — approved-program path explicitly available.
- `coursework: true` — 18 hours specified.
- `practicum: null` — not required by rule; provider may include.
- `test: null` — not required by rule.
- `languageProficiency: false` — no proficiency demonstration is
  required to add the ESL endorsement (matches baseline).
