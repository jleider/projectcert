The Iowa DOE's current canonical domain. The 2019 baseline cited
`https://educateiowa.gov`, which now 301-redirects to
`https://educate.iowa.gov/`. The homepage exposes the Educator
Licensure section (`/educator-licensure`), the PK-12 standards section
(`/pk-12/standards`), and student-supports (`/pk-12/student-supports`).
EL- and ESL-specific content lives under those subtrees rather than on
the homepage. Snapshot is the navigation root for everything else
sourced today.
