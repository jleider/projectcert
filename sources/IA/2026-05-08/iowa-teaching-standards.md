# Iowa Teaching Standards and Model Criteria

- URL: <https://educate.iowa.gov/media/984/download?inline=>

The Iowa Teaching Standards (codified at Iowa Code § 284.3) and Model
Criteria — adopted by the State Board of Education 2002, amended 2010.
Eight standards covering achievement support, content knowledge,
planning, instructional delivery, assessment, classroom management,
professional growth, and professional responsibilities.
