# Iowa licenses and authorizations — Iowa DOE

- URL: <https://educate.iowa.gov/educator-licensure/licenses-authorizations>
- Snapshot: `licenses-authorizations.html`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

Catalog of Iowa licenses and authorizations. Confirms the
**Preliminary Native Language Teaching Authorization** and the
**Native Language Teaching Authorization** as the credentials Iowa
uses for instruction in a non-English language. Both are categorized
under "Limited Teaching" authorizations rather than endorsements on a
regular teaching license. No Bilingual / Bicultural endorsement is
listed.

This validates the existing baseline-2019 note on
`credentials.bilingual` ("Iowa requires an 'Authorization' to teach
in a non-English language.") and supports
`credentials.bilingual.offered: false` for the schema (a Limited
Teaching authorization is not a bilingual credential in our sense).
