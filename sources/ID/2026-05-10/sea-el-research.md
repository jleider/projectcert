# Idaho — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

No newer data found.

The Idaho State Department of Education has not published state-level English learner enrollment figures beyond fall 2021. The most recent snapshot remains:

- **School year:** 2021-22
- **Snapshot date:** Fall 2021 (October)
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 18,167
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 313,015
- **EL percent:** 5.8%
- **Source label:** NCES Digest of Education Statistics Table 204.20
- **Source URL:** https://nces.ed.gov/programs/digest/d23/tables/dt23_204.20.asp
- **Local terminology:** English Learner (EL)

## SEA methodology

The Idaho State Department of Education participates in the NCES EDFacts/Common Core of Data (CCD) reporting system and submits data through the English Learner Management System (ELMS). Per the ELMS documentation, English learners are "students who are eligible for the English language instruction educational program (LIEP) on the testing/reporting date, based on the EL Entry Date submitted by the district or charter school in the English Learner Management System (ELMS)."

The denominator reflects total K-12 public school enrollment as reported through CCD. Idaho's administrative structure includes 104 Local Education Agencies (LEAs) with state funding only and 35 with both state and federal Title III-A funding.

Snapshot date: October (fall cohort).

## Comparison to NCES

NCES historical values (Table 204.20):
- Fall 2018: 19,216 ELs (6.2%)
- Fall 2019: 21,215 ELs (6.8%)
- Fall 2020: 18,426 ELs (6.0%)
- Fall 2021: 18,167 ELs (5.8%)

The SEA does not publish disaggregated multi-year historical tables that would allow direct comparison within the same document. NCES Digest Table 204.20 (consulted at https://nces.ed.gov/programs/digest/d23/tables/dt23_204.20.asp) provides the only publicly available state-level EL enrollment data for Idaho for the years in question.

**Conclusion: NO OVERLAP.** The Idaho SEA does not publish state-level EL enrollment percentages directly; all verifiable figures come from NCES EDFacts submissions (which the SEA supplies to NCES). No independent SEA-authored EL enrollment report or data dashboard with figures for fall 2018–2021 or beyond was found.

## Notes / caveats

1. **No post-2021 data.** Repeated searches of Idaho SDE website (sde.idaho.gov), the Idaho Report Card (idahoreportcard.org), the State Board of Education (boardofed.idaho.gov), Idaho Ed Trends, and the NCES Digest State Dashboard for Idaho all yielded no state-level EL enrollment figures for fall 2022, fall 2023, or fall 2024.

2. **SEA publishes program data, not enrollment totals.** The 2024-2025 Annual Summary of the Idaho English Learner Program (sde.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/H25117-English-Proficiency-ELLs-FY25.pdf) was inaccessible in a readable format but appears to document Title III allocations, program participation, and proficiency outcomes rather than state-level enrollment counts. 

3. **ELMS is the authoritative system.** The English Learner Management System (ELMS) is mentioned in the English-Learner Manual (sde.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/English-Learner-Manual.pdf) as the system through which districts and charters report EL data to the state; however, the state does not appear to publish aggregated state-level summaries from ELMS to the public.

4. **CCD/EDFacts is the public-facing source.** State-level EL figures for Idaho are accessible only through NCES platforms (Digest tables, the Digest State Dashboard, the Elementary/Secondary Information System (ElSi), and the Common Core of Data). These are federal compilations of data submitted by the SEA; they are not independently maintained state-published reports.

5. **Title III reporting to ED.** Idaho submits Consolidated State Performance Report (CSPR) data to the U.S. Department of Education on EL participants, but these reports are not accessible through public web searches and would require direct contact with the SEA or a FOIA request.
