## Transitional Bilingual Educator (TBE) on ELS

Available pathways (any one):

- **Option 1:** Bachelor's degree (English-medium institution) +
  passing score on Target Language Proficiency test, OR Illinois /
  Global Seal of Biliteracy.
- **Option 2:** Bachelor's degree (target-language-medium institution,
  out-of-country) + passing English Language Proficiency test.
- **Option 3:** Two degrees (one English-medium, one target-language-
  medium) — no test required.
- **Option 4:** Bachelor's degree taught bilingually (English/target) —
  no test required.

Validity: 5 years; not renewable.

Scope: "Valid only for teaching transitional bilingual courses in a
PreK-Grade 12 setting and English as a Second Language (ESL) in
Kindergarten-Grade 6."

## Implication for schema

The TBE endorsement on an ELS is what makes the bilingual credential
**standalone** in Illinois — a candidate can hold an ELS-TBE without
first having a PEL, provided language proficiency and degree
requirements are met. This is what allows IL districts to staff TBE
programs at scale (mandated by 105 ILCS 5/14C when 20+ ELs of the
same language classification enroll at an attendance center).

Confirms `bilingual.standalone = true` and `bilingual.addOn = true`
(both pathways exist).
