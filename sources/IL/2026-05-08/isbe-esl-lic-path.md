## Summary

ISBE one-page handout listing the credentials that allow an educator to
teach ESL in Illinois public schools.

### PEL pathways (full licensure)

- Option 1 (PK-6): PEL + ESL endorsement, OR PEL + ENL endorsement,
  OR PEL + Bilingual endorsement, OR PEL + ENL endorsement with a
  language designation.
- Option 2 (7-12): PEL + ESL endorsement, OR PEL + ENL endorsement.

### ELS pathways (alternative)

- Option 1: ELS-VIT (Visiting International Teacher) + passing score
  on the English Language Proficiency test.
- Option 2: ELS-TBE license (Transitional Bilingual Educator), valid
  for grades PK-6 only.

### STA — ESL (Short-Term Approval)

Holder must already have a PEL (any teaching field, any grade range)
plus 9 semester hours of specific ESL coursework. STA is a temporary
credential that allows an educator to teach immediately while pursuing
the full endorsement.
