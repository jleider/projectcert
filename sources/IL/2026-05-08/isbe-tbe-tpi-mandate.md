## Threshold

When a school's enrollment includes **20 or more English Learners of
the same language classification** (across all K-12 grades at an
attendance center), the district must establish a **Transitional
Bilingual Education (TBE) program** for that language.

When the count is **19 or fewer**, the district must instead provide a
**Transitional Program of Instruction (TPI)** — a more flexible
ESL-driven model with native-language support to the extent necessary.

## Program content

### TBE (mandated when ≥ 20 ELs same-language)
- Instruction in core subjects in both the student's home language
  and in English (where required by law).
- Home-language arts instruction.
- ESL instruction aligned to English language development standards.
- Instruction in the history and culture of the country/territory of
  origin.

### TPI (mandated when ≤ 19 ELs same-language, or where TBE
not feasible)
- Native-language support to the extent necessary for grade-level
  pacing.
- ESL instruction aligned to applicable standards.
- Home-language arts and cultural instruction are optional.

## Implication for schema

This is **district-level program mandate**, not a teacher-credential
mandate. It does not flip
`credentials.sei.mandatedForAllTeachers` (Illinois has no SEI
mandate). It does, however, justify the IL `notes` field flagging
the TBE/TPI program-side requirement, which the seed paper called
out and which remains a defining feature of IL EL policy.
