Retrieved: 2026-05-08
Retrieved by: projectcert-2026

## Confirmation

Illinois has been a WIDA Consortium member since 2004.

- ID assessments: WIDA Screener for Kindergarten + WIDA Screener
  (grades 1-12).
- Annual ELP assessment: **WIDA ACCESS**, administered mid-January to
  mid-February. Online for grades 1-12 (Writing for grades 1-3 is on
  paper). WIDA Alternate ACCESS available for eligible students with
  significant cognitive disabilities.
- Standards: Illinois has adopted the 2020 WIDA English Language
  Development Standards Framework, plus the WIDA Spanish Language
  Development Standards.
- Professional development is provided free through ISBE (webinar,
  self-paced, and in-person).
