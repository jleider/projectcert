## SEA methodology

The Kansas State Department of Education administers the Kansas English Language Proficiency Assessment (KELPA) each spring to determine whether a student has reached English proficiency. Students identified as English Learners (EL) receive English Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL) services, which are funded through the state's bilingual weighting formula.

KSDE classifies students as EL when they are new to the country and/or need assistance learning to speak English. Bilingual weighting for district funding is calculated as the higher of either:
- Total contact hours divided by 6, multiplied by 0.395, or
- Total bilingual headcount multiplied by 0.185

However, KSDE does not appear to publish state-level aggregate EL enrollment counts or percentages in a publicly accessible annual report, data dashboard, or statistical summary. The KSDE Data Central portal contains district-level reporting tools but requires direct access to the report generator system, which was not accessible via web search or public documentation.

## Notes / caveats

1. **Data access limitation:** The Kansas State Department of Education maintains enrollment data through their KSDE Data Central portal, but the report generator (at datacentral.ksde.gov) requires registered access and is not directly queryable via public web interface. The system requires authentication, preventing discovery of state-level aggregate EL enrollment tables.

2. **Terminology:** Kansas uses "ESOL" (English Speakers of Other Languages) for their EL services and "bilingual weighting" in the school finance formula, but does not appear to publish state-level "EL enrollment" or "ESOL student count" figures publicly.

3. **Program vs. classification confusion:** KSDE funding is tied to "bilingual weighting headcount" (students enrolled in ESOL-served programs), which may differ from EL *classification* counts. NCES reports classification counts; if KSDE publishes similar data, the methodological alignment is unknown.

4. **No newer data available:** Fall 2021 remains the most recent official state-level EL enrollment figure. No fall 2022, 2023, or 2024 data was located through KSDE public documents, reports, or accessible dashboards.

**Search conclusion:** KSDE does not currently publish state-level EL enrollment counts or percentages in a public, citable format.
