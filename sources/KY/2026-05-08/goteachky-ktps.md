## Findings (quoted/paraphrased from page content)

The Kentucky Teacher Performance Standards (KTPS) — modeled on the
InTASC standards — include the following relevant references:

- **Standard 1: Learner Development** — "learning patterns vary
  individually within and across the cognitive, **linguistic**, social,
  emotional, and physical areas..."
- **Standard 2: Learning Differences** — "The teacher shall use the
  understanding of individual differences and **diverse cultures and
  communities** to ensure inclusive learning environments..."
- **Standard 5: Application of Content** — uses "differing
  perspectives" / "authentic local and global issues" framing.

The KTPS document does **not** contain explicit references to
"English learner," "EL," "ELL," or "English language learner."

## Coding decisions for professionalStandardsMentions

- diverse: **true** (unchanged — Standard 2 "diverse cultures")
- cultural: **true** (unchanged — same reference)
- linguistic: **true** (unchanged — Standard 1 "linguistic")
- el: **false** (unchanged — no explicit EL references)
