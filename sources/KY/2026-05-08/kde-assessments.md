## Finding (quoted/paraphrased from WebFetch)

The KDE Assessments page lists "WIDA Assessments" as **Required** for
**Grades K through 12** as the "Language Proficiency assessment for
students who are English Learners (EL)." The administered components
named are:

- WIDA ACCESS Online
- WIDA Alternate ACCESS
- WIDA Kindergarten ACCESS
- Accommodated forms (Large Print and Braille)
