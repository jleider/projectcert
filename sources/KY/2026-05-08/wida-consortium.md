# WIDA Consortium membership — Kentucky

- Retrieved: 2026-05-08
- URL: https://wida.wisc.edu/about/consortium
- File: wida-consortium.html

## Finding

Kentucky is listed on the WIDA Consortium member map as a current
member. As a member, Kentucky administers the **ACCESS for ELLs**
suite (ACCESS Online, Alternate ACCESS, Kindergarten ACCESS) as its
annual English Language Proficiency assessment for K-12 ELs.

Corroborated by the KDE Assessments page (see kde-assessments.md),
which lists "WIDA Assessments" as required for grades K-12 for English
Learners.

## Coding decision

- elpAssessment.name: **ACCESS for ELLs** (unchanged)
- elpAssessment.consortium: **WIDA** (unchanged)
- elpAssessment.sourceUrl: **https://wida.wisc.edu/about/consortium** (unchanged)

No change from baseline.
