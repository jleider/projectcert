## SEA methodology

Kentucky defines English Learners (ELs) as students aged 3–21 whose primary language is a language other than English, per ESSA Section 8101(20). 

**Identification process:** Home Language Survey (HLS) administered upon initial entry to Kentucky public schools (703 KAR 5:070). If any response indicates a non-English home language, the WIDA Screener Online is administered in grades 1–12; students scoring 4.5 or above do not qualify for EL services.

**Denominator scope:** K–12 public school enrollment. The 634,614 figure for 2024–25 is derived from the reported ethnicity breakdown totals (White [Non-Hispanic] 460,913 + African American 68,542 + Hispanic 58,023 + Asian 12,972 + Hawaiian/Pacific Islander 1,130 + Native American 848 + Two or More Races 32,086).

**Snapshot date:** Fall (October snapshot standard for NCES alignment), though not explicitly confirmed in the single-year source.

**SEA overlapping data found:**
- Fall 2022–23: 44,309 EL students / 634,424 total = **6.98%**

**Conclusion: NO OVERLAP for 2018–2021 comparison.** Kentucky's SEA does not appear to publish historical EL enrollment percentages for 2018–2021 in public-facing data sources. The Education Facts page (the most recent state-published summary) only reports 2022–23 and 2024–25 figures. A direct year-by-year comparison against NCES fall 2018–2021 values is not possible based on available SEA publications.

**Note on trend:** The SEA-reported 6.98% (fall 2022–23) is substantially higher than NCES's 5.4% (fall 2021), suggesting either methodological changes, the state has not published data since 2021, or the SEA includes reclassified/exited ELs still in a monitoring period (NCES does not). This divergence warrants flagging.

## Notes / caveats

1. **No explicit fall 2023–24 SEA publication found.** The most recent complete dataset published by KDE on their Education Facts page is 2022–23 (44,309 EL students). The 2024–25 figure (54,252) appears to be announced in a February 2026 KDE news release / briefing rather than formally published in a data dashboard.

2. **"Multilingual learners" vs. "EL" distinction unclear.** KDE distinguishes between EL count (54,252 for 2024–25) and a broader "multilingual learners" count (84,000+). The 84,000 figure likely includes reclassified/exited ELs. We use the narrower EL count (54,252) consistent with NCES methodology.

3. **No published SEA historical table for 2018–2021.** Searches of the Education Facts page, Open House data portal, and state EL office pages did not surface a SEA-published multi-year EL enrollment series covering fall 2018–2021.

4. **Denominator verification.** Total enrollment 634,614 for 2024–25 is calculated from reported ethnicity totals; not directly confirmed as an officially published "total K–12 enrollment" figure. The 2022–23 total (634,424) is more explicitly cited.

5. **Snapshot date not formally documented.** While fall (October) is the standard NCES snapshot, the KDE announcement does not explicitly state the month or date of the 2024–25 EL count.
