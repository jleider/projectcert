Retrieved: 2026-05-08
Retrieved by: projectcert-2026

## Relevant content

The handbook identifies the program models LEAs may choose
(`English as a Second Language`, `Content-Based ESL`, `Sheltered
English Instruction`, `Dual Language`, `Newcomer Program`,
`Structured English Immersion`, `Transitional Bilingual Education`)
and discusses staffing requirements:

> LEAs must provide the personnel necessary to effectively implement
> the selected EL program(s); necessary personnel include teachers
> who are qualified to provide EL services, core-content teachers
> who are highly qualified in their field as well as trained to
> support EL students, and trained administrators who can evaluate
> these teachers.

The handbook does not prescribe the credential pathway itself
(that is left to Bulletin 746) but it does confirm that:

- Louisiana operates a Structured English Immersion model in some
  systems where teachers "have specialized training in meeting the
  needs of ELs, hold ESL certification."
- Bilingual programs (Dual Language, Transitional Bilingual
  Education) are real but optional at LEA discretion.
- There is no statewide mandate that all teachers complete EL or
  sheltered training to be certified — i.e.,
  `sei.mandatedForAllTeachers` remains `false`.

## Implication

Reinforces the Bulletin 746 reading: ESL endorsement is the primary
credential, Bilingual Specialist exists as a secondary add-on,
sheltered/SEI is a program model rather than a universal teacher
mandate.
