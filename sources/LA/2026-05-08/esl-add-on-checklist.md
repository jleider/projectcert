## What the document says

> English as a Second Language Add-on:
> The following two requirements must be completed to add "English
> as a Second Language" to an existing, valid Louisiana teaching
> certificate:
>
> 1. Valid* OS, Type A, B, C, or Level 1, 2, 3 teaching certificate
> 2. Complete 12 semester hours of coursework, (four 3 hour courses)
>    as listed below and reflected on official transcripts:
>    - Methods of Teaching ESL
>    - Introduction to Language and Culture
>    - Structure of the English Language
>    - Curriculum Design for the Multicultural Classroom

> Revised in accordance with Bulletin 746, Aug. 2023 Emergency Rule
> (anticipated final Jan. 2024). Requirements may be subject to
> change pursuant to state law and policy.

## Implication

Operationally confirms the §1359 reading: 12-hour coursework path,
no test, no practicum, no language-proficiency exam. This is the
form an ESL applicant actually fills out — i.e., the rubber-meets-
road version of the regulatory text.
