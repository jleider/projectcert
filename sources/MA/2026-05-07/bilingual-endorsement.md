# Bilingual Education Endorsement — MA DESE

Source: https://www.doe.mass.edu/licensure/endorsements/bilingual-ed.html
Plus 603 CMR 7.14(3) (https://www.doe.mass.edu/lawsregs/603cmr7.html?section=all)
Retrieved: 2026-05-07

## Type

Add-on **endorsement** to a primary Massachusetts educator license.
The DESE page frames it as "must be properly qualified in the field
and grade level of the assignment, AND hold the Bilingual Education
Endorsement" — i.e., it sits on top of a primary license.

There is no standalone "Bilingual Education" teacher license listed
on the Academic PreK-12 field-grade-levels page. Bilingual Education
appears only in the endorsements list (alongside SEI, Autism,
Transition Specialist).

## Requirements (per 603 CMR 7.14(3))

- Subject matter knowledge per 603 CMR 7.08 / Department guidelines.
- Field-based experience: 75 hours in a Pre-K–12 dual language /
  two-way immersion / TBE / other bilingual setting (practicum).
- Language proficiency: passing score on a foreign language test
  acceptable to the Department in the relevant target language
  (per 603 CMR 7.14(3)(a)1).
- MTEL Bilingual Education test (functions as the language-
  proficiency assessment): info at www.mtel.nesinc.com.

## Renewal

15 PDPs, applicable toward Primary license renewal as well.
