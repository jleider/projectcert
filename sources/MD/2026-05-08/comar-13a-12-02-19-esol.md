## What the regulation requires

To qualify for the ESOL certification, an applicant must complete
the credit pattern in sections B–D:

### B. Content Courses (21 sem hrs)
- 6 sh general linguistics + structure of American English
- 6 sh in a single modern foreign language (college level) — language
  proficiency component
- 3 sh cross-cultural studies
- 6 sh language learning / acquisition / psycholinguistics

### C. Professional Education (27 sem hrs total)
- 6 sh foundations of education
- 12 sh ESOL methodology (methods, reading for LEP, writing for LEP, ESOL
  tests & measurements)
- 3 sh inclusion of special-needs populations
- 6 sh **supervised observation and student teaching in ESOL, OR 1 year of
  successful ESOL teaching experience** (practicum requirement, with a
  prior-teaching alternative)

### D. Reading Courses (6 sem hrs)
Standard reading-methods sequence; ties to the science of reading.

## Mapping to schema (eld credential)

Maryland's "ESOL (Pre-K–12)" is the canonical ELD credential. Per COMAR
13A.12.02.19:

- offered: true
- standalone: true (own program-of-study leading to a certificate)
- addOn: true (also available as endorsement; see endorsements page)
- requirements:
  - program: true (state-approved program is the standard pathway)
  - coursework: true (explicit semester-hour matrix)
  - practicum: true (6 sh student teaching OR 1 yr ESOL teaching)
  - test: true (Praxis 5362 ESOL is required by MSDE per the licensure
    overview; see endorsements update + ESL teacher edu summary)
  - languageProficiency: true (6 sh in a modern foreign language is
    explicitly required — this is rare nationally)

## Notes worth flagging

The 6-sh modern foreign language requirement is unusual and was already
present in the baseline rule. We are upgrading
languageProficiency from `false` (baseline-2019) to `true`: the prior
record appears to have coded this as a *student* language requirement
rather than a *teacher* language requirement. The current COMAR text
(and the secondary ESL Teacher Edu summary) make clear it is a teacher
candidate requirement.
