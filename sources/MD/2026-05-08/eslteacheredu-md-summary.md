# Secondary corroboration: TESOL Certification in Maryland (ESL Teacher Edu)

- URL: https://www.eslteacheredu.org/maryland/
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08
- Use: secondary cross-check of the COMAR 13A.12.02.19 mapping. Not the
  primary source — primary is COMAR — but useful for confirming the
  Praxis test code and the program/endorsement dual pathway.

## Relevant points

- ESOL is offered both as a **standalone** initial certification (e.g.,
  Salisbury University BA in ESOL) and as an **add-on endorsement** for
  practicing Maryland teachers.
- Required Praxis: ESOL **5362** + Teaching Reading: Elementary **5205**.
- Practicum: supervised student teaching is part of approved programs.
- Foreign-language requirement: 6 sh in a modern foreign language is
  enumerated.

## Mapping

Confirms eld.standalone=true, eld.addOn=true, eld.requirements.test=true,
eld.requirements.practicum=true, eld.requirements.languageProficiency=true.

Maryland does **not** offer a separate "Bilingual Education" credential
(no DBE/DLBE/TBE certification track in COMAR 13A.12.02). Bilingual
remains: offered=false, standalone=false, addOn=false. SEI is not
mandated for all teachers (no statewide SEI mandate analogous to AZ/CA/MA).
