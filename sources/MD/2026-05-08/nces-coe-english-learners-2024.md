## Key data point for Maryland

Figure 1, "Percentage of public school students who were English learners
(ELs), by state or jurisdiction: Fall 2021":

> MD, 11.2

Maryland is in the "10.0 percent or higher" band. NCES groups MD with the
13 states (and DC) where ELs were >= 10% in fall 2021: AK, CA, CO, DE, HI,
IL, MD, MA, NV, NM, RI, TX, WA.

Figure 4 (LIEP service rate): Maryland rounds to 100.0 percent (footnote
clarifies "Nevada and Maryland, nearly all ELs received services in LIEPs
(rounding to 100.0 percent).")
