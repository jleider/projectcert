## Confirmation

- Senate Bill 781 (Chapter 232, 2016) created Education Article §7-208,
  the Maryland Seal of Biliteracy Act.
- Approved by Governor 26 April 2016; effective 1 July 2016; first
  awarded in 2017.
- COMAR 13A.03.07 is the implementing regulation.
