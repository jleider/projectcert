# Maine DOE — Administrative Letter on Legal Requirements for ELA Services (Revised Oct 9, 2025)

- Source: https://mainedoenews.net/2025/10/10/administrative-letter-legal-requirements-to-provide-english-language-acquisition-services-to-students-who-are-multilingual-learners-revised-october-9-2025/
- Snapshot: `admin-letter-2025-10.html`

Most recent (Oct 9, 2025) Maine DOE administrative letter restating federal/state legal duties to MLs. Key points relevant to schema:

- Reaffirms 660 ESOL endorsement as the credential to design, oversee, and implement English language support programs.
- Sheltered instruction is one of several optional service components LEAs may use; it is not a statewide mandate for all teachers. Quote: districts "determine the components of an effective English language acquisition program tailored to the needs of each student, which may include, but is not limited to, tutoring, additional classroom support, materials, sheltered instruction, professional development for content area teachers, or other strategies."
- WIDA ACCESS test administrators do not need the 660 endorsement.
- Content-area teachers cannot use English proficiency as a prerequisite to grade-level content classes.

No schema field flips on this letter alone, but it cements the post-2019 status quo that the 660 endorsement is the operative ELD credential and SEI is not universally mandated.
