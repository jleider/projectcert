# Maine DOE — Certification Requirements landing page

- Source: https://www.maine.gov/doe/cert/requirements
- Snapshot: `doe-cert-requirements.html`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

Top-level certification requirements landing page maintained by the Maine DOE Office of Higher Education and Educator Support Services. Directs applicants to Chapter 115 (Part I and Part II) for the binding rules. Confirms Chapter 115 Part II "Section 1.8 Endorsement 660 (ESOL)" as the canonical reference for ESOL endorsement requirements; for ESOL specifically, the page also points to the dedicated 660 educator pathway under the Multilingual Learner program.

Used to anchor the JSON's primary `Maine Department of Education` source and to confirm that `https://www.maine.gov/doe/cert/requirements` is the live cert-requirements URL (the original 2019 source URL was `https://www.maine.gov/doe/home`).
