# Maine DOE — Multilingual Learner program homepage

- Source: https://www.maine.gov/doe/learning/multilinguallearner
- Snapshot: `doe-multilingual-learner.html`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

Hub page for Maine DOE's Multilingual Learner program. Confirms:

- Maine uses **WIDA ACCESS** as its annual ELP assessment.
- Maine is a **member of the WIDA Consortium** (since 2013).
- Maine defines a multilingual learner as "a student with a primary or home language other than English who is in the process of acquiring English."
- Universal identification is required; districts are pointed to the Maine WIDA ID & Placement Guidance.
- ESOL services are delivered through a **660 Educator Pathway**.

No EL percentage is published on this page — that figure is sourced from NCES (see `nces-table-204-20.md`).
