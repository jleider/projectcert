# Maine DOE — English Language Acquisition Service Provision and Staffing Guidance

- Source: https://www.maine.gov/doe/learning/multilinguallearner/services
- Snapshot: `doe-multilingual-services.html`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

Operational guidance for Maine LEAs on staffing English language acquisition (ELA) services. Key passages:

- **"ELD services must be delivered or directly overseen by certified ESOL (660) educators."** — confirms 660 is the operative endorsement.
- Each SAU must designate a "certified Director/Coordinator of Multilingual Programs."
- "Ed Techs, tutors, or other educators cannot provide the core ESOL services required by civil rights law, even under supervision."
- The "consultation model" is explicitly disallowed.
- General classroom teachers are expected to **collaborate** with ESOL specialists and integrate language objectives, but **no statewide SEI mandate is imposed on all teachers** — the document does not require sheltered English instruction training as a condition of any non-ESOL certification.
- WIDA ACCESS Test Administrators do **not** need the 660 endorsement; only an ESOL-endorsed teacher may design/oversee/interpret the language program.

Schema impact: confirms `eld.offered=true`, `sei.mandatedForAllTeachers=false`. The 660 endorsement is the gate to providing the core service, supporting `eld.addOn=true`.
