# Maine Initial Teacher Certification Standards (InTASC, Rev. 8/2016)

- Source PDF (Bowdoin College Education Dept. publication of Maine's adopted InTASC standards): https://www.bowdoin.edu/education/pdf/maine-initial-teacher-certification-standards-and-course-alignment.pdf
- Snapshots: `maine-initial-teacher-standards.pdf`, `maine-initial-teacher-standards.txt`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

Maine's Initial Teacher Certification Standards are the **InTASC Core Teaching Standards**, adopted whole. Chapter 115 Part II references "Maine's Initial Teacher Standards" as the assessment basis for portfolio review (e.g., Section 1.8.B.3.f for ESOL Pathway 3). Maine has not promulgated state-specific teaching standards beyond InTASC.

## Keyword scan

- **diverse** — Standard #2: "uses understanding of individual differences and **diverse** cultures and communities to ensure inclusive learning environments..."
- **cultural** — Standard #2 ("diverse **cultures** and communities"). Stem `cultur-` present.
- **linguistic** — Standard #1: "patterns of learning and development vary individually within and across the cognitive, **linguistic**, social, emotional, and physical areas..."
- **English learner / EL / ELL** — **not present** in any of the 11 standards. The terms do not appear.
