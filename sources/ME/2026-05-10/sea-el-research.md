# Maine — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2023-24
- **Snapshot date:** 2023-10-01
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 7,208
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 172,600
- **EL percent:** 4.2%
- **Source label:** Maine Department of Education; Education Indicators for Maine
- **Source URL:** https://educationindicators.me/maine-school-enrollment-2/
- **Local terminology:** Multilingual Learner (ML); formerly English Learner (EL)

## SEA methodology

Maine identifies students as multilingual learners (formerly called English learners) through a Language Use Survey that screens for "a primary/home language other than English" combined with lack of English proficiency. The state uses WIDA (World-Class Instructional Design and Assessment) standards for screening and exit thresholds. Schools must identify multilingual learners within 30 days of enrollment per federal civil rights law.

The state reports enrollment counts based on the October 1 annual snapshot, which is certified for that school year's funding allocations. Essential Provisions and Services (EPS) funding for the next school year is based on the prior year's October 1 enrollment counts. The October 1, 2023 count of 7,208 multilingual learners represents 4.2% of Maine's 172,600 K-12 public school enrollment for the 2023-24 school year.

The most recent SEA-published figure (7,208 for 2023-24) cannot be directly benchmarked to the prior NCES observations (5,420 in fall 2021). The 26% increase between fall 2021 (5,420) and fall 2023 (7,208) represents either a real surge in multilingual learner identification or a methodological shift, but without overlapping SEA-published data for 2021-2023, continuity cannot be assessed.

The Maine DOE does publish an interactive Comprehensive Multilingual Data Dashboard, but it does not expose historical aggregate percentages or counts for the 2018-2021 period in a consolidated table format suitable for comparison.

## Notes / caveats

- Maine rebranded "English Learner (EL)" to "Multilingual Learner (ML)" in recent years. The underlying identification process (Language Use Survey + WIDA screening/exit) remains consistent.
- The 26% increase from NCES fall 2021 (5,420) to Maine DOE fall 2023 (7,208) is substantial and merits attention. Possible explanations: (1) genuine population growth (Maine has received New Mainers / refugee populations in recent years, particularly Somali and Portuguese speakers), (2) more rigorous or expanded identification, or (3) methodological artifact. Without SEA-published historical data for 2021-2023, the source cannot be pinpointed.
- The 4.2% figure for 2023-24 represents a 15% increase from the prior school year (2022-23), indicating rapid growth in the multilingual learner population.
- The denominator (172,600 K-12) is from fall 2023 enrollment reported in Maine Public news coverage; this aligns with October 1 certified enrollment used for state funding.
- The snapshot date is October 1 per Maine statute and administrative practice for annual enrollment certification.
- Terminology note: Maine's Language Use Survey asks about "a primary/home language other than English"—the state does not distinguish between "classified" (identified) and "ever-classified" (reclassified) in the headline figure; the distinction exists in detailed dashboard data but not in the aggregate 7,208 count used for funding and accountability.
