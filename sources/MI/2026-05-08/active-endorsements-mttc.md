# MI MDE — Active Endorsements and MTTC Alignment

- URL: https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/ed-serv/ed-cert/educator-preparation-providers/specialty-program-standards/active-endorsements-mttc
- Local: `active-endorsements-mttc.html`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08 (HTTP 200)

This MDE page lists every active Michigan teaching endorsement and the
corresponding MTTC subject test required for the endorsement. It
confirms:

- **NS — English as a Second Language**: requires MTTC ESL exam.
- **Y\_ — Bilingual Education** (language-specific): requires the MTTC
  Bilingual Education exam in addition to the ACTFL OPI proficiency
  requirement specified by the Bilingual Education preparation
  standards (Standard 1.1).

Used to corroborate `credentials.bilingual.requirements.test = true`
and `credentials.eld.requirements.test = true` (previously `null`).
