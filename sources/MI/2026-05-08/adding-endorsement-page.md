# MI MDE — Adding an Endorsement to a Michigan Teaching Certificate

- URL: https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/ed-serv/ed-cert/cert-guidance/becoming-a-teacher/adding-an-endorsement
- Local: `adding-endorsement-page.html`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08 (HTTP 200)

## Key facts

- Endorsements (including ESL/NS and Bilingual/Y\_) can be added to a
  Michigan teaching certificate by completing a state-approved
  endorsement program through an approved Educator Preparation
  Provider (EPP) AND passing the appropriate MTTC.
- Confirms add-on pathway: `credentials.bilingual.addOn: true` and
  `credentials.eld.addOn: true` (no change).
- Combined with the Placement Guide: confirms standalone pathway as
  well, since both endorsements can also be earned as the initial
  endorsement on a teaching certificate
  (`credentials.bilingual.standalone: true`,
  `credentials.eld.standalone` requires reconsideration —
  see changes-from-baseline.md).
