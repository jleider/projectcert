## Key facts grounding the schema fields

- Bilingual endorsement is **language-specific**, with codes YA-YT
  covering 17 named languages (French, German, Greek, Russian, Spanish,
  Italian, Polish, Hebrew, Arabic, Other, Vietnamese, Korean,
  Serbo-Croatian, Chaldean, Chinese, Filipino, Japanese).
- **Standard 1.1** (the "most significant change" from the 2004
  standards): candidates must demonstrate at least **Advanced Low** on
  the **ACTFL Oral Proficiency Interview (OPI)** in BOTH English and
  the target non-English language. This is an explicit, named
  language-proficiency exam requirement.
- **Program Requirements** (p. 6) for endorsement recommendation
  include:
  - Approved program of study (≥20 semester hours K-12, paired with ≥30
    semester hours in the primary content area for initial
    endorsement);
  - Demonstrated proficiency in English and the endorsed language per
    Standard 1.1;
  - **Structured field experiences with gradual release of
    responsibility, inclusive of student teaching**, in elementary,
    middle school, and high school settings (= practicum);
  - Coursework in growth and development;
  - Coursework in instructional methods with strategies for ELs/MLs;
  - Language coursework prerequisite/co-requisite beyond the first 4
    semesters of language instruction;
  - For initial certification, a separate ≥20 semester hour
    professional education program aligned to **MI-InTASC** Model Core
    Teaching Standards.

## Standards-language mentions

The introduction explicitly references "linguistically and culturally
diverse learners," "English Learners," and "multilingual learners" in
the foundational beliefs (pp. 2-3). Cultural-perspective standards
(2.1-2.5) are unchanged from 2004. This document — read as part of
Michigan's professional preparation standards corpus — directly
addresses cultural and linguistic diversity and explicitly names ELs.

## What this document does NOT state

- A separate MTTC test number; passage of the relevant MTTC test for
  the endorsement-specific bilingual exam is required per the placement
  guide (and confirmed via MTTC alignment and university program pages
  citing MTTC 122 for Bilingual).
