## Key facts grounding the schema fields

- Statutory anchor: **Michigan Law 380.1233(1)** requires that teachers of
  ELs be appropriately endorsed for the program type and position.
- Endorsement codes:
  - **English as a Second Language (NS)** — single ESL endorsement.
  - **Bilingual Education (YA-YT, Y\_)** — language-specific bilingual
    endorsements (Spanish YF, Arabic YK, Chinese YR, etc.).
- Either NS or Y\_ endorsement is required for any teacher serving as
  the primary ELD provider; both are offered as standalone endorsement
  programs and can be added to an existing certificate (add-on).
- **Pathways to certification/endorsement** (p. 5 — applies to BOTH NS
  and Y\_):
  - "All pathways to certification and/or endorsement require successful
    completion of an approved preparation program, passage of
    endorsement-specific state assessments, and the addition of the
    endorsement to a valid teaching certificate."
  - Traditional route: state-approved teacher prep program at an IHE.
  - Alternative route: non-traditional prep under an Interim Teaching
    Certificate (ITC).
  - Master's in ESL or Bilingual Ed at an approved IHE.
  - Master's in TESOL alone does NOT fulfill the requirement.
- **Bilingual Education additional requirement** (p. 4-6, 17-19): a
  Bilingual Education endorsement OR an ESL endorsement combined with
  EITHER a corresponding world language (FA-FS) endorsement OR passage
  of "the appropriate Oral Proficiency Interview (OPI) or similar
  language proficiency exam at the required level aligned to the ACTFL
  Proficiency Guidelines 2024."
- SEI is not mandated for all teachers in Michigan; LEAs must staff EL
  programs (sheltered, ESL, newcomer, dual language) with teachers
  holding NS or Y\_ endorsements, but general-education teachers are
  not required to obtain an SEI/ESL credential.
- WIDA ELD Standards are the operative ELD standards (referenced
  throughout — confirms WIDA membership).

## What this document does NOT directly state

- It does not enumerate the specific MTTC test number(s); see
  active-endorsements-mttc.md for that. (Cross-state convention: MTTC
  126 ESL, MTTC 122 Bilingual.)
- It does not define `program: true/false` per-credential; in MI both
  ESL and Bilingual require an approved prep program — coded `true`.
