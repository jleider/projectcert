# MI MDE — English as a Second Language (ESL) Course Coding & Placement page

- URL: https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/ed-serv/ed-cert/permits-placement/departmentalized-courses/english-language-literature/esl
- Local: `esl-placement-page.html`
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08 (HTTP 200)

The web companion to the ESL & Bilingual Placement PDF. Lists the
ESL-related course codes (01992 ELD, 01008 ESL, 23xxx Self-Contained
Newcomer / Sheltered codes), and confirms that ESL courses must be
taught by a certified teacher holding either an NS or Y\_ endorsement.

Used to corroborate `credentials.eld.offered: true`,
`credentials.eld.addOn: true`, and `credentials.eld.standalone` (per
the Placement Guide PDF, ESL/NS is offered as a standalone endorsement
on a teaching certificate via an approved EPP — see
adding-endorsement-page.md and bilingual-education-standards.md).
