## Key facts grounding the schema fields

- The NS (English as a Second Language) endorsement requires:
  - Completion of an SBE-approved teacher preparation program
    (program = true);
  - Coursework in linguistics, second-language acquisition, methods,
    assessment, and culture;
  - Field experiences / student teaching (practicum = true);
  - Passage of the MTTC ESL exam (test = true).
- Unlike the Y\_ standards, the NS standards do NOT require a separate
  language-proficiency exam (no ACTFL OPI requirement) — i.e.,
  `languageProficiency: false` for ESL is consistent with the
  preparation standards.
- The standards explicitly address the role of culture, linguistics,
  and the needs of "English learners" / "multilingual learners"
  throughout (Standards 1.x and 2.x).

This document corroborates that ESL (NS) is offered as a standalone
endorsement program and as an add-on to existing certificates, with
program + coursework + practicum + test as the four core pathways
elements and no separate language proficiency requirement.
