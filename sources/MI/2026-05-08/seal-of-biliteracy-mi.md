## Adoption

Michigan adopted the Seal of Biliteracy in **2018** (State Board of
Education action, January 2018; first cohort awarded 2018-19 school
year). Sources: sealofbiliteracy.org state directory; MDE Michigan
Seal of Biliteracy program page; MISD policy summary; multiple
secondary references in 2018-2020 national Seal of Biliteracy reports.
