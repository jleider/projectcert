## Summary

Minnesota is a long-standing WIDA Consortium member state and uses
the **ACCESS for ELLs** suite (incl. Alternate ACCESS) for annual
English language proficiency measurement of K–12 ELs. The MDE EL
Education page identifies WIDA as the standards provider and
references "WIDA (World-Class Instructional Design and Assessment)"
under its assessment resources, alongside guidance on standardized EL
identification procedures and LIEPs.
