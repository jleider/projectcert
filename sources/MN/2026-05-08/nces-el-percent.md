# NCES — Minnesota English Learners Percentage

URL: https://nces.ed.gov/programs/digest/d23/tables/dt23_204.20.asp
Retrieved: 2026-05-08

## Datum

NCES Digest of Education Statistics Table 204.20 ("English learners
[ELs] enrolled in public elementary and secondary schools, by state or
jurisdiction") reports Minnesota's EL percentage at **8.8%** for fall
2021 (the most recent year in the 2023 Digest).

## Coding

- `elPercent`: 8.5 → 8.8
- `elPercentAsOf`: "2019-10-01" → "2021-10-01"

Per the project's working principle to prefer NCES over SEA-specific
counts for cross-state comparability.
