# MN Rule 8710.4150 — Teachers of Bilingual/Bicultural Education

URL: https://www.revisor.mn.gov/rules/8710.4150/
Retrieved: 2026-05-08
Effective: 2001-09-01; last amended L 2017 1Sp5 art 12 s 22.

## Scope (Subpart 1)

Authorizes the holder to teach academic content in students' native or
first language at the grade levels of the prerequisite classroom-
teacher license, for the purpose of giving English learners access to
curriculum while developing both their first language and English.

## Licensure requirements (Subpart 2)

The candidate must:

- Hold a baccalaureate degree from a regionally accredited institution.
- **Hold a prerequisite Minnesota license**: elementary education, or
  a 5–12 / 7–12 license in mathematics, a science field, social
  studies, or health education.
- Demonstrate oral and written proficiency in the students' native
  language under Subpart 3.
- Meet effective-practice standards under part 8710.2000.
- Complete a PELSB-approved preparation program in bilingual/bicultural
  education.

The prerequisite-license requirement makes this an **add-on**
credential. There is no separate "bilingual" standalone licensure
field outside the elementary/secondary teacher's primary license.

## Language proficiency (Subpart 3)

Candidate must verify either:

- High-school or postsecondary education conducted in the target
  language, OR
- Advanced oral proficiency under ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines plus
  advanced written proficiency (with carve-outs for languages without
  written form).

## Subject-matter standards (Subpart 4, items A–I)

Linguistics; L1/L2 acquisition; history and culture; assessment;
communication skills; community communication; content-based language
instruction; English-language modeling (advanced ACTFL speaking);
instructional methods and materials.

## Coding decisions

- `bilingual.offered`: true
- `bilingual.standalone`: false — the rule requires a prerequisite
  Minnesota license. There is no separate bilingual-only license field.
  Baseline-2019 had `standalone: true`; this is a coding correction
  against the current rule (which has been on the books since 2001 and
  was last touched in 2017 — not a 2019→2026 regulatory change).
- `bilingual.addOn`: true (prerequisite license required; functions as
  an add-on)
- `bilingual.requirements.program`: true
- `bilingual.requirements.coursework`: true (9 enumerated competency
  areas A–I)
- `bilingual.requirements.practicum`: null — Subpart 4 does not specify
  a separate practicum/student teaching requirement for the bilingual
  add-on; the candidate's prerequisite licensure already includes
  student teaching, and the bilingual rule does not add explicit hours.
  Coding ambiguous → null.
- `bilingual.requirements.test`: null — MTLE Bilingual is not
  uniformly required; PELSB removed MTLE content/pedagogy/basic-skills
  test requirements for state-approved program completers as of
  2023-08-01.
- `bilingual.requirements.languageProficiency`: true (Subpart 3 ACTFL
  advanced oral + advanced written, or target-language schooling)

Baseline-2019 had `bilingual.requirements.program: true` and
`languageProficiency: true` only; coursework now coded `true` based on
the explicit competency list.
