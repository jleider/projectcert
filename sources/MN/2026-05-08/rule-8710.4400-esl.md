# MN Rule 8710.4400 — Teachers of English as a Second Language

URL: https://www.revisor.mn.gov/rules/8710.4400/
Retrieved: 2026-05-08
Last amended: L 2017 1Sp5 art 12 s 22 (published 2017-08-21)

## Scope (Subpart 1)

Authorizes the holder to provide K–12 instruction "designed to provide
English language instruction to students whose English proficiency is
not sufficient to benefit from the curriculum through mainstream
instruction in English or to collaborate and consult with other
classroom teachers for the purpose of integrating language and content
teaching for English learners."

## Licensure requirements (Subpart 2)

A candidate must:

- Hold a baccalaureate degree from a regionally accredited institution.
- Demonstrate the standards for effective practice for beginning
  teachers under part 8710.2000.
- Show verification of completing a PELSB-approved preparation program
  under chapter 8705.

There is **no requirement to hold a prior teaching license**. ESL is
structured as a standalone licensure field.

## Subject-matter standards (Subpart 3, items A–J)

Ten competency areas covering: English-proficiency modeling +
candidate's own L2 study (≥ 2 yrs HS or 1 yr postsecondary in another
language); methods and program models; content-based methodologies;
cross-cultural communication; communication-skills instruction;
assessment; linguistics (phonology, morphology, syntax, semantics);
L1/L2 acquisition theory; cultural pluralism and sociolinguistics;
integrated educational principles.

## Field experience and student teaching (Subpart 3a)

- ≥ 100 hours of field-based experience prior to student teaching.
- Experience teaching at three grade spans: K–6, 5–8, 9–12.
- Student teaching: minimum 12 continuous weeks, full time, face-to-
  face, supervised by a cooperating teacher and evaluated at least
  twice by qualified faculty supervisors.

## Coding decisions

- `eld.offered`: true
- `eld.standalone`: true (own preparation program; no prerequisite
  license required)
- `eld.addOn`: false (rule does not establish ESL as an endorsement
  added to another license; it is a standalone licensure field)
- `eld.requirements.program`: true (PELSB-approved program required)
- `eld.requirements.coursework`: true (10 enumerated competency areas)
- `eld.requirements.practicum`: true (100 hrs + 12-wk student teaching)
- `eld.requirements.test`: null — Effective 2023-08-01 the MTLE content
  test is not required for completers of MN-state-approved licensure
  programs (per PELSB Compliance Manual). MTLE ESL exists but is not
  uniformly required. Coding ambiguous → null.
- `eld.requirements.languageProficiency`: false — the rule asks the
  candidate to model English and have studied a second language for
  two years (HS) / one year (postsecondary), but it does not require
  formal proficiency *in another language* the way the bilingual rule
  does. The L2-study clause is a coursework prerequisite, not a
  proficiency assessment.

Baseline-2019 had `eld.addOn: false` and the requirements all null.
Coursework, practicum and program flags are now affirmatively `true`
based on the explicit text of the current rule; coding correction.
