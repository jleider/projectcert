## Summary

Minnesota adopted bilingual/multilingual seals through the 2014 LEAPS
Act ("Learning English for Academic Proficiency and Success Act").
sealofbiliteracy.org records the adoption date as 2014-05-16. The
codified Bilingual/Multilingual Seals provisions in
120B.022 subd. 1b were placed by 1Sp2015 c 3 art 2 s 2 with subsequent
amendments in 2017, 2023, and 2024.

The current statute defines two tiers:

- Bilingual gold seal — "intermediate high" ACTFL proficiency in one
  additional language; multilingual gold for multiple languages.
- Bilingual platinum seal — "advanced-low or above" ACTFL proficiency
  in one additional language; multilingual platinum for multiple.

A separate Minnesota World Language Proficiency Certificate program
predates the seal (legislated 2009).
