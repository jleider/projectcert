Educator Evaluation and Professional Growth System
2024-2025 School Year
The Western Line School District will implement the Mississippi Educator and
Administrator Growth System (PGS) during the 2024-2025 school year for
observations and evaluations. The PGS is designed to improve student
achievement by providing teachers, administrators, and other licensed personnel
with feedback to inform continuous improvement.
All administrators using the PGS rubrics to observe and evaluate staff will receive
Professional Growth System (PGS) Combined Rubric Training offered by the
Mississippi Department of Education (MDE). Upon completion of the training,
each administrator will provide a copy of his or her completion certificate to the
central office.

and submitted to the Mississippi Educator Career Continuum Archive (MECCA).
