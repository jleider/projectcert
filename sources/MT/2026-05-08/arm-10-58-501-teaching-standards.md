# ARM 10.58.501 — Teaching Standards

- URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/montana/Mont-Admin-r-10.58.501
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

## What the rule does

This is the Montana teaching standards rule, governing the
professional standards an OPI-approved educator preparation program
must meet. It is the canonical document for
`professionalStandardsMentions.*`.

## Verbatim relevant text

- **Diverse / cultural / language**: "use understanding of individual
  differences and diverse cultures, languages, and communities,
  including American Indians and tribes in Montana and English
  Language Learners (ELL), to ensure inclusive environments that
  enable each learner to meet high standards."
- **Cultural (American Indian)**: "demonstrate understanding of and
  ability to integrate history, cultural heritage, language, and
  contemporary status of American Indians."
- **Linguistic**: "patterns of learning and development vary
  individually within and across the cognitive, linguistic, social,
  emotional" domains.
- **English Language Learners (ELL)**: explicitly named — see first
  quote.

## Schema implications for `professionalStandardsMentions`

- `diverse: true` — explicit "diverse cultures, languages, and
  communities".
- `cultural: true` — explicit "diverse cultures" plus "cultural
  heritage" (Indian Education for All).
- `linguistic: true` — explicit "linguistic" within the cognitive/
  linguistic/social/emotional list.
- `el: true` — explicit "English Language Learners (ELL)". This is a
  change from baseline-2019, which coded `el: false`. The 2014
  predecessor standards did not name ELL specifically; current rule
  does.
