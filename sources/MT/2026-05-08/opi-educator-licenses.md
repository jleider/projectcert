# OPI Educator License Options & Requirements

- URL: https://opi.mt.gov/Educators/Licensure/Educator-Licensure/Educator-Licenses
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

## What it covers

Lists Montana's license classes (1–8). The ESL endorsement is added
to a Class 1 (Professional) or Class 2 (Standard) teaching license
under ARM 10.57.412. The page does not enumerate per-endorsement
coursework/practicum/test requirements; those are specified in
ARM 10.57.412 (and per-program-approval standards under chapter
10.58).

## Schema implications

- The license framework confirms that ESL is an add-on endorsement,
  not a standalone license class.
- The license-options page does not impose an SEI mandate on all
  teachers. SEI universal mandate remains `false`.
- All teachers must complete the free "An Introduction to Indian
  Education for All in Montana" online course — recorded as a
  cultural-competency baseline, not an EL credential requirement.
