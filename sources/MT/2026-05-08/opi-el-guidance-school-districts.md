# OPI English Learner Guidance for School Districts

- URL: https://opi.mt.gov/Portals/182/Page%20Files/English%20Language%20Learners/Docs/EnglishLearnerGuidanceForSchoolDistricts.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-05-08

## What the document is

OPI's federal-compliance guidance for districts on identifying,
serving, and assessing ELs. It addresses district obligations under
Title III / Title VI / EEOA, ELP screening (WIDA Screener), annual
ACCESS for ELLs administration, exit criteria, and parent
notification.

## Teacher-credential content

The guidance does **not** mandate that classroom teachers hold the
ESL endorsement. It references the federal "qualified" requirement
for Title III-funded EL programs and recommends professional
development. The K-12 ESL endorsement (06A ESL) remains optional for
classroom teachers; districts may staff EL programs with endorsed
teachers but the state does not require universal SEI training of
all teachers.
