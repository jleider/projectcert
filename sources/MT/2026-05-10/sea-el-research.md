# Montana — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

No newer data found. The Montana Office of Public Instruction does not publish state-level English Learner enrollment statistics through public-facing documents or dashboards beyond what is submitted to the National Center for Education Statistics (NCES).

- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 3,627

## SEA methodology

Montana's Office of Public Instruction (OPI) collects EL data through its AIM (Achievement in Montana) system, which is the statewide school data information system used for Title III Part A submissions to the federal government (EDFacts). The system tracks all students identified as EL, regardless of program participation.

**Definition:** Montana follows federal Title III definition. The OPI requires Home Language Surveys and standardized assessments to identify ELs. Students who score below proficient on the WIDA ACCESS for ELLs assessment are classified as EL.

**Denominator:** K-12 public school enrollment only.

**Snapshot date:** Fall (October). EDFacts collections align to federal reporting cycle.

## Comparison to NCES

NCES historical values provided (Digest Table 204.20):

- Fall 2019: 2.4%
- Fall 2020: 2.5%
- Fall 2021: 2.4%

Montana does not publish state-level annual reports that include comparable EL enrollment figures for the 2018-2021 overlapping period. The only overlap is NCES's own reporting.

**Conclusion: NO OVERLAP.** The OPI does not make state-level annual EL enrollment statistics publicly available through general-access reports, fact sheets, or dashboards. EL data is maintained in the secured GEMS (Growth & Enhancement of Montana Students) portal, which requires login credentials and is not publicly available without special data requests. No secondary sources (news releases, legislative reports, state fact sheets) publish Montana's EL enrollment percentages.

## Notes / caveats

- **Data source opacity:** Montana publishes total K-12 enrollment figures (148,585 for 2023-24) but does not break out EL subgroup enrollment in public announcements. The OPI maintains this data but restricts access to authenticated users of the GEMS system.

- **Recent EL assessment data:** The 2024-25 legislative presentation to the Senate Finance and Interim Committee (SFIC) mentions 3,176 EL students assessed for proficiency in 2024, with 34% demonstrating progress toward proficiency and 4% achieving proficiency. However, this is an assessment snapshot, not a full enumeration of the EL classification count, and does not map directly to total EL enrollment.

- **Data collection schedule:** The OPI collects EL participation data three times per school year (Fall, Testing Window, End of Year) through the AIM system, but state-level aggregate results are not published to the public.

- **2023-24 baseline:** Total public K-12 enrollment for Montana in 2023-24 was 148,585 students (down 1.3% from prior year). If the EL percentage remained at the 2020 rate of 2.5%, this would imply approximately 3,715 EL students, but this is an extrapolation only, not a cited figure.

- **Recommended next step:** To obtain current EL enrollment counts, contact the Montana Office of Public Instruction's Assessment or Data Reporting division directly. The GEMS Helpdesk (opigemshelpdesk@mt.gov or 406-444-5222) handles data requests and may supply state-level aggregates with appropriate authorization.
