# ESPB — English Learner (EL) Endorsement page

Source URL: https://www.nd.gov/espb/licensure/endorsement-information/english-learner-el-endorsement
Retrieved: 2026-05-08

The ESPB licensure page for the EL Endorsement (the ELD endorsement
referred to in NDAC 67.1-02-03-05 as "English language development
endorsement (24000)").

## Key statements (paraphrased and quoted)

- **Add-on endorsement** to a regular teaching license. Applied for
  through ND Teach with an $80 fee.
- Required course areas: Multicultural Education; Foundations of
  Second Language Instruction; 6 credits of Linguistics; Methods of
  teaching EL; Assessment of EL; Field Experience in teaching EL
  (2 credit minimum).
- **Praxis test pathway**: "Teachers may pursue a Praxis test for
  English to Speakers of Other Languages instead of completing all
  coursework." (i.e., a test-only pathway is available for the ELD
  endorsement.)
- Provisional pathway: if coursework is incomplete, the endorsement
  is added for two years; the holder must finish coursework or pass
  Praxis ESOL to keep the endorsement.

## Coding implications

- credentials.eld.requirements.test: **true** (Praxis ESOL is an
  alternative pathway — the SEA explicitly authorizes it).
- The page does not describe a separate bilingual endorsement; the
  bilingual variant is in NDAC 67.1-02-03-05 only.
