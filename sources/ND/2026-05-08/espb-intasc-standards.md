# ESPB — InTASC Standards (Final, August 2015) — adopted by ND ESPB

Source URL: https://www.nd.gov/espb/sites/www/files/documents/FINAL-INTASC-Standards-August-2015.pdf
Retrieved: 2026-05-08

ND ESPB's program-approval document, which formally adopts the
InTASC Model Core Teaching Standards as the standards all ND
educator preparation programs must meet. This is the operative
"professional teaching standards" document for projectcert's
`professionalStandardsMentions` coding.

## Search hits for the four canonical terms

### diverse / diversity (TRUE)

- "uses understanding of individual differences and diverse cultures..."
- "designs, adapts, and delivers instruction to address each student's
  diverse..."
- "values diverse languages and dialects and seeks to integrate them..."
- "Admission of Diverse Candidates who Meet Employment Needs."
- Many additional hits across InTASC standards 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9.

### cultural / culture (TRUE)

- "Human relations and cultural diversity. North Dakota education
  standards and practices board licensure requires coursework a
  minimum of two semester hours in multicultural education..."
- "the role of language and culture in learning"
- "incorporate culturally relevant content"
- "respond to learners' personal, family, and community experiences
  and cultural norms"

### linguistic (TRUE)

- "each learner's cognitive, linguistic, social, emotional, and
  physical development"
- "developmentally, culturally and linguistically..." (8(k))
- "modify instruction to meet learners' needs in each area of
  development (cognitive, linguistic, social, ...)"

### English learner / EL / ELL (TRUE)

- 2(e): "incorporates tools of language development into planning
  and instruction, including strategies for making content accessible
  to **English language learners**..."
- 2(i): "knows about second language acquisition processes and knows
  how to incorporate instructional strategies and resources to
  support language acquisition."
- "language learner specialists" referenced in collaboration
  standard.

## Coding implications

All four `professionalStandardsMentions` flags resolve to **true**
under ND's adopted InTASC standards:

- diverse: true
- cultural: true
- linguistic: true
- el: true   (was false in baseline-2019 — change!)

The 2019 baseline-2019 record had `linguistic: false, el: false`.
The current InTASC document explicitly references both linguistic
development and English language learners. This is consistent with
the August-2015 InTASC adoption being already in force in 2019, so
the baseline-2019 coding likely reflected a stricter reading of an
earlier ND-specific standards document or a coding difference.
Coded as a change in this refresh because the operative document
today contains these terms unambiguously.
