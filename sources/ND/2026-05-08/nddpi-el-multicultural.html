Automatic translation disclaimer

The State of North Dakota provides automatic translation for nd.gov websites, courtesy of Google Translate. Please remember that the English language version is considered the most accurate. English Learner/Multicultural Education

The North Dakota English Learner (EL) education program offers training and resources for educators and parents to identify and support multilingual learners. EL programs offer English learners opportunities to develop their English language skills while also learning academic content.

North Dakota EL Coordinators can find additional information and resources in NDDPI's EL Coordinator Teams application. If you are not currently a member and would like to be added, please contact the EL Programs Office.

Updated EL identification and reclassification criteria are now available on the 

EL Guidance and Resources page.
