# Seal of Biliteracy — North Dakota state page

Source URL: https://sealofbiliteracy.org/state/north-dakota/
Retrieved: 2026-05-08

State-specific page on sealofbiliteracy.org confirming North Dakota's
adoption of the Seal of Biliteracy program.

## Key facts

- ND adopted the Seal of Biliteracy program in **2019**.
- Operated by NDDPI as the "North Dakota Seal of Biliteracy (NDSB)"
  award program.
- Program lives within NDDPI's English Learner / Multicultural
  Education section: see also
  https://nd.gov/dpi/education-programs/english-learnermulticultural-education/seal-biliteracy
