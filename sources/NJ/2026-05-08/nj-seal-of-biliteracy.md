# NJ State Seal of Biliteracy

Source: https://www.nj.gov/education/standards/worldlang/SealofBiliteracy.shtml
Retrieved: 2026-05-08
Retrieved by: projectcert-2026

## Summary

"The NJDOE, pursuant to P.L. 2015, c. 303 enacted by the Senate and
General Assembly of the State of New Jersey, established the State
Seal of Biliteracy program in 2016."

NJ became the 15th state to legislate a Seal of Biliteracy
(January 19, 2016). Program requirements were later codified at
N.J.A.C. 6A:8-5.3.
