# N.J.A.C. 6A:9-3.3 — New Jersey Professional Standards for Teachers

Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-jersey/N-J-A-C-6A-9-3-3
Retrieved: 2026-05-08
Retrieved by: projectcert-2026

## Mentions found

### diverse / diversity
- Standard Two (Learning Differences): "diverse cultures and
  communities to ensure inclusive learning environments"
- Standard Two: "values diverse languages, dialects, and cultures and
  seeks to integrate them"
- Standard Eleven (Ethical Practice): "cultural, ethnic, gender, and
  learning differences to build stronger relationships"

### cultural / culture
- Standard One (Learner Development): "understands the role and impact
  of language and culture in learning"
- Standard Four (Content Knowledge): "knows how to integrate culturally
  relevant content to build on learners background knowledge"
- Standards Six and Eight: "culturally, and linguistically appropriate
  instructional strategies" / "developmentally, culturally, and
  linguistically appropriate instructional strategies"

### linguistic / language
- Standard One: "understands the role and impact of language and
  culture in learning"; "knows how to modify instruction to make
  language comprehensible"
- Standards Six and Eight: "culturally, and linguistically
  appropriate instructional strategies"
- Standard Two: "incorporates tools of language development into
  planning and instruction, including strategies for making content
  accessible to multilingual learners"

### English Learner / EL / multilingual learner / second language
- Standard Two: "strategies for making content accessible to
  **multilingual learners**"
- Standard Two: "process of **second language acquisition** and knows
  how to incorporate instructional strategies"
