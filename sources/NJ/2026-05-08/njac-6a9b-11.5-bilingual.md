# N.J.A.C. 6A:9B-11.5 — Bilingual/Bicultural Education endorsement

Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-jersey/N-J-A-C-6A-9B-11-5
Retrieved: 2026-05-08
Retrieved by: projectcert-2026

## Summary

NJ Bilingual/Bicultural Education is an **add-on endorsement** layered on
an existing instructional certificate (CE, CEAS, or standard NJ
instructional certificate). Two general pathways exist for the
endorsement itself:

1. **Approved program**: completion of a Department-approved educator
   preparation program at an accredited NJ college/university for the
   preparation of teachers of bilingual/bicultural education
   (CEAS pathway).
2. **Coursework**: completion of 12 credit hours of formal instruction
   covering linguistics, language acquisition, developing literacy
   skills for second-language learners, methods of teaching content in
   bilingual education, and theory and practice of teaching bilingual
   education (CE pathway).

- credentials.bilingual.offered: true
- credentials.bilingual.standalone: false
  - The endorsement explicitly requires holding/being eligible for
    another instructional certificate. It is **not** a primary,
    standalone teaching license. The 2019 baseline coded standalone:
    true; that appears to have been a coding error against current and
    historical NJ structure.
- credentials.bilingual.addOn: true
- requirements.program: true (CEAS path)
- requirements.coursework: true (CE path: 12 credit hours specified)
- requirements.practicum: null
  - 11.5 references exemption from the standard performance assessment;
    practicum specifics for the bilingual endorsement are not stated
    cleanly true/false in the cited regulation.
- requirements.test: null
  - 11.5 does not require a separate subject-matter ESL/bilingual test
    distinct from the language-proficiency requirement.
- requirements.languageProficiency: true
  - Department-approved nationally recognized test of oral and written
    proficiency in target language (or being primarily educated at the
    undergraduate/graduate level in the target language); plus English
    proficiency under 11.5 / cross-reference to 11.6.
