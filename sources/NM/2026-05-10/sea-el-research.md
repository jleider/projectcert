# New Mexico — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

**No newer data found.**

The New Mexico Public Education Department has not published state-level English learner enrollment counts or percentages more recent than fall 2021. The most recent figures available are from NCES Digest Table 204.20 (fall 2021).

## SEA methodology

### Current NMPED EL Identification Policy

The New Mexico Public Education Department defines English learners according to federal Title III requirements and state regulation. Per NMPED's State Policy for EL Identification and Proficiency Criteria (revised March 2023), all districts must identify and provide services to students based on home language surveys and English language proficiency assessments. The NMPED references WIDA English Language Proficiency (ELP) standards as guidance for identification and proficiency criteria.

New Mexico operates the STARS (Student Teacher Accountability Reporting System) and the newer NOVA data collection system for capturing K-12 student enrollment data by subgroup, including English learner classification. Both systems collect 40-day, 80-day, 120-day, and end-of-year enrollment snapshots.

### Data Availability

While NMPED maintains state-level enrollment data by subgroup (including EL classification) through NOVA and STARS, these data require login credentials to access and are not published as downloadable aggregate state-level files on the public NMPED website. The NOVA Reporting portal (at eui.ped.state.nm.us) provides access to reports for SY 2023-24 and beyond; the STARS Staging system contains reports for SY 2022-23 and prior. However, publicly accessible, pre-formatted tables with state-level EL counts are not available.

## Comparison to NCES

**NO OVERLAP — SEA does not publish historical values for fall 2022 or fall 2023.**

The most recent NCES Digest Table 204.20 data (2023 Digest edition, covering fall 2011 through fall 2021) shows New Mexico EL enrollment for the four years tested:

| School Year | NCES EL Count | NCES EL % |
|---|---|---|
| Fall 2018 | 51,107 | 15.3% |
| Fall 2019 | 52,898 | 16.0% |
| Fall 2020 | 49,497 | 15.6% |
| Fall 2021 | 59,564 | 18.8% |

The notable jump from fall 2020 (49,497 ELs, 15.6%) to fall 2021 (59,564 ELs, 18.8%) — an increase of approximately 10,000 identified ELs and 3.2 percentage points — warrants investigation. This shift may reflect changes in identification practices, data collection methodology, or pandemic-related reclassification.

### Search for SEA-Published Comparison Data

Extensive searches of the NMPED website, the NCES Digest State Dashboard for New Mexico, and public data portals (NOVA, STARS, NM Vistas) did not yield any publicly accessible state-level EL enrollment data for fall 2022 or fall 2023. The NMPED's EDFacts submissions (Title III data) are routed to the U.S. Department of Education, and no summary report is published by NMPED itself.

## Notes / caveats

1. **NOVA and STARS require authentication.** The two official NMPED data systems (NOVA for SY 2023-24 onward, STARS for SY 2022-23 and prior) are not publicly browsable. State-level aggregate reports are available only to users with NMPED login credentials.

2. **No SEA fact sheet, annual report, or public data download.** Unlike some states, NMPED does not publish an annual EL enrollment fact sheet, public data dashboard with state-level summary tables, or downloadable CSV/Excel files showing statewide EL identification counts.

3. **NM Vistas references enrollment subgroups but does not display state totals in free-text search.** The NM Vistas performance-reporting website (nmvistas.org) is described as containing "enrollment by subgroup" data, but the specific state-level EL count or percentage is not accessible via web search or non-authenticated access.

4. **Fall 2021 represents the most recent national snapshot.** NCES Digest Table 204.20 has not yet been updated to include fall 2022 or fall 2023 EL figures by state. The 2024 Digest edition (published December 2024) extends total enrollment through fall 2023 but does not yet include the EL-specific subdivision for that year. The next update is expected in the 2025 Digest.

5. **Methodology continuity unclear.** The sharp rise in EL count and percentage from fall 2020 to fall 2021 (3.2 percentage points) is unusual relative to the relatively stable 15–16% range in prior years. Without access to NMPED's methodology documentation or year-over-year data, the cause of this shift cannot be determined. It may reflect:
   - Pandemic-driven changes in identification or re-classification practices.
   - Changes in how NMPED reported EL status to NCES.
   - Changes in the underlying student population (overall enrollment shifts, in- or out-migration).

6. **NCES definition includes all identified EL students, regardless of program participation.** Starting in 2015, NCES counts all students identified as EL, not only those enrolled in EL programs. New Mexico's own data submission to NCES should follow the same scope.
