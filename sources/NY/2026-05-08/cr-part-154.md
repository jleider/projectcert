# CR Part 154 — NY Commissioner's Regulations on ELL/ML Services

**URLs:**
- https://www.nysed.gov/bilingual-ed/english-language-learner-and-multilingual-learner-regulations-compliance (snapshot: `nysed-cr-part-154.html`)
- https://www.nysed.gov/bilingual-ed/cr-part-154-comprehensive-ell-education-plan-ceep

## Scope

Part 154 of the Commissioner's Regulations holds all NY school districts accountable for identifying and serving English Language Learners. Districts must adopt ELL policies, plan services, deliver appropriate ENL/bilingual instruction, and evaluate academic achievement.

## Teacher certification mandate

Part 154 does **not** impose a universal SEI-style mandate that all NY teachers obtain ENL or bilingual certification. The certification mandates are role-specific:

- **ENL stand-alone instruction** must be delivered by a teacher certified in ESOL.
- **Bilingual education** instruction must be delivered by a teacher with a bilingual extension on top of their content-area certificate.
- **Integrated ENL** (co-teaching) must pair an ESOL-certified teacher with a content-area-certified teacher.
- **General classroom teachers** of ELLs are not required to hold ESOL/bilingual credentials but **must** dedicate **15% of their CTLE clock hours to language acquisition** addressing the needs of ELLs (per CR Part 154 PD requirement).

- `credentials.sei.mandatedForAllTeachers` = **false** — the Part 154 PD requirement is a continuing-education obligation, not a credential mandate. NY does not belong to the AZ/CA/MA universal-mandate cluster.
