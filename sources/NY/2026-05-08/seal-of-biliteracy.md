# NY State Seal of Biliteracy (NYSSB)

**URL:** https://www.nysed.gov/world-languages/new-york-state-seal-biliteracy-nyssb
**Snapshot:** `nysed-seal-of-biliteracy.html` (retrieved 2026-05-08)

## Adoption

- NY State Legislature passed the Seal of Biliteracy bill July 31, 2012.
- Signed into law and adopted by the Board of Regents in 2012; first awarded in the 2015–2016 school year.

The widely reported "2013" date refers to a later regulatory codification step. Treating 2012 as adoption year is consistent with sealofbiliteracy.org's state directory and the legislative history.
