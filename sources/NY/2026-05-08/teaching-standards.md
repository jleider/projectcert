# NY State Teaching Standards (2011)

**URL:** https://www.highered.nysed.gov/tcert/pdf/teachingstandards9122011.pdf
**Snapshot:** `nys-teaching-standards-2011.pdf` (retrieved 2026-05-08)

## Status

The 2011 NYS Teaching Standards remain the operative professional teaching standards adopted by the NY Board of Regents. They have not been formally superseded; new programs registered after Sept 1, 2027 will additionally need to align with the NYS Culturally Responsive-Sustaining Education (CR-SE) Framework, but the CR-SE Framework is not itself a replacement for the Teaching Standards.
