# OEQA — State Requirements for Educator Preparation

Source URL: https://oklahoma.gov/oeqa/educator-preparation/accreditation/state-requirements.html
Retrieved: 2026-05-08

## Bottom line for OK schema

- **State Requirement 1** explicitly references **"Emergent Bilingual
  (English Learner) students."** This is the operative professional-
  standard mention of EL students for educator preparation in Oklahoma.
  → `professionalStandardsMentions.el = true`.
- The requirement is satisfied by *either* novice-high foreign-language
  proficiency *or* knowledge/skills to address Emergent Bilingual
  (English Learner) needs. EPPs choose one pathway. So Oklahoma does
  not mandate EL training for *every* candidate, but the SEA does
  embed an EL-focused alternative in every educator preparation program.
- **State Requirement 5** mandates "60 hours of diverse field
  experiences prior to student teaching" → `diverse = true`.

## State Requirement 1 — verbatim

"The unit has a policy in place that ensures that educator preparation
candidates demonstrate: Conversational skills at a novice high level,
as defined by the American Council on the Teacher of Foreign
Languages, in a language other than English. Demonstration of
competency must occur prior to candidate completion of the educator
preparation program; **or** The knowledge and skills necessary to
address the needs of Emergent Bilingual (English Learner) students in
the P-12 classroom and are proficient in the strategies required for
successful delivery of P-12 instruction in that area."

## SEI mandate

Nothing here mandates sheltered English instruction for *every*
practicing teacher (the requirement is on the educator-preparation
*unit* level, with an either/or pathway). Combined with the EL FAQ's
acknowledgment that some LEAs have no EL teacher, OK is **not** an
SEI-mandate state.
→ `credentials.sei.mandatedForAllTeachers = false`.
