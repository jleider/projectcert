# OSDE — Full (Subject-Matter) Competencies for Licensure and Certification

Source URL: https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/osde/documents/services/teacher-certification/teacher-certification-resources/Competencies.pdf
Retrieved: 2026-05-08

## Bottom line for OK schema

- **English as a Second Language (ESL) (PK-12)** is a named subject-area
  competency (TOC entry, p. 16). Sub-domains: (A) Language, (B) Culture,
  (C) Planning, Implementation & Managing Instruction, (D) Assessment,
  (E) Professionalism. Includes "Demonstrate English fluency in
  listening, speaking, reading, and writing" — i.e., English language
  proficiency, not foreign-language proficiency.
- **No "Bilingual Education" entry.** TOC includes only ESL and
  "Foreign Languages." Oklahoma does not offer a Bilingual Education
  certification or endorsement of its own. The state's "Non-EL
  Bilingual" designation in EL data tracking is a *student* label, not
  a teacher credential. → `credentials.bilingual.offered = false`.
- **No mention of "English Learner," "ELL," or "LEP"** anywhere outside
  the ESL competencies block. The General Competencies (pp. iii–iv,
  HB 1549 of 1996) reference "individual differences of learners" and
  "diverse ethnic and cultural groups" but not English learners
  specifically. (See OEQA general-teacher-competencies for the current
  InTASC-aligned standards used in licensure — that's the operative
  professional-standards document.)

## ESL competencies — verbatim domains

(A) Language — fundamentals of linguistics, second language
acquisition theory, role of primary language, sociocultural variables.

(B) Culture — culture in language development, home/school
communication, ESL students' cultural identities, world events,
inclusive of "diverse cultural groups."

(C) Planning, Implementation, and Managing Instruction — standards-
based instruction, integration of listening/speaking/reading/writing,
phonology/morphology/orthography, reading instruction.

(D) Assessment — language proficiency assessment, varied assessment
strategies.

(E) Professionalism — knowledge of ESL field, advocacy for ESL
students, **"Demonstrate English fluency in listening, speaking,
reading, and writing the English language"** (this is a candidate
English-fluency expectation, not a non-English language proficiency
requirement → `eld.requirements.languageProficiency = false`).

## Document provenance

The PDF is the OSDE-published "Full (Subject-Matter) Competencies for
Licensure and Certification" document. The General Competencies
section (HB 1549) was adopted in 1996 by the Oklahoma State Board of
Education. Subject-matter competencies are revised periodically; the
ESL section retains 2010-era TESOL-aligned phrasing.
