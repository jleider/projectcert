# OSDE — English Learner Process and Practice FAQ (2025)

Source URL: https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/osde/documents/services/english-language-proficiency/el-faq-and-legal-obligations/FAQ%202025.pdf
Retrieved: 2026-05-08

## Bottom line: SEI is *not* mandated for all teachers in OK

Quote (FAQ 2025, p. 17, "What if my LEA does not have an EL teacher
or EL classes?"):

> "An LEA's responsibilities to an EL student, required under state
> and federal statute, are detailed above. The lack of EL staff
> and/or EL-specific classes does not exempt an LEA from finding ways
> to meet the language and learning needs of an EL student, providing
> scaffolded support, and making content-area information accessible
> for every learner."

OSDE's framing acknowledges that Oklahoma LEAs may not employ a
dedicated EL teacher. The state expects scaffolded support from
existing staff but does not mandate sheltered English instruction
training for every certified teacher.
→ `credentials.sei.mandatedForAllTeachers = false` (unchanged from
baseline).

## ESL endorsement remains optional, test-based add-on

The FAQ describes WIDA test administration certifications (a test-
proctoring credential), not a teaching endorsement. The teaching ESL
endorsement is added via the OSAT 177 (English as a Second Language)
exam — confirmed via CEOE (https://www.ceoe.nesinc.com/) and the
OSDE competencies document. → `eld.requirements.test = true`,
`addOn = true`.
