Sources retrieved 2026-05-08:
- https://oklahoma.gov/education/services/standards-learning/world-languages/seal-of-biliteracy.html
- https://sealofbiliteracy.org/state/ok (linked from sealofbiliteracy.org states list; the state-detail page was not in the public state listing on 2026-05-08, but sealofbiliteracy.org's main "Seal of Biliteracy States" list confirms OK is among the 40+ states with adoption)
- 70 O.S. § 11-103.2; OAC 210:10-1-16

## Bottom line for OK schema

- `sealOfBiliteracy.adopted: null → true`
- `sealOfBiliteracy.year: null → 2020`
  (established September 2020 by OSDE under 70 O.S. § 11-103.2;
  Oklahoma is the 40th state plus DC to adopt)
- `sealOfBiliteracy.sourceUrl`: switched from generic Wikipedia to
  the OSDE Seal of Biliteracy page (state-specific authoritative
  source).

## Two seal levels (OK-specific)

- Gold Seal — intermediate-mid proficiency
- Platinum Seal — advanced-low proficiency
