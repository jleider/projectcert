English Language Proficiency Assessment

Oregon is a member of the English Language Proficiency Assessment for the 21st Century (ELPA21), a group of states committed to supporting educators, school administrators, and communities as they adopt and implement the new English Language Proficiency (ELP) Standards and college- and career-ready standards.

The ELPA measures and reports on students’ English language proficiency in reading, writing, speaking, listening, and comprehension. Delivered online, ELPA is designed to be interactive and includes questions that reflect real-world scenarios. Its main purpose is to qualify students for appropriate language services and help guide schools to best support student needs.

Alternate English Language Proficiency Assessment (Alt ELPA)

The Alternate English Language Proficiency Assessment (Alt-ELPA) measures the English proficiency of eligible English learners with significant cognitive disabilities in grades K-12.

  • ​Alt ELPA User Guide (Updated 11/20/25)
    This User Guide contains step by step instructions for administering the Alt ELPA. It is a required element of Alt ELPA training, along with the requirements listed in Section 1.4 of the Test Administration Manual.
  • Alt ELPA Performance Standards (cut scores) 
  • Alt ELPA Proficiency Descriptors
  • Alt ELPA Performance Level Descriptors
  • Sample Tests​ are found on the OSAS Portal
Please contact the ODE Assessment Team​ for publicly available technical information about the Alt ELPA, such as publicly available sections of the Alt ELPA Technical Report.

  • Alternate Assessment Decision Making Guidance​​​
  • Domain Exemptions on ELPA: Definitions and Examples


  • ​ELP Standards for Alternate Instruction and Assessment
    This document details how Oregon’s adopted English Language Proficiency (ELP) standards apply for students eligible to participate in the Alt ELPA. It contains information about students experiencing the most significant cognitive disabilities, and describes levels of proficiency for each standard with examples specific to this student population. The performance expectations described in this document have been modified in depth, breadth, and complexity from performance expectations described in the ELP standards.
