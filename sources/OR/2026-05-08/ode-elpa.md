# ODE — English Language Proficiency Assessment

Source: https://www.oregon.gov/ode/educator-resources/assessment/pages/english-language-proficiency.aspx
Retrieved: 2026-05-08

> Oregon is a member of the English Language Proficiency Assessment for the
> 21st Century (ELPA21), a group of states committed to supporting
> educators, school administrators, and communities as they adopt and
> implement the new English Language Proficiency (ELP) Standards and
> college- and career-ready standards.

The ELPA measures students' English proficiency in reading, writing,
speaking, listening, and comprehension. Oregon is a founding member of the
ELPA21 consortium.

Alt-ELPA is administered for English learners with significant cognitive
disabilities.
