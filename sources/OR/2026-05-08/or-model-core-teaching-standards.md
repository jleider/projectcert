# Oregon Model Core Teaching Standards

Source: https://www.oregon.gov/ode/educator-resources/educator_effectiveness/Documents/or-model-core-teaching-standards.pdf
Adopted by State Board and TSPC (codified at OAR 581-022-2415 and the TSPC
Program Review and Standards Handbook). Aligned with InTASC.

The standards organize teaching into 10 standards across four domains:
(A) The Learner and Learning, (B) Content, (C) Instructional Practice,
(D) Professional Responsibility.
