# TSPC — Adding Endorsements (rules in effect 11/1/2023)

Source: https://www.oregon.gov/tspc/LIC/Documents/Endorsements%2012-2023.pdf

Two pathway tracks for endorsements:

## Endorsements requiring TEST + PRACTICUM
(Practicum required for Preliminary Teaching License only)

Advanced Math, Ag Science, Biology, Business: Generalist, Business: Marketing,
Career Trades: Generalist, Chemistry, English Language Arts, Family & Consumer
Science, Foundational ELA, Foundational Math, Foundational Science,
Foundational Social Studies, Health, Integrated Science, Physics, Social Studies,
Speech (Forensics), World Language: Chinese / French / German / Latin /
Russian / Spanish.

## Endorsements requiring a PREPARATION PROGRAM through a college (PCR pathway)

- Art
- Drama
- Elementary-Multiple Subjects
- **English to Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL)**
- Library Media
- Music
- Physical Education
- Reading Intervention
- Special Education: Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing
- Special Education: Early Intervention
- Special Education: Generalist
- Special Education: Visually Impaired
- World Language: Japanese

For the PCR pathway, applicants must submit:
- application + fee via eLicensing
- Program Completion Report submitted electronically by the college
- Official transcripts
- Score report for the required subject matter test (note: Drama, Japanese,
  Russian have no corresponding subject test)

## Implications for ESOL endorsement coding

ESOL is a PCR-pathway endorsement. The PCR is the gating credential —
i.e., **approved program is required**. The subject-matter test (ORELA ESOL)
is also required for ESOL specifically (only Drama / Japanese / Russian are
exempted from a subject test). Practicum is embedded in the approved program
rather than separately enumerated, but is a standard component of any
TSPC-approved EPP program (per TSPC Program Review and Standards Handbook).
Coursework similarly is embedded.
