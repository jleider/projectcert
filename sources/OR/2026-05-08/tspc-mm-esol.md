# TSPC — Multiple Measures-ESOL Endorsement Testing Option Worksheet

Source: https://www.oregon.gov/tspc/EPP/Documents/MMs%20for%20ESOL_6.2024_Approved.pdf
(June 2024 Commission Approval, revision date 9/13/24)

Optional alternative for EPPs to document candidate competency for the ESOL
endorsement *in lieu of* the ORELA ESOL subject-matter test. Candidates must
accumulate ≥70 of 110 points across three areas:

1. Content-area GPA (max 40 points; minimum 20 semester / 30 quarter hours
   aligned with ESOL standards OAR 584-420-0360, **plus a practicum/field
   experience or completion of a TSPC-approved ESOL program**).
2. Clinical experience observations in an ESOL classroom (max 30 points;
   requires ≥3 distinct observations).
3. Commission-approved subject-matter test (max 30 points).

The 70-point threshold can be met from any combination, so the test is not
strictly required if the candidate scores high enough in (1) + (2).

## Implications

This confirms that **practicum/field experience IS part of the ESOL
endorsement standards** (either as a standalone field experience, or
embedded in a TSPC-approved ESOL program). It also confirms coursework is
a structural requirement (≥20 semester hours aligned with ESOL standards).

The test (ORELA ESOL) remains the Commission's adopted gate, though the
Multiple Measures option allows a high-performing candidate to meet
competency without it. Because Multiple Measures still requires either a
test score or program completion + clinical observations, we continue to
code `eld.requirements.test: true` (the test is still the adopted mastery
measure; MM is an alternative pathway documented by EPPs, not a removal).

References OAR 584-420-0360 for ESOL standards, and aligns with the Adding
Endorsements doc in classifying ESOL as a program-pathway endorsement.
