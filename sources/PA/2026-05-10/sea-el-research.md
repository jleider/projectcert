# Pennsylvania — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2022-23
- **Snapshot date:** (FY 2022-23; presumed snapshot in fall 2022 per standard practice)
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 87,717
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** ~1.65 million K-12 public school students
- **EL percent:** ~5.3%
- **Source label:** Pennsylvania Department of Education, Division of Instructional Quality, ESL/Bilingual Program Area
- **Source URL:** https://www.pa.gov/agencies/education/data-and-reporting/enrollment
- **Local terminology:** English Learner (EL); formerly called ELL or LEP

## SEA methodology

Pennsylvania's definition of an English Learner is codified in 22 Pa. Code § 4.26 (ESOL Program Requirements): "a student whose dominant language is not English." The Pennsylvania Department of Education's Division of Instructional Quality collects and reports EL data via the Home Language Survey, with identification procedures requiring completion within 30 calendar days of the start of school or 14 days of enrollment during the school year if a student enrolls after the first day of school.

The denominator is K-12 public school enrollment only (excludes pre-K and adult education). Snapshot date is presumed to be October (fall) based on standard public K-12 reporting practice, though the SEA has not explicitly stated the snapshot month in available materials.

The Pennsylvania Department of Education provides annual downloads: "EL Student Counts by LEA and School" files for each school year (e.g., 2023-2024 file available at pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/education/documents/data-and-reporting/english-learners/2023-2024%20el%20student%20counts%20by%20lea%20and%20school.xlsx).

## Comparison to NCES

NCES Digest Table 204.20 (2023) provides Pennsylvania EL enrollment percentages for fall 2018–2021:

| Year | NCES % | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Fall 2018 | 4.0% | 68,443 ELs |
| Fall 2019 | 4.2% | 72,200 ELs |
| Fall 2020 | 4.2% | (methodology transition: 2019 onward from state-level collection) |
| Fall 2021 | 4.6% | (count: ~72,000–76,000 estimated based on percentage) |

**Conclusion: DIVERGES**

The FY 2022-23 SEA figure of 87,717 ELs (~5.3% of 1.65M total enrollment) represents a substantial increase from the NCES fall 2021 figure of 4.6%. The increase of ~0.7 percentage points year-to-year (fall 2021 to FY 2022-23) significantly exceeds the prior annual increments observed in the NCES series (which ranged from +0.2pp or flat). 

This divergence merits investigation. Possible explanations:
1. **Actual growth.** Pennsylvania's EL population accelerated post-pandemic (consistent with national trends and anecdotal reporting of immigration impacts in PA schools).
2. **Methodological shift.** Pennsylvania may have refined its EL identification procedure or definition scope between fall 2021 and FY 2022-23 (e.g., expanding the definition or screening criteria).
3. **Data quality / reconciliation.** The SEA may have conducted a reconciliation or backfill of prior-year data during the transition to state-level collection, affecting comparability.

**Recommendation:** Contact the Pennsylvania Department of Education (Data Quality Office: Ra-DDQDataCollection@pa.gov) to clarify whether the jump from 4.6% to 5.3% reflects actual enrollment growth, a methodological change, or both. The 2023-2024 and 2024-2025 EL enrollment files (available as Excel downloads) should be consulted to assess the trajectory post-2022-23.

## Notes / caveats

- The 2022-23 figure (87,717) is the most recent state-level total found via web search. The Pennsylvania Department of Education publishes "EL Student Counts by LEA and School" for each school year, but the aggregate state total is not prominently summarized on the SEA website—it must be derived by summing the Excel file or calculated from published percentage + total enrollment.
- The "more than 71,000 ELs" figure appearing in some SEA-adjacent summaries (e.g., Colorín Colorado) may refer to a different year or a subset of the population and should not be treated as the official state total without verification.
- Pennsylvania includes ELs "regardless of program participation" per the NCES methodology note, which is consistent with federal civil rights reporting requirements (Title VI, Language Minority Compliance Procedures).
- The snapshot month (October) is assumed based on NCES standard practice but has not been explicitly confirmed by the SEA in materials reviewed.
- Total K-12 enrollment (~1.5–1.65 million) is consistent with prior-year figures and the Commonwealth Foundation's reporting but was not independently verified against the most recent SEA enrollment dashboard for 2024-25.
