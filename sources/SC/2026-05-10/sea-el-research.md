The state's official term for classified EL students is "Multilingual Learner" (ML), defined through Title III enrollment processes.

## Notes / caveats

1. **No primary SEA source identified.** Despite extensive search of the South Carolina Department of Education's website (ed.sc.gov), including data portals, Title III program pages, Quick Facts documents, DashboardSC, and Active Student Headcounts, no SEA-published state-level EL enrollment totals or historical series were found. The 66,719 figure originates from Colorín Colorado, a secondary aggregator of state ELL resources, and its original source is not documented.

2. **Snapshot date unclear.** The cited 2022 figure lacks a specific month or formal snapshot date (e.g., October fall count). NCES uses October fall snapshots; South Carolina's student counting uses 45th, 135th, and 180th day snapshots. Unknown which applies.

3. **Terminology ambiguity.** South Carolina's official Title III program uses "Multilingual Learner" (ML) in contemporary documentation, but historical federal reporting and secondary sources may use "ELL" or "English Language Learner." The Colorín Colorado page uses "English language learners" in describing the 66,719 figure.

4. **No verification of classification vs. program enrollment.** The 66,719 figure is unverified as to whether it represents EL *classification* (identification) or program *enrollment*. These are distinct populations; federal EDFacts reporting captures both. Without access to South Carolina's submission records or detailed methodology, the population cannot be confirmed.

5. **No newer data found.** Searches for 2023–24 or fall 2023/2024 SEA publications yielded no results. The most recent available figure remains the 2022 estimate cited by Colorín Colorado.
