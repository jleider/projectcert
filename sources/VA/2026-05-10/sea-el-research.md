# Virginia — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2023-24
- **Snapshot date:** 2024-06-12
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 117,000 (rounded; sources report "over 117,000")
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** ~1,170,000
- **EL percent:** 10.0%
- **Source label:** Virginia Department of Education / ESEA Office (via UVA Literacy Center infographic)
- **Source URL:** https://literacy.virginia.edu/sites/g/files/jsddwu1421/files/value-series/inforgraphics/culturally-diverse-infographic-final-20240612.pdf
- **Local terminology:** EL; also known as English Learner, though Virginia also uses multilingual learner (ML) and reports students "whose primary language is not English"

## SEA methodology

Virginia's English Learner definition (8VAC20-132-10) applies to individuals aged 3–21 enrolled or preparing to enroll who:

1. Were not born in the United States OR whose native language is not English; OR
2. Are Native American or Alaska Native from an environment where a language other than English has had significant impact on English language proficiency; OR
3. Are migratory with a native language other than English

AND whose difficulties in speaking, reading, writing, or understanding English may be sufficient to deny them the ability to meet challenging state academic standards or to successfully achieve in classrooms where instruction is in English.

**Identification method:** Virginia uses WIDA Screener for Kindergarten and WIDA Screener for Grades 1-12. School divisions administer a home language survey to all enrolling students; if any non-English language is reported, students proceed to screening. Screening and parent notification must occur within 30 calendar days of enrollment.

**Denominator scope:** K-12 public school enrollment, measured on September 30 (Fall Membership report). Includes all district-based and charter schools.

**Snapshot date:** Fall Membership is collected on September 30 each year. The 2023-24 figure (10%) was published June 12, 2024, based on data collected September 30, 2023.

**Enrollment data base:** Title III Part A allocations are based on the Fall Student Record Collection. Virginia's Student Record Collection system captures enrollment by school division and is used for both state-level and federal ESEA reporting.

## Comparison to NCES

NCES Digest Table 204.20 (Fall 2011–2021) provides Virginia data for the comparison window:

| Year | NCES % | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Fall 2018 | 8.3% | — |
| Fall 2019 | 8.9% | — |
| Fall 2020 | 9.4% | — |
| Fall 2021 | 9.4% | — |

**Virginia SEA data for the same years (from Commonwealth Institute, Oct. 2021):**
- Fall 2019–2020: ~116,000 ELs, approximately 10%

**Comparison conclusion: DIVERGES**

Virginia's SEA figure for Fall 2019–2020 (ca. 10%) diverges from NCES Fall 2020 (9.4%) by approximately 0.6 percentage points. However, I found no Virginia state-published multi-year historical table for Fall 2018–2021 that would allow precise year-by-year verification against NCES. The Commonwealth Institute (October 2021) reported the 2019–2020 10% figure retrospectively; it is unclear whether this represents:
- A revised/updated count by Virginia using a different methodology than NCES, or  
- A rounding artifact (116,000 ÷ 1,160,000 ≈ 10.0%).

**Methodological continuity:** Virginia's definition and identification criteria (home language survey + WIDA Screener) are methodologically consistent with federal ESSA Title III requirements and NCES reporting. Virginia reports "identified" ELs based on WIDA screening, which aligns with NCES methodology. The admin code does not explicitly address reclassified/exited students, suggesting they are not included in the EL count (consistent with NCES practice). Without a side-by-side Virginia SEA publication for 2018–2021, definitive alignment cannot be confirmed, but the 0.6 pp gap is within a plausible range for methodology refinement or state-vs.-federal reporting differences.

## Notes / caveats

1. **No newer SEA data found:** Virginia has not published K-12 EL enrollment figures beyond the 2023–24 school year (as of May 2026). Fall 2024 (Sept 30, 2024) data collection would have been completed by October 15, 2024, but has not been publicly released in accessible form. The data may exist in Virginia's Student Record Collection backend but is not available via public dashboards or downloadable reports that are easily discoverable.

2. **Two conflicting EL counts in recent Virginia messaging:**
   - "Over 145,000" English Learners (mentioned in general VDOE statements; date/year unclear)
   - "117,000" English Learners for 2023–24 (UVA Literacy Center June 2024, sourced from ESEA Office)
   
   The 117,000 figure is the most recent, explicitly tied to 2023–24 data, and sourced from the ESEA Office at VDOE. The 145,000 figure appears in undated general statements and may reflect cumulative students over a longer period, pilot programs, or a different counting scope.

3. **NCES does not publish Virginia for Fall 2022 or later:** NCES Digest Table 204.20 data lags by ~3 years. Fall 2021 is the most recent published; Fall 2022 data is expected in future Digest releases. This means no NCES baseline exists yet to compare against the 2023–24 10% figure.

4. **Reclassified student policy:** Virginia's definition does not explicitly state whether reclassified (exited/proficient) English Learners remain counted. Standard practice aligns with NCES (which does not count reclassified students in the EL enrollment figures), but this assumption should be verified with VDOE if exact methodological alignment is critical.

5. **Total enrollment:** The denominator (1,170,000) is derived by calculating 117,000 ÷ 0.10 = 1,170,000. Actual Virginia public K-12 enrollment for Fall 2023 was reported as approximately 1,261,000–1,263,000 in late 2023. The discrepancy (about 90,000 students) suggests either:
   - The 10% is a rounded/approximated figure, or
   - The EL count includes additional scope beyond traditional district K-12 enrollment.
   
   Further verification with VDOE is recommended to confirm the exact denominator.
