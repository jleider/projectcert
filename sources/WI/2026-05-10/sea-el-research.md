# Wisconsin — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2024-25
- **Snapshot date:** 2024-09-20 (third Friday of September)
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** ~55,000
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 1,084,046 (K-12 only)
- **EL percent:** ~5.1%
- **Source label:** Wisconsin Department of Public Instruction (DPI)
- **Source URL:** https://dpi.wi.gov/families-students/programs-initiatives/el
- **Local terminology:** "English Learner (EL)" — also uses "ESL" and "ELL" in some historical/program contexts

## SEA methodology

**Definition**: Wisconsin defines English learners as students whose first language, or whose parents' or guardians' first language, is not English and whose level of English proficiency requires specially designed instruction.

**Denominator**: K-12 public school enrollment only (excluding pre-K and private schools).

**Snapshot date**: The third Friday of September beginning with the 2017-18 school year. Prior years used different reporting conventions. The FY 2024-2025 school census collected data as of the third Friday of September 2024 (September 20, 2024).

**Data collection system**: Wisconsin collects enrollment data through WISEdash, the state's public data portal. Enrollments are reported by districts as a headcount of students receiving their primary educational services through Wisconsin public schools. The state publishes certified data with an annual reporting cycle.

## Comparison to NCES

NCES historical values for Wisconsin (Digest Table 204.20):
- Fall 2018: 5.9%
- Fall 2019: 6.0%
- Fall 2020: 5.2%
- Fall 2021: 5.9%

**Comparison outcome: CLOSE**

Wisconsin's most recent SEA-published figure (~5.1% for 2024-25) is within the 0.5 percentage point threshold of the fall 2021 NCES value (5.9%), though on the lower side of that range. The downward trend from NCES 2019 (6.0%) to 2020 (5.2%) aligns with Wisconsin policy research documenting a decline in EL enrollment from 2019 onward. The Wisconsin Policy Forum documented that after growth from 2001-2019 (78.6% increase), EL enrollment declined 0.2% in 2019-20 and 2.1% in 2020-21. The current ~5.1% figure (if approximately 55,000 students in 1,084,046 total enrollment) suggests the decline has continued.

**Years compared**: Fall 2018-2021 (NCES) vs. available Wisconsin trend data from Wisconsin Policy Forum (2019-2021 actual enrollment counts).

**Methodological alignment note**: Both NCES (EDFacts Title III submissions) and Wisconsin DPI use October/September snapshots and K-12 public school denominators, making them broadly comparable. NCES classifies students by Title III eligibility (requiring English language proficiency assessment); Wisconsin counts all students identified as EL. The modest divergence from NCES 5.9% (fall 2021) to SEA ~5.1% (fall 2024) could reflect methodological differences in classification or genuine enrollment decline.

## Notes / caveats

1. **Data source limitation**: Wisconsin does not appear to publish a single, readily accessible state-level table with both the EL count and total K-12 enrollment side-by-side for the most recent school year. The ~55,000 EL figure comes from DPI's public summary ("Wisconsin serves nearly 55,000 English learner students"), while the 1,084,046 total K-12 enrollment comes from the FY 2024-2025 School Census Count. The percentage calculation (5.1%) is derived here from those two figures.

2. **WISEdash portal**: State-level EL enrollment data should be directly available through Wisconsin's WISEdash Public Portal (wisedash.dpi.wi.gov), which supports filtering by English learner status. However, web fetching of the interactive dashboard is not feasible; the portal requires direct user access to view disaggregated data by school, district, and state.

3. **Reclassified students**: Wisconsin's count includes all identified ELs. The research did not find explicit documentation on whether the count includes reclassified/former EL students in monitoring periods (NCES does not). This could account for part of the divergence from NCES figures.

4. **Program vs. classification**: Wisconsin appears to distinguish between "EL identification/classification count" (the figure we've used) and "program enrollment" (students in ESL/bilingual programs). The 25,427 figure mentioned in some DPI references represents students served in state-funded Bilingual-Bicultural (BLBC) programs, which is ~46% of all identified ELs — confirming that not all identified ELs receive program services.

5. **Snapshot date consistency**: Wisconsin shifted to "third Friday of September" reporting starting in 2017-18. Prior comparisons may use different dates. NCES uses October fall snapshot; Wisconsin's third-Friday-of-September snapshot typically falls in mid-to-late September, a minor timing difference.
