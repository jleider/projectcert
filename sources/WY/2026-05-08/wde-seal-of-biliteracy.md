## Key extractions

> "Senate Enrolled Act 47 created a Wyoming Seal of Biliteracy. The
> Wyoming Seal of Biliteracy (WY-SoBL) is an award given by the
> Wyoming Department of Education in recognition of students who have
> studied and attained proficiency in English and one or more
> additional languages by high school graduation."

- **Legislation**: 2022 Wyoming SF0078 (Senate Enrolled Act 47)
- **State adoption**: confirmed
- **Eligible languages**: English plus an additional language; the
  page explicitly notes that American Sign Language, classical
  languages, and Indigenous languages all qualify
- **Page last updated**: ~July 2024 per page metadata; legislation
  enacted 2022
