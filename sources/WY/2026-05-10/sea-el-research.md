# Wyoming — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

Wyoming's Department of Education maintains enrollment data through its data portal and reporting tools at `reporting.edu.wyo.gov`, which provide "Fall Enrollment K12 by English Learner Status" and "Fall Enrollment K12 by English Learner Home Language" reports covering 2008 to present. However, these interactive tools require direct navigation and do not publish summary tables with state-level totals on public-facing pages.

## SEA methodology

Wyoming defines English Learners as "those students in Wyoming schools whose primary language is a language other than English" (Wyoming Department of Education, English Learners page). The state uses a three-step identification procedure:

1. Home Language Survey completion upon enrollment
2. Follow-up Family Interview
3. WIDA Screener administration (Kindergarten to Grade 12)

Students identified as Active English Learners or in Monitor Status (monitoring period after reclassification) are tracked for Title III federal funding and the Wyoming Funding Model. The state uses ACCESS for ELLs and WIDA Alternate ACCESS as the English Language Proficiency Assessment; all Active English Learners are required to participate once per year.

Snapshot date: October 1 (fall snapshots only).

Denominator: K-12 public school enrollment only (excludes pre-K and post-secondary).

## Notes / caveats

1. **SEA data exists but is not publicly aggregated.** Wyoming's Department of Education confirmed in interviews with researchers that enrollment data "Fall Enrollment K12 by English Learner Status" is collected and available through their data portal, but the platform requires form submission and interactive navigation rather than publishing pre-computed state totals. This is consistent with the state's shift to the EDPASS system (implemented 2022-23 school year) for data collection, which changed how districts submit and states report granular data.

2. **No newer data found.** Multiple searches of Wyoming's website, EDFacts portal, state report cards, and assessment reports yielded no published EL enrollment figures for fall 2022, fall 2023, or fall 2024. The most recent NCES Digest Table 204.20 covers only through fall 2021; newer data may exist in Wyoming's internal systems but has not been publicly released or indexed.

3. **Methodology continuity:** Wyoming's definition (WIDA-based, same as prior years) and snapshot date (October 1) suggest continuity with NCES. However, without a direct SEA-published comparison table, methodological drift (e.g., in reclassification/monitoring-period inclusion) cannot be ruled out.

4. **Title III context:** Wyoming submitted 2023 data to the U.S. Department of Education via EDFacts as required for Title III federal funding accountability. This data is not readily accessible to the public but may be available through direct USDOE data systems (e.g., PEERS, NCES Public Use Microdata).
