## SEA methodology

The Alabama State Department of Education (ALSDE) publishes EL enrollment data through multiple channels:

1. **Federal Report Card System** (`reportcard.alsde.edu`) — state-level and district-level demographic data, including English learner classifications by school year.
2. **NCES coordination** — Alabama's data flows to the National Center for Education Statistics Digest Table 204.20.

Alabama's EL definition aligns with federal Title III classification standards. The state counts students identified as English Learners in public K-12 schools (denominator: total public K-12 enrollment, not including early childhood). The most recent methodologically consistent snapshot in the NCES public record is October (fall) of each school year.

Newer SEA-published data (Fall 2022 or later) was not located during this research.

## Notes / caveats

1. **No newer data found.** Despite multiple searches of the Alabama State Department of Education website, the Federal Report Card system, and the NCES Digest, no SEA-published EL enrollment figures for Fall 2022, Fall 2023, or Fall 2024 were located.

2. **Report Card system architecture.** Alabama's Federal Report Card (`reportcard.alsde.edu`) appears to house disaggregated (district and school-level) demographic data but does not display or make easily downloadable state-level summary aggregates in the web interface. The site structure suggests data export is possible but requires navigation of a dynamic interface not fully captured by web-scraping tools.

3. **NCES Digest lag.** NCES Digest Table 204.20 in the 2023 edition (most recent located) covers only through Fall 2021. The 2024 Digest edition may contain Fall 2022 data, but was not accessed during this research window.

4. **Alabama Framework document.** The Alabama Framework for English Learner Success (January 2023) references EL enrollment as "approximately 4%" of total enrollment but does not cite a specific count, total, or school year.

5. **EL classification vs. program enrollment.** Alabama's EL count in NCES and SEA reporting reflects students identified as English Learners under federal Title III standards, not program enrollment (bilingual/ESL participation). The distinction is clear in NCES documentation but should be verified with ALSDE if direct contact is made.

6. **Next steps for verification.** To obtain post-2021 SEA-published data:
   - Contact the Alabama EL Office directly (c/o ALSDE).
   - Navigate the Federal Report Card system and export state-level aggregates from the data portal (reportcard.alsde.edu).
   - Check if a 2024 or 2025 NCES Digest has been released with Fall 2022+ data.
