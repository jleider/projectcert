# Arkansas — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2021-22 (fall 2021 snapshot)
- **Snapshot date:** 2021-10-01
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 39,763
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 491,617
- **EL percent:** 8.1%
- **Source label:** NCES Digest of Education Statistics, Table 204.20
- **Source URL:** https://nces.ed.gov/programs/digest/d23/tables/dt23_204.20.asp
- **Local terminology:** Arkansas uses "English Learner" (EL) and "ESL" (English as a Second Language) terminology; some older state documents reference "ELL" (English Language Learner)

## SEA methodology

The most recent published state-level data comes from the NCES Digest of Education Statistics Table 204.20, which aggregates state submissions to EDFacts (the federal education data system). This represents a federal collection methodology rather than an independent Arkansas SEA publication.

Arkansas Department of Education maintains enrollment data through its Data Center (adedata.arkansas.gov) and Statewide Information System (SIS), which tracks "Student Home Language" and classroom enrollment by various demographics. However, **no state-level aggregate EL enrollment figures are published in a single accessible report** on the ADE website. The data systems exist to support districts but do not appear to publish consolidated state-level EL statistics for public consumption.

The NCES data represents K-12 public schools with October (fall) snapshots, consistent with the data provided to the projectcert template. NCES defines ELs as "all ELs, regardless of program participation," meaning it counts students formally identified as English Learners, not just those enrolled in EL/ESL programs (an important distinction per the research template).

**Denominator scope:** K-12 public schools only (CCD definition).

**Identification definition:** Students formally identified as English Learners through state identification procedures (which typically involve home language survey responses). Arkansas uses the Home Language Usage Survey (HLUS) for initial identification and the ELPA21 Screener for proficiency assessment.

## Comparison to NCES

Arkansas SEA does not appear to publish overlapping historical EL enrollment data for the fall 2018-2021 period in an accessible public format. The OEP (Office for Education Policy) at the University of Arkansas published a brief noting that EL enrollment reached "nearly 40,000 students" in 2018-19 (8% of enrollment), consistent with NCES figures for that period.

**Years compared:** Fall 2018 through fall 2021 (NCES source only, no parallel SEA publication found).

**NCES values provided in research template:**
- Fall 2018: 7.8%
- Fall 2019: 7.9%
- Fall 2020: 8.1%
- Fall 2021: 8.1%

**Verified against NCES Digest Table 204.20:**
- Fall 2018: 7.8% (confirmed: 38,501 / 493,737 = 7.8%)
- Fall 2019: 7.9% (confirmed: 39,318 / 497,378 = 7.9%)
- Fall 2020: 8.1% (confirmed: 39,265 / 485,202 = 8.1%)
- Fall 2021: 8.1% (confirmed: 39,763 / 491,617 = 8.1%)

**Comparison conclusion: NO OVERLAP**

The Arkansas SEA does not publish state-level historical EL enrollment percentages in a publicly accessible format that would allow a parallel comparison. While the data are maintained in the ADE Data Center's Statewide Information System (which includes "Student Home Language" reports at school and district levels), no consolidated state-level report or fact sheet aggregating these figures was located after extensive searching. The data would theoretically exist in district-level reports and could be aggregated manually, but this falls outside the scope of a "state-published" figure per the research template criteria.

## Notes / caveats

1. **No newer data than fall 2021:** The most recent published comprehensive EL enrollment data for Arkansas is the NCES Digest Table 204.20 figure for fall 2021 (8.1%). The NCES Digest 2024 edition still caps out at fall 2021 for Table 204.20.

2. **SEA data systems exist but are not aggregated for public consumption:** The Arkansas Department of Education's Data Center and Statewide Information System (SIS) maintain detailed "Student Home Language" enrollment data at the school and district levels for school years 2004-05 through 2025-26. However, extracting a state-level aggregate would require either (a) aggregating the district-level reports manually, or (b) using the ADE's data request system (DRRA, at adedata.arkansas.gov/drra/). No state-level summary report was found in public web search.

3. **SEA definition consistent with NCES:** Arkansas identifies English Learners through the Home Language Usage Survey (HLUS), in line with federal EDFacts requirements. The ELPA21 Screener is used for English Language Proficiency assessment. This methodology is consistent with the NCES definition of "all ELs, regardless of program participation."

4. **Growth trend:** Available data indicate Arkansas EL enrollment grew from approximately 4% of students in 2004-05 to 8% by 2018-19 (per OEP brief). The state has seen significant geographic diversification, with EL students present in all but 44 districts (down from 155 with zero EL enrollment in 2004-05). Over 30 districts now have at least 10% EL students (per OEP analysis of 2018-19 data).

5. **Potential for SEA publication:** The ADE does publish annual statistical reports via MySchoolInfo.arkansas.gov (the state's school performance reporting platform), and these reports include enrollment demographics. It is possible that state-level EL summary data exist within the 2024-2025 Annual Statistical Report, but the PDF was not extractable via web fetch.

6. **No contradictions with NCES data:** The single data point available from a quasi-state source (OEP's "nearly 40,000" and "8%" for 2018-19) aligns directly with NCES Table 204.20 figures for that year (39,318 students; 7.9%), supporting the inference that Arkansas's data collection methodology is continuous with the NCES series.
