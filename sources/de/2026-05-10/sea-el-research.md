# Delaware — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2023-24
- **Snapshot date:** 2023-09-30
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 18,778
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 141,279
- **EL percent:** 13.3%
- **Source label:** Delaware Department of Education Multilingual Learner Data (March 2025 revision)
- **Source URL:** https://education.delaware.gov/legacy/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2025/03/delaware-design-hq-multilingual-education-3-2025.pdf
- **Local terminology:** Multilingual Learners (MLLs); Delaware also uses "English Learners" (EL) interchangeably

## SEA methodology

Delaware identifies English Learners (termed "Multilingual Learners" in recent publications) using a three-component Alternative English Learner Identification Protocol: (1) Parent Questionnaire, (2) Observation Protocol, and (3) English Learner Determination Narrative. For grades 1–12, the WIDA Screener assessment is administered to identify students as EL. Delaware is a founding member of the WIDA Consortium (since 2003) and uses the WIDA English Language Development Standards.

The denominator is K-12 public school enrollment only (pre-K excluded from the 141,279 figure). Snapshot date follows the state's standard reporting date of September 30 of the reported school year.

## Comparison to NCES

NCES Digest Table 204.20 reports:
- Fall 2018: 10.6%
- Fall 2019: 10.9%
- Fall 2020: 10.6%
- Fall 2021: 11.5%

Delaware SEA does not publish multi-year EL enrollment data in a format accessible via web search (no historical EL enrollment table available for 2018–2021 comparable to NCES). The most recent federal CRDC data required for 2021-22 and 2023-24 school years; however, CRDC data portal access (https://civilrightsdata.ed.gov) did not yield readable EL counts during research.

**Conclusion: NO OVERLAP.** Delaware's SEA-published data begins with the 2023-24 school year (13.3%). No SEA-published EL figures for 2018–2021 were located to allow year-by-year comparison with NCES. The 2023-24 percentage (13.3%) is notably higher than the most recent NCES figure (11.5% in fall 2021), suggesting either methodological differences, a genuine increase in the EL population, or continued growth following the 2019 baseline period. Without overlapping years, methodological continuity cannot be established.

## Notes / caveats

- Delaware uses the term "Multilingual Learner" (MLL) in official 2025 publications alongside the term "English Learner" (EL); both refer to the same population.
- The 2023-24 count of 18,778 represents "more than 13%" of the 141,279 total K-12 enrollment (calculates to 13.3%).
- A more recent preliminary count for 2024-25 shows approximately 19,000 MLLs in nearly the same proportion (~13% of ~142,500 total), but final verified 2024-25 figures were not available.
- The EL population has grown significantly from ~14,000 (10%) reported in earlier years to 18,778–19,000 (13%+) in 2023-25.
- No reclassification/monitoring-period data found in search results; unclear whether Delaware's count includes former-EL students in monitoring period (unlike NCES, which does not).
- Delaware sources define the denominator as K-12 public schools only, matching NCES scope.
- Snapshot date (September 30) aligns with NCES standard October fall snapshot practice.
