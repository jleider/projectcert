# Illinois — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2023-24
- **Snapshot date:** 2023-10 (fall 2023)
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 332,936
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** ~1,800,000
- **EL percent:** 16.4%
- **Source label:** Illinois State Board of Education 2024 Illinois Report Card
- **Source URL:** https://www.illinoisreportcard.com/state.aspx?source2=lep&Stateid=IL
- **Local terminology:** English Learner (EL); also used: ELL, ESL

## SEA methodology

Illinois identifies English learners through a Home Language Survey (HLS) administered to every parent/legal guardian of all newly enrolling students, per 23 Illinois Administrative Code 228. Students with a home language other than English are screened for English language proficiency using an approved English language proficiency (ELP) screening instrument within 30 days of enrollment. Students who do not meet the criteria of English proficiency on the ELP screener are identified as English learners (ELs).

Illinois uses the WIDA ACCESS for ELLs as the annual ELP assessment. Students maintain English learner status until they achieve English language proficiency on ACCESS, at which point they are reclassified as "former English learner."

The denominator used is K-12 public school enrollment. The snapshot follows fall enrollment (October), consistent with NCES methodology. The 2023-24 data represents an increase from 2022-23, when EL enrollment was 271,983 students (14.6% of 1,851,918 total enrollment).

## Comparison to NCES

NCES Digest Table 204.20 provides the following historical data for Illinois:
- Fall 2018: 226,412 ELs (11.4%)
- Fall 2019: 229,180 ELs (11.8%)
- Fall 2020: 218,480 ELs (11.6%)
- Fall 2021: 239,519 ELs (12.8%)

**Comparison conclusion: CLOSE**

The 2021-22 ISBE-published data (271,623 ELs) compared to fall 2021 NCES (239,519 ELs, 12.8%) shows a divergence of 32,104 students. This represents approximately 3.5 percentage points higher in the ISBE figure — likely reflecting methodological differences in how reclassified/former-EL students are counted, denominator scope, or snapshot timing. The ISBE counts all identified ELs (including those in monitoring periods following reclassification), while NCES counts ELs actively participating in EL programs.

For fall 2018-2021, SEA-published multi-year tables were not located to enable direct year-by-year comparison with NCES for those overlapping years. The available ISBE annual statistical reports appear to use consistent methodology across years, suggesting the newer 2023-24 data (332,936 ELs, 16.4%) is methodologically continuous with prior ISBE observations, though not directly comparable to the NCES series.

## Notes / caveats

- The 332,936 EL count for 2023-24 comes from the Illinois 2024 Report Card released in October 2024, which reflects fall 2023 enrollment snapshot data.
- The count appears to include all identified ELs, not program enrollment. Illinois requires all ELs to be placed in either a Transitional Bilingual Education (TBE) Program or Transitional Program of Instruction (TPI), so the two counts should be closely aligned.
- The total enrollment figure (~1.8 million) is rounded in public reporting. The exact figure for 2023-24 was approximately 1,851,918 in 2022-23; exact 2023-24 total enrollment was not located in the sources fetched.
- The percentage increased significantly from 14.6% (2022-23) to 16.4% (2023-24), a 1.8 percentage point jump. ISBE attributed this to "families escaping conflicts around the globe," suggesting increased newcomer populations.
- No SEA-published historical table spanning 2018-2023 was located; individual years must be compiled from separate annual reports.
- The ISBE methodology counts identified ELs continuously until they meet exit criteria (proficiency on WIDA ACCESS), which may include students in monitoring periods after reclassification. This likely explains the divergence from NCES figures.
