## SEA methodology

Louisiana defines English Learners through a standardized entrance and exit process aligned with Every Student Succeeds Act (ESSA) requirements. The primary identification tool is the English Language Proficiency Screener (ELPS), which all students who indicate a home language other than English must complete upon enrollment. Students scoring below proficient on the ELPS are identified as EL and receive an EL Accommodations Checklist with supportive services.

Exit criteria require students to demonstrate proficiency across all domains on the English Language Proficiency Test (ELPT), scoring a combination of 4s and 5s. The snapshot date for Louisiana's EL data collection appears to align with the fall reporting period (October), consistent with NCES methodology.

The denominator scope is K-12 public schools. Louisiana statutes use "Limited English Proficiency (LEP)" synonymously with EL in regulatory text, though the agency prefers "English Learner (EL)" in contemporary publications.

## Notes / caveats

- **PDF access limitation:** The official Louisiana EL Data Profile 2024-2025 PDF could not be fully parsed. The 35,660 count and 5.3% figure were extracted from web search summaries citing the document, but the full contents (including prior-year historical tables, detailed snapshot dates, and footnotes on methodology) remain unverified by direct document inspection.
- **Snapshot date assumption:** Louisiana's EL data is inferred to be October 1, 2024 (fall snapshot) based on NCES convention and the SEA's stated use of ESSA-aligned entrance/exit criteria. The exact snapshot date is not explicitly confirmed in the sources accessed.
- **Total enrollment derived, not verified:** The 673,000 total enrollment figure is back-calculated from the 5.3% and 35,660 count (35,660 ÷ 0.053 ≈ 673,000). Alternative sources cited ~677,000 public K-12 students in Louisiana for 2024, which would yield 5.3% × 677,000 ≈ 35,881 EL students—consistent with the reported 35,660 within rounding. Direct verification of Louisiana's official total enrollment denominator was not accessed.
- **No prior-year SEA data found:** The research did not locate older versions of Louisiana's EL Data Profile (e.g., 2023-2024, 2022-2023) that would enable a year-by-year comparison check between SEA and NCES values for 2018-2021 overlap. This comparison would be valuable for assessing methodological continuity.
- **Historical note:** Louisiana uses both "EL" and "LEP" in different statutory/policy contexts. User-facing agency materials prefer "EL," but regulations and some technical documents retain "LEP" (Limited English Proficiency).
