# English as a Second Language (ESL) Teacher License — MA DESE

Source: https://www.doe.mass.edu/licensure/academic-prek12/teacher/field-grade-levels.html
Plus 603 CMR 7.04, 7.06 (https://www.doe.mass.edu/lawsregs/603cmr7.html?section=all)
Retrieved: 2026-05-07

## Type

Standalone **teacher license** under Academic PreK-12. Two grade
spans: PreK–6 and 5–12.

There is no "ESL endorsement" in MA — ESL is a primary teaching
license, not an add-on. (DESE's endorsements list, current 2026,
contains only SEI, Bilingual Education, Autism, Transition
Specialist.)

## Requirements

Initial license issued under 603 CMR 7.04 generally requires:
- Bachelor's degree.
- Completion of a Department-approved educator preparation program
  OR an equivalent route.
- Subject matter knowledge per 603 CMR 7.06 / DESE-published ESL
  guidelines (coursework).
- Practicum (per 603 CMR 7.04 / educator-prep regulations).
- MTEL Communication & Literacy + MTEL ESL Subject Matter Knowledge
  test (Test 54).

ESL teachers do not need a foreign-language proficiency test (that
requirement is bilingual-only).
