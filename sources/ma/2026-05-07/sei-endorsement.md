# SEI Endorsement — MA DESE

Source: https://www.doe.mass.edu/licensure/endorsements/sei.html
Retrieved: 2026-05-07

## Mandate scope

"Core Academic Teachers" (early childhood, elementary, teachers of
students with moderate and severe disabilities, English, reading,
language arts, mathematics, science, civics and government,
economics, history, geography), Academic Administrators
(principal/assistant principal, supervisor/director), and — as of
July 1, 2021 — vocational educators must hold the SEI endorsement
to be assigned ELLs (or acquire it within one year).

Initial-licensure applicants have been required to hold SEI since
July 1, 2014.

## Pathways (per 603 CMR 7.14(1))

SEI Teacher Endorsement awarded upon demonstration of subject matter
knowledge per 603 CMR 7.08(3)(a)–(e) and 7.14(1)(b), via one of:

1. Department-approved course of study specific to providing
   sheltered English instruction.
2. Passing a Department-approved assessment.
3. Bachelor's degree in a Department-approved major, or other
   Department-approved graduate-level training.
4. Possession of an ESL or ELL license.
5. Equivalent out-of-state credential under NASDTEC Interstate
   Agreement.
