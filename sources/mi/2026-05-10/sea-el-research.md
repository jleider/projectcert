# Michigan — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2021-22
- **Snapshot date:** 2021-10 (fall count)
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 92,000
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 1,440,000
- **EL percent:** 6.4%
- **Source label:** Citizens Research Council of Michigan (citing Michigan Department of Education / CEPI data)
- **Source URL:** https://crcmich.org/michigans-changing-demographics-reflected-in-growth-of-english-language-learners
- **Local terminology:** English Learner (EL), also referenced as "students in the process of mastering the English language"

## SEA methodology

Michigan collects EL enrollment data through the **Michigan Student Data System** (MSDS), administered by the **Center for Educational Performance and Information (CEPI)**. Student counts are collected on the legislated **fall count day**, which is the **first Wednesday in October**, making the snapshot date typically October 2021 for the 2021-22 school year. The denominator includes all K-12 public school enrollment in Michigan, consistent with NCES Common Core of Data (CCD) reporting. Michigan's EL definition aligns with federal Title III requirements under the Every Student Succeeds Act (ESSA).

## Comparison to NCES

NCES Digest Table 204.20 figures for Michigan:
- Fall 2018: 6.4%
- Fall 2019: 6.3%
- Fall 2020: 6.3%
- Fall 2021: 6.4%

**2021-22 comparison:** The SEA figure of 6.4% in 2021-22 aligns with NCES fall 2021 figure of 6.4%. **Result: MATCHES.** The two data streams are methodologically continuous.

**Historical alignment:** For the overlapping NCES years (fall 2018-2021), the SEA data remains stable around 6.3–6.4%, consistent with the NCES trajectory. The slight variation (within 0.1 percentage point) across years suggests the SEA and NCES methodologies are aligned on EL identification, denominator scope, and snapshot timing.

## Notes / caveats

- No newer data found than 2021-22. While reports reference Michigan's EL population growing to "over 7%" and "more than 100,000 students" by 2023-24, these figures appear in secondary sources (EPIC report from November 2025, Education Trust opinion pieces) but I was unable to access the raw SEA-published numbers or the specific school year and snapshot date for the most recent figures. The official MI School Data portal (mischooldata.org) hosts a Student Enrollment Counts Report and English Learner Dashboard with live data, but the portal requires interactive access and secure login for detailed breakdowns and suppressed/unsuppressed counts.

- The 2021-22 figure of 92,000 students ÷ 1,440,000 total enrollment = 6.39%, which rounds to 6.4%.

- Reclassification status: NCES EDFacts Title III data do not include students exited from EL status but still in a monitoring period. Michigan's MSDS data appear to report based on current EL classification/identification status; the schema does not explicitly document whether monitoring-period students are included, but the alignment with NCES suggests consistent treatment.

- The 2010-11 to 2021-22 growth from 60,000 to 92,000 EL students represents a 53% increase, significantly outpacing the 10% decline in total K-12 enrollment over the same period, indicating demographic shift rather than methodological change.
