# Minnesota — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2023-24
- **Snapshot date:** 2023-10-01
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 84,050
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 869,967 (PreK-12)
- **EL percent:** 9.6%
- **Source label:** English Learners in Minnesota Report 2023-24 (Minnesota Department of Education)
- **Source URL:** https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED676212.pdf
- **Local terminology:** "English learner" (EL); Minnesota also recognizes "multilingual learner" in contemporary policy discussions, but "English learner" is the statutory term.

## SEA methodology

Minnesota defines an English learner under Minnesota Statutes, section 124D.59, subdivision 2, as "a pupil in kindergarten through grade 12 or a prekindergarten student enrolled in an approved voluntary prekindergarten program who meets the requirements" based on: (1) declaration by parent or guardian that the pupil uses a language other than English, and (2) determination by a valid assessment measuring the pupil's English language proficiency (using developmentally appropriate measures).

The Minnesota Language Survey (MNLS) is the initial screening tool; only students who complete the MNLS, participate in the grade-appropriate English language screening assessment (typically the WIDA Screener), and are identified as English learners may be enrolled in an English language development (ELD) program. Initial identification and parent notification must occur no later than 30 days after the beginning of the school year or within two weeks of initial enrollment if not enrolled at the beginning of the school year.

The denominator is K-12 public school enrollment only. The snapshot date is October 1, based on the Minnesota Automated Reporting Student System (MARSS) fall enrollment count. The reported figures include all identified English learners regardless of program participation (i.e., classification count, not program enrollment count). The MDE does not explicitly state whether this count includes students in active monitoring periods post-exit, though the identification framework permits former-EL students to move through the system.

## Notes / caveats

1. **PreK inclusion:** Minnesota's reported total enrollment (869,967) is listed as "PreK-12" in the 2023-24 report. NCES uses K-12 only. The PreK students enrolled in approved voluntary pre-K programs and school readiness programs are eligible for EL identification under Minn. Stat. 124D.59, so the denominator is slightly broader than NCES. This could account for a portion of the higher percentage reported by MDE versus the NCES K-12 baseline.

2. **Identification vs. program enrollment:** The task explicitly instructs "Report classification counts, not program enrollment counts." The figures cited are EL identification counts (all students flagged as EL) regardless of whether they are enrolled in a formal bilingual/ESL program. This is the correct measure and matches NCES methodology.

3. **Reclassified students in monitoring:** Minnesota's statutes and procedures do not explicitly state whether reclassified (exited) students in monitoring status are counted in the EL total. NCES does not include post-exit monitored students. If Minnesota's procedure differs, it would not be evident from the public summary data.

4. **Publication lag:** The 2023-24 report was published in June 2025 (based on the ERIC record date). The snapshot date is October 2023, making the data approximately 1.5 years old as of the research date (2026-05-10).

5. **Most common languages:** The 2023-24 report notes that the top languages spoken by ELs in Minnesota continue to be Spanish, Somali, Hmong, Karen languages, and Vietnamese, consistent with historical patterns.
