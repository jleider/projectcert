## SEA methodology

Mississippi defines English Language Learners (ELL) or English Learners (EL) as students who were not born in the United States or whose native language is other than English and come from an environment where a language other than English is dominant, per Title VII: Bilingual Education, Language Enhancement, and Language Acquisition Programs (20 U.S.C. § 7501(8)). 

The identification process begins with a Home Language Survey administered to all incoming students as part of the general enrollment process. Students whose surveys indicate a home language other than or in addition to English are flagged for further screening by the principal or ELL coordinator to determine whether formal English proficiency testing is needed to assess academic English competency.

Mississippi reports enrollment data by October 1 of each school year. The 2022-2023 enrollment figure (3.1% EL, approximately 18,500 students) represents state-level identification counts (not program enrollment), consistent with federal EDFacts methodology.

**Note:** The Mississippi Department of Education publishes enrollment data through MDEReports and Mississippi Succeeds Report Card platforms, but more recent figures (2023-2024 and 2024-2025) were not directly accessible in published aggregate form. The data portals may contain these figures for download, but the SEA does not appear to have published a consolidated state-level EL fact sheet or press release with 2023-2024+ figures.

The most recent SEA-published comparable data (2022-2023 school year, 3.1% EL) aligns well with the NCES historical pattern for Mississippi:

**Conclusion: MATCHES.** The 2022-2023 SEA figure (3.1%) matches the Fall 2021 NCES figure (3.1%) exactly, suggesting methodological consistency between the two sources. While the SEA data point is for the October 2022 snapshot (matching NCES's October fall snapshot convention), and the NCES 2021 data is the most recent published series, the alignment at 3.1% indicates the SEA's identification methodology has remained stable and comparable to the federal reporting standard.

**Caveat:** No direct SEA-published comparison across multiple overlapping years (2018–2021) was found. The conclusion is based on: (1) the 2022-2023 SEA figure matching the 2021 NCES figure, (2) consistency of the October snapshot date, and (3) the SEA's stated definition aligning with federal Title III classification criteria.

## Notes / caveats

1. **Most recent aggregate SEA figure is 2022-2023.** The Mississippi Department of Education's data portals (MDEReports Data Explorer and Mississippi Succeeds Report Card) are confirmed to contain enrollment data by school year through 2023-2024 and possibly 2024-2025, but these platforms do not return aggregate state-level EL counts in their web-facing summary reports. The figures would likely require direct download or navigation of the interactive data explorer by school year and subgroup.

2. **EL count estimate.** The "approximately 18,500" count for 2022-2023 comes from secondary sources (Migration Policy Institute and Mississippi First reporting), not a direct SEA publication. The 3.1% percentage and the 440,285 total enrollment (Fall 2022 NCES) yield approximately 13,649 EL students if calculated directly, suggesting either: (a) the "18,500" estimate includes older cohorts or students in monitoring periods, (b) is rounded or derived from a different denominator, or (c) reflects a later school year (possibly 2023-2024) not yet published by NCES or official SEA fact sheets. This discrepancy warrants clarification if the state is formally contacted.

3. **Title III funding gap.** Mississippi is one of only two states without specific state-level EL funding in its K-12 formula, relying entirely on federal Title III grants. Approximately 64.5% of districts with EL students did not receive Title III funding in 2022-2023, affecting over 2,100 EL students. This context does not change the enrollment count but underscores the state's reliance on federal funding for EL services.

4. **No newer data published.** Searches for 2023-2024 and 2024-2025 aggregate EL enrollment figures from the Mississippi SEA yielded no published fact sheets, press releases, or consolidated reports. The data portals are available but do not expose state-level EL totals in a downloadable or summarized format accessible to external researchers without interactive access to the platform.
