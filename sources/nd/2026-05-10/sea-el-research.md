# North Dakota — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

**NO NEWER DATA FOUND.** The North Dakota Department of Public Instruction publishes EL enrollment data by district through the State Automated Reporting System (STARS) and the Insights.nd.gov portal, but aggregated state-level EL enrollment figures for fall 2022, fall 2023, or fall 2024 are not publicly accessible as a single compiled total. A color-coded map showing district-level EL enrollments for the 2022-2023 school year exists on the EL Data and Reporting page, but the state does not appear to publish a consolidated state total with snapshot date and percentage breakdown.

## SEA methodology

The North Dakota Department of Public Instruction defines and tracks English Learners through the State Automated Reporting System (STARS), which requires:

- **Definition**: EL classification based on the Home Language Survey (HLS) required for all students at enrollment. The state uses "English Learner" terminology.
- **Denominator**: K-12 public school enrollment. The data portal Insights.nd.gov reports enrollment for grades pre-K through 12, with options to filter to K-12 only.
- **Snapshot date**: Fall reporting snapshots (October). Districts submit MIS01 Fall Report data including EL student enrollment records through STARS.
- **Privacy restrictions**: Due to FERPA regulations, districts reporting 1–24 EL students do not publish specific numbers; percentages are rounded to the nearest whole percent.

## Comparison to NCES

**Conclusion: NO OVERLAP.** NCES published EL enrollment data for North Dakota through fall 2021 (Table 204.20, Digest of Education Statistics 2023):

- Fall 2018: 3,997 ELs (3.5%)
- Fall 2019: 4,212 ELs (3.6%)
- Fall 2020: 4,018 ELs (3.5%)
- Fall 2021: 3,887 ELs (3.3%)

The North Dakota Department of Public Instruction does not appear to publish comparable multi-year state-level totals for fall 2022 onwards, either in historical tables or in current dashboards. The data exists in STARS at the district level but is not aggregated into a public state-level figure suitable for methodological continuity comparison.

## Notes / caveats

1. **District-level data exists; state-level does not.** The ND DPI website explicitly notes a color-coded map and data table for 2022-2023 showing EL student counts "reported by North Dakota public school districts." This is a district-by-district view, not an aggregated state total.

2. **Insights.nd.gov demographics portal.** The "Enrollment by Demographics" page on Insights.nd.gov includes English learner status as a demographic filter and allows year-to-year comparison, but the actual EL enrollment percentages and student counts are not visible in the publicly fetchable summary; they appear to require interactive dashboard access or direct portal navigation.

3. **District funding allocations suggest recent enrollment stability.** Title III Part A allocations for 2025-2026 include Fargo ($75,740.83), West Fargo ($92,489.25), and Williston Basin ($41,904.95). West Fargo and Fargo remain the largest EL population centers, but no state aggregate is published alongside these figures.

4. **FERPA suppression impact.** Many smaller North Dakota districts fall below the 25-student reporting threshold, making manual aggregation incomplete. A statewide total would need to include suppressed cells, which the state does not appear to publish.

5. **Terminology consistency.** North Dakota uses "English Learner" (EL), not ELL, ESOL, or ESL in official documentation. The state's EL program is sometimes referenced as "English Language Development" (ELD) in standards and policy contexts, but the student classification term is EL.
