# Nebraska — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** Fall 2021 (no newer state-level aggregate data found)
- **Snapshot date:** October 2021
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 23,928
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 328,110
- **EL percent:** 7.3%
- **Source label:** NCES Digest of Education Statistics, Table 204.20 (fall 2021)
- **Source URL:** https://nces.ed.gov/programs/digest/d23/tables/dt23_204.20.asp
- **Local terminology:** English Learner (EL); some districts use "Multilingual Learner" (MLL) or reference programs as ESL or bilingual programs

## SEA methodology

The Nebraska Department of Education collects EL enrollment data through the ADVISER (Automated Data Verification and Instruction Reporting Engine) system, which gathers data from local school districts' Student Information Systems. The data represents students identified as English Learners regardless of program participation. The NCES data reflect October fall snapshots for K-12 public school enrollment only and derive from EDFacts Title III data submissions.

The Nebraska Department of Education publishes EL demographic information through:
- County-level maps of EL students
- EL students by grade and languages spoken
- Annually updated data (most recent found: 2024-2025 school year data available on the EL Demographic Information page)

However, state-level aggregate enrollment counts and percentages for school years after fall 2021 do not appear to be published in a single consolidated document accessible via the SEA's data portal.

## Comparison to NCES

**Fall 2018-2021 NCES baseline (Digest Table 204.20):**
- Fall 2018: 22,127 ELs; 7.2% of 307,293 total enrollment
- Fall 2019: 21,593 ELs; 7.0% of 308,361 total enrollment
- Fall 2020: 22,223 ELs; 6.8% of 327,649 total enrollment
- Fall 2021: 23,928 ELs; 7.3% of 328,110 total enrollment

**Conclusion: NO OVERLAP**

The Nebraska Department of Education does not publish historical state-level EL enrollment figures (percentage or count) for fall 2018-2021 in a format accessible via web search or their public data portals. The EL Demographic Information page (education.ne.gov/natlorigin/state-ell-data/) provides county maps and breakdowns by grade/language for recent years, but not a multi-year state aggregate table suitable for direct year-by-year comparison.

The state references a "state average of 10%" for EL enrollment in informal district comparisons (e.g., Grand Island Public Schools with 27% ELs vs. the state average), but this does not correspond to NCES's 7.3% figure for fall 2021, and its methodological basis (e.g., which year, snapshot date, program enrollment vs. classification) is unclear.

## Notes / caveats

1. **Data lag:** The most recent verified state-level aggregate EL enrollment data (NCES fall 2021) is approximately 3 years old. Newer school years (2022-2025) may have been collected by the SEA in ADVISER, but these figures are not published in consolidated form on the publicly accessible portions of the Nebraska Department of Education website.

2. **"10% state average" discrepancy:** Multiple news articles and district comparisons reference a 10% state average for EL enrollment in Nebraska (e.g., Grand Island Public Schools reporting that its 27% EL population is "well above the state average of 10%"). This figure does not align with NCES's fall 2021 figure of 7.3%. Possible explanations include:
   - The 10% may refer to a more recent school year (2022-2025) and could represent an actual increase in EL enrollment.
   - The methodologies may differ (program enrollment vs. classification, scope of denominator).
   - The figure may be informal or drawn from ADVISER data not yet published in SEA summaries.

3. **Methodology uncertainty:** The comparison of the state average "10%" figure to NCES's 7.3% is labeled NO OVERLAP rather than DIVERGES because the methodologies and years are not clearly specified. A proper comparison would require the SEA to publish year-by-year state-level enrollment counts and percentages with explicit definitions of "English Learner" and denominator scope.

4. **Program vs. classification:** District-level reports sometimes distinguish between "ELL program enrollment" and "EL classification." The sources reviewed do not consistently clarify whether reported figures represent classified EL students (per NCES methodology) or program enrollment.

5. **Snapshot date:** NCES uses October fall snapshots. The "10% state average" in recent comparisons does not specify whether it represents fall 2023, fall 2024, or another snapshot date.
