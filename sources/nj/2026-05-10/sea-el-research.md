# New Jersey — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2023-24
- **Snapshot date:** 2023-10-15 (fall snapshot)
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 151,245
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 1,426,840
- **EL percent:** 10.6%
- **Source label:** New Jersey Department of Education 2023-2024 School Performance Reports
- **Source URL:** https://www.nj.gov/education/sprreports/202324/State-Detail/St.pdf
- **Local terminology:** Multilingual Learner (ML); synonymous with English Learner (EL) or English Language Learner (ELL)

## SEA methodology

New Jersey uses the term "Multilingual Learner" (ML) to describe students developing proficiency in multiple languages whose primary language is not English. This is defined in N.J.A.C. 6A:15 (New Jersey Administrative Code, Chapter 15: Bilingual Education).

**Identification criteria:** A student is identified as ML when:
1. The home language survey (HLS) indicates a language other than English at home.
2. A records review is conducted and documents indicate the student is developing English proficiency.
3. Assessment results (using approved English Language Proficiency instruments per state guidance) support classification.

**Denominator:** The total figure includes K-12 public school enrollment only (excludes pre-K and post-secondary). The 2023-2024 figure of 1,426,840 represents "Total Students" reported in the NJDOE School Performance Reports.

**Snapshot date:** Fall snapshot data, typically collected mid-October (per NJDOE documentation, October 15 for 2024-2025 submissions).

**Definition source:** N.J.A.C. 6A:15, readopted with amendments in 2023. Official state guidance: https://www.nj.gov/education/title3/district/implementation.shtml

## Comparison to NCES

NCES Digest Table 204.20 provides the following historical percentages for New Jersey:
- Fall 2018: 6.0%
- Fall 2019: 7.0%
- Fall 2020: 6.9%
- Fall 2021: 8.2%

**Conclusion: DIVERGES**

The 2023-2024 SEA figure (10.6%) diverges from the NCES 2021 baseline (8.2%) by 2.4 percentage points—well above the 0.5pp threshold. This indicates either:

1. **Methodological changes:** New Jersey's transition to the term "Multilingual Learner" and expanded identification criteria (implemented in 2023 via amended N.J.A.C. 6A:15) may have broadened the population counted.
2. **Genuine enrollment growth:** An actual increase in the ML population (supported by the legislative memo A4988, which notes growth from "nearly 94,000 students in the 2019-2020 school year to over 130,000 students in the 2023-2024 school year").
3. **Reporting change:** Possible shift in how reclassified/monitored students are counted (NCES excludes former-EL students in monitoring; NJ's guidance does not explicitly exclude them).

**No overlapping NCES years for SEA data:** NCES Digest Table 204.20 terminates at fall 2021. The NJDOE 2023-2024 School Performance Reports do not publish multi-year historical tables showing SEA-reported percentages for fall 2018-2021 for direct year-by-year comparison. The divergence is detected only by comparing the SEA's most recent (2023-24, 10.6%) to NCES's most recent (2021, 8.2%).

## Notes / caveats

1. **Calculation:** 10.6% × 1,426,840 = 151,244.64, rounded to 151,245 ML students.
2. **Terminology transition:** The NJDOE officially adopted "Multilingual Learner" terminology in recent years; prior communications used "ELL" or "English Language Learner." Both are treated as equivalent in this analysis.
3. **Legislative context:** Assembly Bill A4988 (introduced 2024) references the "130,000+ students in 2023-2024" figure and "approximately nine percent" in summary language, but the official NJDOE School Performance Reports cite the more precise 10.6% figure. The 9% figure may be an estimate or rounded for legislative summary; the 10.6% is the official published rate.
4. **Denominator scope:** The reported total enrollment (1,426,840) includes only public K-12 schools. The NJDOE does not include pre-K in "Total Students" on the state-level performance report.
5. **Snapshot timing:** New Jersey's fall enrollment data are collected via the Official Fall Snapshot submission (typically mid-October). The 2023-2024 snapshot corresponds to October 2023.
6. **No recent confirmation of newer data:** The 2024-2025 enrollment data (enrollment_2425.xlsx) is available for download but has not yet been accompanied by official state-level performance reports with published ML percentages as of the research date (2026-05-10).
