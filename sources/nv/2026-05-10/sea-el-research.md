## SEA methodology

Nevada identifies English Learners using a home language survey screener that identifies any student whose primary language, home language, or peer communication language is not English. Identified students are administered the WIDA Screener; those scoring below 4.5 on the WIDA Screener qualify for English Learner services and assessment (Nevada Department of Education, Office of Student and School Supports).

Denominator: K-12 public school enrollment, captured as of October 1 each school year (validation day). Nevada does not count reclassified/former-EL students in monitoring status as ongoing EL counts—the identification system is based on current WIDA Screener performance.

Definition: Nevada adopts the federal definition under 20 U.S.C. (per Nevada Revised Statutes Chapter 385). Students are formally identified via NRS 388 provisions requiring annual evaluation and instructional support.

Snapshot date: October 1st of each school year (state validation day).

## Notes / caveats

1. **Data availability mismatch.** The Nevada Department of Education collects comprehensive enrollment data (stated as "at least 14% of Nevada's school population" are EL/immigrant students), but SEA-published summary statistics are sparse. The raw data files are downloadable but not presented in a standard disaggregated summary table or annual report.

2. **Most recent figure is tentative.** The 14.4% figure for 2024-25 is based on references in search results but was not directly cited in a downloadable SEA publication—it appears to be derived from news reporting on enrollment data rather than from an official SEA data dashboard or published report.

3. **No explicit methodology publication.** While Nevada uses WIDA Screener (score < 4.5) for EL identification, the formal methodology document describing denominator scope (K-12 vs. PK-12), treatment of reclassified students, or snapshot-month specificity was not located in SEA-published guidance.

4. **Terminology.** Nevada's official terminology is "English Learner" (EL). Historical documents and some program references use "ELL" (English Language Learner) and "ESL" (English as a Second Language) interchangeably, though the current preferred term is EL.

5. **Most current accessible point:** Fall 2021 (13.8% from NCES; confirmed to match SEA data). Newer figures (2022, 2023, 2024, 2025) are collected by Nevada DOE but not published in an easily accessible aggregated form.
