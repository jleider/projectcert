# New York — SEA ELL/MLL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published ELL/MLL figures

- **School year:** 2023-24
- **Snapshot date:** 2023-10-04 (BEDS Day, first Wednesday of October)
- **ELL/MLL count:** 259,829
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 2,418,513
- **ELL/MLL percent:** 10.74%
- **Source label:** New York State Education Department Data Site, English Language Learners Data
- **Source URL:** https://data.nysed.gov/ell.php?year=2024&state=yes

## SEA methodology

NYSED defines English Language Learners (ELLs) per Section 154.3 of the Commissioner's Regulations as those who, by reason of foreign birth or ancestry, speak or understand a language other than English and speak or understand little or no English, and require support in order to become proficient in English. The term "Multilingual Learner" (MLL) is used interchangeably by NYSED and refers to the same population.

The ELL count reflects students identified as ELL during the school year, reported on BEDS Day (Basic Education Data System), which is the first Wednesday of October. For the 2023-24 school year, BEDS Day was October 4, 2023. Total enrollment uses the same BEDS Day snapshot date.

Enrollment includes K-12 public school students only (excludes pre-K and non-public schools).

## Comparison to NCES

NCES Digest Table 204.20 (fall 2018–2021) reported:
- Fall 2018: 8.8%
- Fall 2019: 8.7%
- Fall 2020: 9.2%
- Fall 2021: 9.7%

NYSED's 2023-24 figure of 10.74% shows an upward trend from the 2021 NCES baseline (9.7%), an increase of 1.04 percentage points over two school years (2021-22 and 2023-24).

## Notes / caveats

- NYSED data became available on May 10, 2026 (the same date as this research).
- 2024-25 enrollment data has been published (K-12 enrollment: 2,421,491) but 2024-25 ELL data is not yet available on the NYSED Data Site.
- The data site uses the term "English Language Learners" officially; "Multilingual Learner" (MLL) is an alternative term also used by NYSED.
