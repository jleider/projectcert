## SEA methodology

Oklahoma's State Department of Education tracks English learner enrollment as part of EdFacts Title III submissions to NCES. The data includes all students formally identified as English learners, regardless of program participation (transitional bilingual, English as a Second Language, sheltered instruction, or mainstream with EL support).

The NCES figures represent K-12 public school enrollment with a standard October snapshot date (fall enrollment). Oklahoma does not appear to publish statewide EL enrollment figures independently; data is accessible through NCES reporting and the Oklahoma School Report Cards dashboard (oklaschools.com), which displays progress indicators (ELPA metrics) but not total enrollment counts.

## Comparison to NCES

NCES published data for fall 2018–2021 shows Oklahoma's EL enrollment percentage rising consistently:
- Fall 2018: 7.9%
- Fall 2019: 8.5%
- Fall 2020: 8.7%
- Fall 2021: 9.3%

No newer state-published data found to extend beyond Fall 2021 for direct comparison. A reference in the PROPEL Oklahoma Forward Comprehensive Literacy Plan (2024) mentions "10% of the student population" for SY 2022-23, but the primary source document was not accessible for verification of the exact count, denominator, and snapshot date.

**Conclusion: NO OVERLAP** — Oklahoma does not appear to publish independent statewide EL enrollment figures post-2021. The most recent NCES data available is Fall 2021. No authenticated SEA source with newer data (Fall 2022, Fall 2023, or Fall 2024) was located.

## Notes / caveats

1. Oklahoma's Office of English Language Proficiency (OELP) manages EL identification and the WIDA ACCESS assessment (administered January–March annually), but does not appear to publish a dedicated statewide EL enrollment fact sheet or annual report with enrollment counts and percentages.

2. The Oklahoma School Report Cards system (oklaschools.com) displays English Language Proficiency Assessment (ELPA) progress metrics (% of ELs meeting language acquisition targets), not total EL enrollment counts. This is contextual data useful for accountability but not suitable for tracking population estimates.

3. A reference in the PROPEL Oklahoma Forward Comprehensive Literacy Plan (published 2024, covering SY 2022-23) states that ELs constitute "10% of the student population," which would mark an increase from 9.3% in Fall 2021. However, the underlying source document did not permit extraction of the exact numerator, denominator, or snapshot date to confirm methodological continuity with NCES reporting.

4. No dedicated statewide EL enrollment dashboard, data table, or annual fact sheet with historical comparisons was found on the Oklahoma Department of Education website.

5. The Fall 2021 NCES figure (9.3%) is the most recent verifiable statewide EL enrollment percentage for Oklahoma from an authenticated, citable source.
