# South Dakota — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2023-24
- **Snapshot date:** 2023-09-29 (last Friday in September)
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 4,230
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 141,467
- **EL percent:** 2.99%
- **Source label:** South Dakota Department of Education Fall 2023 Public School Enrollment Census Count
- **Source URL:** https://asbsd.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Fall-2023-Public-School-Enrollment-DOE.pdf
- **Local terminology:** English Learner (EL); the state uses "ELL Percentage" in enrollment reports and defines EL per ESEA Section 3201(5)

## SEA methodology

South Dakota defines an English Learner according to the Federal government definition in ESEA Section 3201(5). A student is classified as an English learner if:

- Aged 3 through 21 and enrolled or preparing to enroll in elementary or secondary school; AND
- Who either: (i) was not born in the United States or whose native language is a language other than English; (ii) is a Native American, Alaska Native, or native resident of an outlying area whose language background indicates potential language deficiency; OR (iii) is migratory and whose native language is other than English; AND
- Whose difficulties speaking, reading, writing, or understanding English may be sufficient to deny the individual the ability to meet challenging state academic standards, achieve successfully in classrooms where English is the language of instruction, or participate fully in society.

The enrollment snapshot is taken on the last Friday in September each school year and reflects students "enrolled more than 49 percent of the school day." The denominator is K-12 public school enrollment.

## Comparison to NCES

South Dakota's fall 2023 EL percentage (2.99%) **DIVERGES significantly** from the NCES reported values for the overlapping years:

| Year | NCES % | Search result |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Fall 2018 | 4.3% | Not searched (pre-2023 data) |
| Fall 2019 | 4.7% | Not searched |
| Fall 2020 | 4.7% | Not searched |
| Fall 2021 | 4.6% | Not searched |

**Comparison outcome: DIVERGES** — The fall 2023 SEA figure (2.99%) is substantially lower than the NCES fall 2021 value (4.6%), a difference of 1.61 percentage points. This is far beyond the 0.5 percentage point threshold for "CLOSE" comparison.

**Note on comparison limitation:** I was unable to retrieve overlapping SEA-published data for fall 2018-2021 to directly compare year-by-year with NCES values. The South Dakota Department of Education enrollment reports (PubschEL and PubdisEL Excel files) appear to be available on the enrollment page but are not directly web-fetchable. The fall 2023 data was located in a PDF generated by the South Dakota Board of School Administrators. A direct comparison would require accessing the district-level or school-level ELL reports (PubdisEL-2024.xlsx, PubdisEL-23c.xlsx, etc.) to extract state-totals for prior years.

## Notes / caveats

1. **Data source accessibility:** The South Dakota Department of Education publishes detailed ELL percentage files (PubschEL and PubdisEL Excel files) on their enrollment page (https://doe.sd.gov/ofm/enrollment.aspx), updated annually in December. These files contain school-level and district-level breakdowns with EL counts and percentages but are not directly web-accessible as individual files. The state total row visible in the fall 2023 census PDF (4,230 EL students of 141,467 total) appears to be the official state-level figure.

2. **Potential methodological shift:** The substantial divergence from NCES fall 2021 (4.6%) to the 2023 SEA figure (2.99%) warrants investigation. This 1.61-point drop is too large to attribute to demographic change alone and may reflect:
   - A change in EL identification practices
   - A change in the denominator (e.g., now including/excluding certain grade levels or school types)
   - A data reporting or classification change

3. **Reclassified students:** The South Dakota EL Identification & Screening Manual (August 2023) does not explicitly address whether reclassified/monitored students are included in the enrollment count. NCES includes all classified ELs regardless of program participation; if South Dakota excludes monitored (formerly-EL) students, that could partially explain the lower percentage.

4. **Snapshot timing:** The fall 2023 data were published by ASBA in February 2024 and are the most recent SEA-published enrollment figures accessible. Fall 2024 enrollment data have not yet been published on the enrollment page as of 2026-05-10.
