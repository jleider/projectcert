# Utah — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2024-25
- **Snapshot date:** 2024-10-01
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 58,419
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** 667,789
- **EL percent:** 8.7%
- **Source label:** Utah State Board of Education fall enrollment count
- **Source URL:** https://schools.utah.gov/datastatistics/reports
- **Local terminology:** "English learner" (EL); also uses "Multilingual Learner" (ML) synonymously

## SEA methodology

Utah's State Board of Education (USBE) collects annual fall enrollment data as of October 1 for all K-12 public schools (including charter schools) through the Utah eTranscript and Record Exchange (UTREx) system. This October 1 count is used for legislative budgeting and is the official enrollment measurement date across all states for comparison purposes.

**EL Identification Process:**
- All students are screened using the Home Language Survey (HLS) at school registration. If any of the three required HLS questions are answered in a language other than English, students proceed to WIDA screening.
- Students scoring 1–4.9 on any WIDA Screener (WIDA Alternate Screener, WIDA Screener for Kindergarten, or WIDA Screener Online for grades 1–12) are identified as English learners and are eligible for English Language Services.
- Utah's legal framework for EL services is defined in state statute HB 302 (Educational Language Services Amendments) and implements Title III of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act.

**Denominator:** K-12 public school enrollment (district and charter schools combined), excluding preschool.

**Snapshot date:** October 1, collected for budgeting purposes but published in annual reports later in the school year (fall 2024 enrollment figures published in 2024 and referenced in early 2025).

## Comparison to NCES

NCES Digest Table 204.20 published English learner percentages for Utah through fall 2021:
- Fall 2018: 7.5%
- Fall 2019: 7.9%
- Fall 2020: 7.9%
- Fall 2021: 8.3%

**Comparison:**
- Fall 2021 (NCES): 8.3%
- Fall 2024 (USBE): 8.7%

**Conclusion: CLOSE.** The USBE October 1, 2024 figure (8.7%) is 0.4 percentage points above the NCES fall 2021 figure (8.3%), placing it within the "CLOSE" range (≤0.5 percentage points difference). This suggests methodological continuity between the NCES series and USBE reporting. No divergence detected. NCES has not yet published fall 2022 or fall 2023 state-level EL percentages for comparison; the data presented here represents the most current SEA observation available for Utah, measuring a three-year increase of 0.4 percentage points from fall 2021 to fall 2024.

## Notes / caveats

- The most recent comprehensive published NCES state-level EL data ends at fall 2021 (Digest Table 204.20). USBE's October 1, 2024 count represents newer data not yet incorporated into the national NCES series.
- USBE distinguishes between "English learners" (identified students eligible for services) and "English learner program enrollment" (actual service participation). The 58,419 figure is identification count, not program enrollment.
- The 8.7% figure (58,419 ÷ 667,789) reflects a slight rounding; USBE reports this as "9%" in some public communications (rounding to nearest whole percent).
- Utah's total K-12 enrollment declined by 4,873 students (0.7%) from fall 2023 to fall 2024, but the EL count remained relatively stable, reflecting the steady upward trend in EL identification over the past three years.
- No additional SEA-published multi-year tables comparing fall 2022–2024 EL percentages were located during this search; comparison to NCES is limited to the fall 2021 endpoint of the national series.
