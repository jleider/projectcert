# Washington — SEA EL enrollment research (2026-05-10)

## Most recent SEA-published EL figures

- **School year:** 2024-25
- **Snapshot date:** 2024-10-01
- **EL (or equivalent) count:** 167,665
- **Total enrollment (denominator):** ~1,110,596 (calculated from reported 15.1%)
- **EL percent:** 15.1%
- **Source label:** OSPI Transitional Bilingual Instruction Program (TBIP) Legislative Report, 2024-25
- **Source URL:** https://ospi.k12.wa.us/sites/default/files/2026-01/tbip-legislative-report-2024-25.pdf
- **Local terminology:** Multilingual Learner (ML); TBIP serves these students

## SEA methodology

Washington State Education Agency (OSPI) uses "Multilingual Learner" (ML) as the primary term, equivalent to English Learner (EL) classification. The TBIP (Transitional Bilingual Instruction Program) serves multilingual learners identified for language development services. The enrollment count is taken as of October 1 of each school year, consistent with federal reporting practices. The 15.1% figure represents ML enrollment as a percentage of total statewide student population on October 1, 2024.

## Comparison to NCES

NCES Digest Table 204.20 historical data for Washington (fall snapshot dates):

| Year | NCES EL % | NCES EL Count |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Fall 2018 | 11.5% | 128,774 |
| Fall 2019 | 11.3% | 129,564 |
| Fall 2020 | 11.6% | 125,822 |
| Fall 2021 | 11.4% | 123,785 |

Washington SEA-published data:

| Year | OSPI ML % | OSPI ML Count |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| 2022-23 | 13.4% | 141,434 |
| 2023-24 | ~14.7% | 165,662 |
| 2024-25 | 15.1% | 167,665 |

**Comparison conclusion: DIVERGES**

The SEA-published figures (13.4–15.1% for 2022-23 through 2024-25) diverge substantially from the NCES 2021 endpoint (11.4%) and show a significant upward trend. The gap between the most recent NCES report (fall 2021, 11.4%) and the most recent OSPI report (fall 2024, 15.1%) is 3.7 percentage points — well above the 0.5 percentage-point threshold for DIVERGES.

Key methodological considerations:
- OSPI classification data (fall 2022–2024) is not directly comparable to NCES (which reports through fall 2021). The NCES series ended in 2021; no NCES data exists for fall 2022 or later.
- The substantial jump from 11.4% (fall 2021 NCES) to 13.4% (2022-23 OSPI) suggests either a genuine increase in identification/enrollment OR a methodological shift in how OSPI counts ML students vs. how NCES/EDFacts reported ELs.
- OSPI terminology shifted from "EL" to "ML" around 2023. It is unclear whether the definition of the population or counting methodology changed concurrently.
- Washington's reclassification policies may affect the count (e.g., whether students in a monitoring period post-reclassification are still counted). NCES does not count reclassified students; OSPI's guidance is not explicit on this point from the sources consulted.

## Notes / caveats

1. **No detailed OSPI historical table available**: Washington does not appear to publish a multi-year EL/ML enrollment table on the OSPI website. The 2022-23 figure (141,434 at 13.4%) and 2023-24 figure (165,662) were found in OSPI program updates and legislative reports, not a consolidated historical enrollment dataset.

2. **Denominator uncertainty**: The 2024-25 total enrollment denominator (~1,110,596) is calculated backward from the 15.1% figure and not explicitly confirmed in a single source. The most recent NCES data (2022-23) reported 1,090,227 total Washington K-12 enrollment; the SEA figures suggest modest growth to ~1,110,000 by 2024-25, which is plausible but not independently verified.

3. **Terminology transition**: Washington's shift from "EL" to "ML" occurred around 2022-23. The 2022-23 Multilingual Education Program updates from PESB-OSPI (August 2023) use "ML" consistently. It is possible that OSPI's pre-2023 reports used "EL" but were not consulted in this research.

4. **TBIP vs. ML classification**: The TBIP legislative reports conflate TBIP program enrollment with ML identification count. The research confirms these are being reported as equivalent (i.e., OSPI reports ML identification count, which is the TBIP-eligible population). However, some students may be identified as ML but not enrolled in a TBIP-funded program (e.g., in districts without TBIP programs). The reported figures appear to be classification counts, not program enrollment counts.

5. **No data for fall 2022**: Washington does not appear to have published an October 1, 2022 enrollment snapshot for ML students. The 2022-23 figure (141,434) is the earliest post-NCES SEA figure located.
