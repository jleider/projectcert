## SEA methodology

West Virginia does not appear to publish state-level EL enrollment data publicly through a dedicated SEA report or data portal. The available data comes from the National Center for Education Statistics (NCES) Common Core of Data (CCD), collected via the Public Elementary/Secondary School Universe Survey.

The NCES defines ELs as "all ELs, regardless of program participation" (EdFacts Title III submission definitions). The denominator is total K-12 public school enrollment. The snapshot date follows the standard fall October collection date used by NCES.

West Virginia's Department of Education maintains an education data portal (ZoomWV) and indicates data can be requested through their data request process at https://wvde.us/education-data/, but no standalone state-published EL enrollment fact sheet or report was found during this research.

## Notes / caveats

1. **No newer data beyond fall 2021.** Despite multiple search strategies (searches for 2022–2024 data, WVDE education data portals, Title III program reports, and state fact sheets), no published state-level EL enrollment figures for 2022 or later were found.

2. **Limited SEA publication.** West Virginia does not appear to maintain a publicly accessible, standalone state report on EL enrollment counts and percentages. The state relies on NCES CCD data collection rather than publishing its own aggregate statistics.

3. **Terminology:** West Virginia uses "English Learner" (EL) and "English Language Learner" (ELL) terms. Both refer to the same population.

4. **Data request option:** The West Virginia Department of Education indicates EL data can be requested through their ZoomWV system (https://zoomwv.k12.wv.us/) or via direct data request, but public reports were not located.

5. **No research on classification vs. program enrollment.** Available sources do not clarify whether the NCES figures include only classified ELs or also encompass students in EL-related programs. The NCES definition states "all ELs, regardless of program participation," suggesting these are classification counts, but SEA-specific confirmation was not found.
